## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,冠词密集)

> I have just moved into an apartment on Oak Street. Yesterday a beggar knocked on my door. He asked me for a sandwich and a cup of coffee. In return, the beggar stood on his head and sang a funny song. I gave him some food. He ate the sandwich and drank the coffee. Then he put a cookie in his pocket and walked away. Later, a neighbor told me about him. Everybody on the street knows him — his name is Percy Buttons. He calls at every house once a month. He always asks for a meal and never takes money.

**前置听力题**:What did the beggar do in return for the food? → **He stood on his head and sang** ✓ / He paid money / He washed the dishes / He cleaned the room
