### s5 seq1 | ans: barks
"每当…就…"这种经常性、习惯性动作用一般现在时;主语 he 用 barks。✓ Every time he wants to come in, he barks. ✗ is barking:此处不是此刻正在进行;✗ barked:一般过去;✗ has barked:现在完成。

### s5 seq2 | ans: removed
Yesterday 表明是过去某时的动作,用一般过去时;remove 的过去式是 removed。✓ Yesterday my husband removed the gate. ✗ removes:一般现在;✗ has removed:不与 yesterday 连用;✗ is removing:现在进行。

### s5 seq3 | ans: complained
叙述过去发生的原因用一般过去时;complain 的过去式是 complained。✓ As the neighbors complained of the noise… ✗ complain:一般现在;✗ have complained:现在完成;✗ are complaining:现在进行。

### s5 seq4 | ans: has developed
Since then 表"从那时起到现在",用现在完成时。✓ Since then, he has developed another bad habit. ✗ developed:一般过去,不与 since then 连用;✗ is developing:现在进行;✗ develops:一般现在。

### s5 seq5 | ans: have not seen
"此后一直没见到"用现在完成时(动作延续到现在);主语 we 用 have not seen。✓ we have not seen him since. ✗ did not see:一般过去;✗ are not seeing:现在进行;✗ do not see:一般现在。

### s5 seq6 | ans: have been
"一连几周一直在来"用现在完成进行时 have been + 现在分词;主语 tourists 复数用 have been。✓ Tourists have been coming to see the tree for weeks. ✗ are:现在进行不含"几周一直";✗ were:过去;✗ has been:主语复数不用 has。

### s5 seq7 | ans: used to
"过去常做、现在不做了"的习惯用 used to + 动词原形。✓ Our dog used to sit outside the gate and bark. ✗ would be:构不成这个意思;✗ uses to:形式错;✗ is used to:意思是"习惯于"。

### s5 seq8 | ans: would
过去反复、经常发生的动作用 would + 动词原形。✓ Every time he wanted to come in, he would bark. ✗ used to be:构不成这个意思;✗ will:将来;✗ is:构不成过去反复。

### s5 seq9 | ans: was
过去被动 = was + 过去分词;主语 dog 单数用 was trained。✓ The dog was trained to press the latch. ✗ is:时态应是过去;✗ has:构不成被动;✗ were:主语单数不用 were。

### s5 seq10 | ans: said
be said to 表"据说…";此处填过去分词 said(is said to possess)。✓ The village is said to possess a cursed tree. ✗ say:原形;✗ saying:现在分词;✗ told:be told to 是"被告知去做",词义不同。

### s5 seq11 | ans: has been
现在完成被动 = has been + 过去分词。✓ Your wallet has been found. ✗ is being:进行被动;✗ was being:过去进行被动;✗ has:少了 been。

### s5 seq12 | ans: to
表示目的最简单的方式是用不定式 to do。✓ He ran to the station to catch the train. ✗ for:后不接动词原形表目的;✗ so / that:单独不引导目的。

### s5 seq13 | ans: so
表目的"为的是"用 so that 引导从句,此处填 so。✓ He was barking so that someone would let him out. ✗ in:in that 不表目的;✗ for / as:不与 that 构成目的从句。

### s5 seq14 | ans: so
表目的的否定"以免、免得"用 so as not to,此处填 so。✓ I opened the door quietly so as not to disturb him. ✗ in / for / to:不构成 so as not to 结构。

### s5 seq15 | ans: order

### s5 seq16 | ans: Every
every time 作连词,表"每当、无论何时",引导时间状语从句。✓ Every time he wanted to come in, he barked. ✗ Some / Any / No:不与 time 构成"每当"的连词。

### s5 seq17 | ans: so
