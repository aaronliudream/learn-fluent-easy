### u4.01 动词短语 / 短语动词(选正确的小品词:up/on/away/in/out/forward to 等)
- After graduation, she decided to take ____ volunteer work in a poor village.
  **up✓** / off / in / over
  考点:动词短语 / 短语动词(选正确的小品词:up/on/away/in/out/forward to 等) ｜ 解析:take up sth = 开始从事;take up volunteer work 开始做志愿工作。

  考点:动词短语 / 短语动词(选正确的小品词:up/on/away/in/out/forward to 等) ｜ 解析:carry on = 继续(进行);其他小品词不与 carry 构成'继续'之意。
- He gave ____ most of his old clothes to families in need.
  up / in / **away✓** / off
  考点:动词短语 / 短语动词(选正确的小品词:up/on/away/in/out/forward to 等) ｜ 解析:give away = 捐赠/赠送出去;give up 是放弃,不合句意。
- The students look ____ to meeting the children at the hospital each year.
  back / down / after / **forward✓**
  考点:动词短语 / 短语动词(选正确的小品词:up/on/away/in/out/forward to 等) ｜ 解析:look forward to = 期待;固定搭配,后接动名词。
- A group of young people set ____ a charity to help the Roma in Budapest.
