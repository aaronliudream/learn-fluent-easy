### s5 seq2 | ans: to

### s5 seq3 | ans: likely
‘很可能…’= be + likely + to do;填形容词 likely。✓ Mars is likely to attract settlers. ✗ like(像)/ alike(相似的,作表语)/ likeness(名词):此处用 likely。

### s5 seq4 | ans: than

### s5 seq5 | ans: wider

### s5 seq6 | ans: sixth
分数‘六分之一’,分母用序数词 sixth(a sixth of …)。✓ only a sixth of the Earth's gravity. ✗ six / sixty / sixteen:分母用序数词 sixth。

### s5 seq7 | ans: takes
‘做某事需要/花费…’用 it takes …;填动词 takes。✓ It takes much less energy to accelerate. ✗ makes / gives / does:‘花费/需要’用 takes。

### s5 seq8 | ans: to

### s5 seq9 | ans: to

### s5 seq10 | ans: less
