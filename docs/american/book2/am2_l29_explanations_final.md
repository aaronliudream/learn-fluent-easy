### s5 seq2 | ans: stayed

### s5 seq3 | ans: did
过去某次、已结束的事,用一般过去时;否定用 did not + 动词原形。✓ Captain Fawcett did not take him to Rockall.

### s5 seq4 | ans: since

### s5 seq5 | ans: has

### s5 seq6 | ans: for

### s5 seq7 | ans: have

### s5 seq8 | ans: ever

### s5 seq9 | ans: never
never 表"从未",问经历,配现在完成时,放在 have 和过去分词之间。✓ Up till now, I have never been abroad.

### s5 seq10 | ans: before
before(以前)表经历,配现在完成时。✓ I have not been abroad before. ago / last year 是明确过去时间,要配一般过去。

### s5 seq11 | ans: has

### s5 seq12 | ans: begun

### s5 seq13 | ans: went

### s5 seq14 | ans: Have

### s5 seq15 | ans: who

### s5 seq16 | ans: which

### s5 seq17 | ans: that

### s5 seq18 | ans: that
