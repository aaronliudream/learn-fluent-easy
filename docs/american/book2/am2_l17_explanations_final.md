### s5 seq1 | ans: 不可逃避的义务(必须)
must 常用来表示一种不可逃避的必须,说话人觉得没有选择余地。✓ She must appear in a red dress.

### s5 seq2 | ans: must
表"必须"用 must,后面直接跟动词原形 appear。must 本身不加 -s、也不加 to。✓ She must appear in a bright red dress.

### s5 seq3 | ans: must
表"必须"用 must + 动词原形 finish。没有 musts、must to、musting 这些形式。✓ I must finish my homework tonight.

### s5 seq4 | ans: have to / have got to
must 表"必须"时,可以用 have to 或 have got to 替换,意思一样。✓ I must go = I have to go = I have got to go.

### s5 seq5 | ans: He has to leave now.
must 表"必须",和 have to 同义,所以 He must leave now = He has to leave now。may/can/might 是"可以 / 也许",意思不同。

### s5 seq6 | ans: have to
跟在 will 后面说将来的"必须",要用 have to(will have to);must 没有将来式,不能说 will must。✓ Jennifer will have to take part in a new play.

### s5 seq7 | ans: Do you have to go now?
问"必须…吗"时常改用 have to:Must you go? = Do you have to go? 用 do 帮忙提问,不能说 Do you must。

### s5 seq8 | ans: had to
must 没有过去式,过去的"必须"用 had to;musted、did must 都不存在。✓ Yesterday she had to leave early.

### s5 seq9 | ans: had to
Last year(去年)是过去,过去的"必须"用 had to,不能用 must(它没有过去式)。✓ Last year she had to wear short socks.

### s5 seq10 | ans: 有把握的推测("一定是…")
must 除了"必须",还能表示很有把握的推测"一定是"。✓ She must be at least thirty-five.

### s5 seq11 | ans: must
"一定有三十五岁"是很有把握的推测,用 must be。may/might/could 是"也许",把握没那么大。✓ She must be at least thirty-five.

### s5 seq12 | ans: must
说话人对"长大成人很可怕"很确定,用 must be 表强推测。may/can/would 语气弱或意思不对。✓ It must be terrible to be grown up.

### s5 seq13 | ans: had to
Yesterday(昨天)是过去,过去的"不得不"用 had to,不用 must。✓ Yesterday I had to work late.

### s5 seq14 | ans: as
as 可以表原因,相当于 because。✓ I cannot come, as I am busy(因为我忙)。so / or / but 逻辑不对。

### s5 seq15 | ans: 作为,担任
work as + 职业 = 担任、以…身份工作。✓ He works as an engineer,他做工程师的工作。

### s5 seq16 | ans: 当…的时候
as 在这里表"当…的时候",相当于 when。✓ As I was leaving, the phone rang,我正要离开时电话响了。

### s5 seq17 | ans: 名词 / 动名词
in spite of 意思是"尽管",后面接名词或动名词,不接完整句子。✓ in spite of the rain / in spite of being tired.

### s5 seq18 | ans: In spite of
后面直接跟名词 the rain,用 In spite of(尽管)。though / although / because 后面要接完整句子,不能只跟一个名词。✓ In spite of the rain, they went out.
