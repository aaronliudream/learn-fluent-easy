### s5 seq1 | ans: could
过去的一般能力用 could。✓ As a boy, he could swim very well. ✗ managed to:用于"设法做成一件具体的难事",不表一般能力;✗ was able:缺 to;✗ did:不表能力。

### s5 seq2 | ans: couldn't
过去做不到用 couldn't(= wasn't able to)。✓ I couldn't understand him; he spoke too fast. ✗ didn't could:形式错;✗ wasn't able:缺 to;✗ don't could:形式错。

### s5 seq3 | ans: managed to
过去"设法做成了一件有难度的事"用 managed to。✓ Tazieff managed to set up his camp close to it. ✗ could:表一般能力,不强调这一次费劲做成;✗ must:表推测/必须;✗ may:表可能。

### s5 seq4 | ans: was able to
过去成功做成某事用 was able to(= managed to)。✓ He was able to take a number of brilliant photographs. ✗ could to:could 后不加 to;✗ managed:缺 to;✗ can:是现在。

### s5 seq5 | ans: managed to
过去费劲终于做成用 managed to。✓ I managed to persuade him. ✗ could:表一般能力;✗ must:必须/推测;✗ would:表意愿/过去习惯。

### s5 seq6 | ans: was able to
