### s5 seq1 | ans: whom
定语从句先行词指人、在从句里作宾语,用 whom。✓ the writers whom I respect. ✗ which(指物)/ whose(所有格)/ what(不引导定从):指人作宾语用 whom。

### s5 seq2 | ans: whom
定语从句指人、作宾语,用 whom。✓ a writer whom everyone admires. ✗ which / whose / what:指人作宾语用 whom。

### s5 seq3 | ans: whom
介词 + 关系代词,指人作宾语,介词后只能用 whom。✓ the reader for whom he writes. ✗ which / who(介词后不用 who)/ that(介词后不用 that):for + whom。

### s5 seq4 | ans: Once
‘一旦…就…’的时间连词是 Once。✓ Once the journey has begun … ✗ Unless 除非 / Because 因为 / Although 虽然:‘一旦’用 Once。

### s5 seq5 | ans: once
‘一…就…’用 once。✓ once the writer sets pen to paper. ✗ unless / because / so:‘一…就’用 once。

### s5 seq6 | ans: Once

### s5 seq7 | ans: well
might as well do,‘还不如/不妨’;固定搭配填 well。✓ might as well try to explain a crime. ✗ good / better / best:此结构用 well。

### s5 seq8 | ans: go
might as well + 动词原形;填原形 go。✓ we might as well go home. ✗ going / went / to go:might as well 后接原形。

### s5 seq9 | ans: as
‘做A无异于做B’= might as well A as B;填 might 后紧跟的那个词。✓ might as well ask the wind to stop as … ✗ so / too / very:此结构该位置用 as。

### s5 seq10 | ans: till
‘直到…’的时间连词是 till。✓ will not rest till he has captured it. ✗ unless 除非 / because 因为 / so 所以:‘直到’用 till。

### s5 seq11 | ans: have
until/till 从句表将来时用现在完成;填助动词 have。✓ until you have read the final draft. ✗ will(从句不用 will)/ are / did:现在完成用 have + 过去分词。

### s5 seq12 | ans: had
till 从句里跟随主句过去时,用过去完成;填助动词 had。✓ kept rewriting till the story had found its shape. ✗ will / is / does:此处用 had found。

### s5 seq13 | ans: of
be of + 抽象名词 = 形容词;填介词 of。✓ is of an indescribable fascination(= fascinating). ✗ in / at / for:此结构用 of。

### s5 seq14 | ans: value
be of + 抽象名词,‘很有价值’= of great value;填名词 value。✓ was of great value. ✗ valuable(形容词)/ valued / values:be of 后接名词 value。

### s5 seq15 | ans: use
be of + 抽象名词,‘没什么用’= of little use;填名词 use。✓ of little use until it is remade. ✗ useful(形容词)/ used / uses:be of 后接名词 use。

### s5 seq16 | ans: confess
(尤指不情愿地)承认某事是 confess(坦白、承认)。✓ to admit reluctantly is to confess. ✗ conceal 隐瞒;✗ confuse 使糊涂;✗ connect 连接——都不对。

### s5 seq17 | ans: inspiration
驱动创作的突发妙想是 inspiration(灵感)。✓ a sudden brilliant idea is inspiration. ✗ imitation 模仿;✗ hesitation 犹豫;✗ isolation 孤立——都不对。

### s5 seq18 | ans: discern
费力地看出或觉察到某物是 discern(看出、辨别)。✓ to perceive with difficulty is to discern. ✗ dismiss 打发;✗ disturb 打扰;✗ dissolve 溶解——都不对。
