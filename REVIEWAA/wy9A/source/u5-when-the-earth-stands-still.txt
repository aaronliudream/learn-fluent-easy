When the earth stands still
（题材:人类若消失,自然界会怎样的思想实验。非灾害题材。原文含虚拟语气 would…if…were,九上未教。）

Have you ever thought about this question: what would happen to the natural world if people were gone? Let's imagine it. One day, all human activities stop. Cars disappear from the roads, boats from the rivers, and planes from the sky. Streets, beaches and parks that are usually full of people remain empty. What happens in such a world?

It is no surprise that animals soon increase in number. In cities, traffic noise often means that animals cannot easily hear each other. Without humans, the noise drops greatly as traffic stops. This allows natural sounds to fill the air. It is much easier for animals to find each other and form families. Also, tourists disappear from beaches and seas. Sea animals are not troubled by people around them.

But not only animals enjoy the change. Plants benefit, too. Trees grow freely without the danger of being cut down. Forests take back the space that humans now live in. The earth is soon a green world again.

The natural wonders which have been hidden by pollution for years appear again. Cleaner air and water allow them to show all their beauty once again. But there aren't any people to admire such an amazing world. What a sad thing to think about!

Throughout history, humans have left so many marks on the earth. The rapid development of human society has made us care less for nature. But the world isn't a game. We can't cancel it and start over. We need to think more about how to get along with nature. Perhaps we should remember the wise words which Montaigne once wrote - let us permit nature to have her way.
