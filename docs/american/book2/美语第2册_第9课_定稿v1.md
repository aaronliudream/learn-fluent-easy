## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,时间介词密集)

> It was New Year's Eve, and that year it fell on the weekend. On Saturday night, my friends and I went to the old Town Hall. A huge crowd gathered on the sidewalk under the big clock. People stood in a long line, waiting to welcome the New Year. The clock would strike twelve at midnight, in just a few minutes. But at five to twelve, the big clock suddenly stopped! The minute hand did not move at all. We waited and waited in the cold, but nothing happened. At that moment, someone shouted, "It's already past twelve — the clock has stopped!" I looked at my watch, and it was true: the clock refused to welcome the New Year. Everybody began to laugh, and then we all started to sing.
