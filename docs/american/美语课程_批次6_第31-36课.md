# Lesson 31  I have a bad cold
## 我得了重感冒

> 语法目标：have + 病症（a cold / a fever / a sore throat） · I don't feel well. · Do you have...?
> 场景：打电话向老板请病假

## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: What does Kayla have?
（a) A fever only　b) A bad cold ✓　c) A headache）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | KAYLA: Good morning, Mr. Reed. This is Kayla. | 凯拉：早上好，里德先生。我是凯拉。 |
| 2 | MR. REED: Morning, Kayla! What's up? | 里德先生：早，凯拉！什么事？ |
| 3 | KAYLA: I don't feel well today. I have a bad cold. | 凯拉：我今天很不舒服，得了重感冒。 |
| 4 | MR. REED: Oh no. Do you have a fever? | 里德先生：哎呀。发烧吗？ |
| 5 | KAYLA: A little. And I have a sore throat too. | 凯拉：有点。嗓子也疼。 |
| 6 | MR. REED: That sounds rough. Stay home and rest. | 里德先生：听着挺难受的。在家好好休息吧。 |
| 7 | KAYLA: Thank you. I'm really sorry. | 凯拉：谢谢您。真不好意思。 |
| 8 | MR. REED: Don't worry about it. Drink a lot of water! | 里德先生：没事别放心上。多喝水！ |
| 9 | KAYLA: I will. Thanks, Mr. Reed. | 凯拉：我会的。谢谢您，里德先生。 |
| 10 | MR. REED: Feel better soon! | 里德先生：祝早日康复！ |

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① have + 病症**

| 病症 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| a cold 感冒 | I **have a** bad **cold**. |
| a fever 发烧 | Do you **have a fever**? |
| a sore throat 嗓子疼 | I **have a sore throat**. |
| a headache 头痛 | He **has a headache**.（三单 has 复现） |

**② I don't feel well.（感觉不舒服）**：feel + 形容词。

**③ Do you have...? 复现**：Do you have a fever? — Yes, I do. / No, I don't.

**④ 美式 vs 英式**：美语说 I **have** a cold；英式常说 I've got a cold（本课程统一用 have）。

| rough | 同（口语） | 难受/不好过 |

## 关8 · 听对话理解题

1. Who is Kayla talking to? — a) Her boss ✓　b) Her mom　c) Her doctor　d) Her roommate

## 关9 · 情景应答

1. 说你感冒了 → I have a cold.
2. 告诉老板你今天不舒服 → I don't feel well today. I have a bad cold.
3. 朋友生病了，祝他早日康复 → Feel better soon!

## 关10 · 本课通关

A. 选择填空
1. I ___ a sore throat.　a) have　b) has　c) am　d) having → **a**
2. She ___ a fever.　a) have　b) is　c) haves　d) has → **d**
3. I don't ___ well.　a) feels　b) feel　c) feeling　d) felt → **b**
4. 朋友生病，你说：　a) Have a good one!　b) You got it!　c) Feel better soon!　d) Lucky you! → **c**

B. 句型转换
1. You have a cold.（变一般疑问句）→ Do you have a cold?
2. I have a headache.（变否定句）→ I don't have a headache.
3. He has a fever.（变一般疑问句，用 Does）→ Does he have a fever?

# Lesson 32  You must rest
## 你必须休息

> 语法目标：must（必须，医嘱/规定） · mustn't（禁止） · must vs mustn't 对照
> 场景：诊所看医生（承接 L31 的 Kayla）

## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: How many days must Kayla rest?
（a) Two　b) Three ✓　c) Five）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | DR. PATEL: Hi, Kayla. What brings you in today? | 帕特尔医生：你好，凯拉。今天哪里不舒服？ |
| 2 | KAYLA: I have a bad cold, and my throat hurts. | 凯拉：我重感冒，嗓子疼。 |
| 3 | DR. PATEL: Let's take a look... Say "ahh." | 帕特尔医生：我看看……说"啊——"。 |
| 4 | KAYLA: Ahh. | 凯拉：啊——。 |
| 5 | DR. PATEL: Okay. It's not serious, but you must rest for three days. | 帕特尔医生：嗯，不严重，但你必须休息三天。 |
| 6 | KAYLA: Three days? I have a big meeting on Friday! | 凯拉：三天？我周五有个重要会议！ |
| 7 | DR. PATEL: Health comes first. You must drink a lot of water. | 帕特尔医生：健康第一。你必须多喝水。 |
| 8 | DR. PATEL: And you mustn't go to work. No exceptions! | 帕特尔医生：而且千万不能去上班。没得商量！ |
| 9 | KAYLA: Okay, doctor. Anything else? | 凯拉：好吧，医生。还有别的吗？ |
| 10 | DR. PATEL: Yes—you mustn't drink coffee for now. Only water and soup. | 帕特尔医生：有——这段时间不许喝咖啡。只能喝水和汤。 |
| 11 | KAYLA: No coffee?! That's the hard part. | 凯拉：不能喝咖啡？！这才是最难的。 |

## 关2–4 · 核心词汇

| 词汇 | 美式音标 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 / 搭配 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| What brings you in? | — | 短语 | 今天哪里不舒服（医生开场） | What brings you in today? |
| hurt | /hɜrt/ | v. | 疼 | My throat hurts. |
| take a look | /teɪk ə lʊk/ | 短语 | 看一看 | Let's take a look. |
| Say "ahh." | — | 短语 | 说"啊——"（检查喉咙） | Say "ahh." |
| serious | /ˈsɪriəs/ | adj. | 严重的 | It's not serious. |
| meeting | /ˈmitɪŋ/ | n. | 会议 | a big meeting |
| health | /hɛlθ/ | n. | 健康 | Health comes first. |
| Health comes first. | — | 短语 | 健康第一 | Health comes first. |
| exception | /ɪkˈsɛpʃən/ | n. | 例外 | No exceptions! |
| soup | /sup/ | n. | 汤 | water and soup |
| for now | /fɔr naʊ/ | 短语 | 暂时；这段时间 | no coffee for now |
| the hard part | — | 短语 | 最难的部分 | That's the hard part. |

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① must + 动词原形（必须——医嘱/规定的强制要求）**
- You **must rest** for three days. / You **must drink** a lot of water.

**② mustn't + 动词原形（禁止——千万不能）**
- You **mustn't go** to work. / You **mustn't drink** coffee.

⚠️ mustn't ≠ "不必"，是"**禁止**"！"不必"下一课学（don't need to）。

**③ must vs mustn't 对照**

| 情态 | 含义 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| must | 必须做 | You must rest. |
| mustn't | 禁止做 | You mustn't go to work. |

**④ 三单复现**：My throat hurt**s**. / Health come**s** first.

## 关6 · 美语点睛

| 美语 🇺🇸 | 英语 🇬🇧 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| What brings you in today? | What seems to be the problem? | 医生看诊开场白 |
| Say "ahh." | 同（诊室经典） | 检查喉咙的指令 |
| Health comes first. | 同（俗语） | 健康第一 |
| No exceptions! | 同（口语） | 没得商量/没有例外 |

## 关7 · 对话填空

DR. PATEL: What ___1___ you in today?
KAYLA: My throat ___2___.
DR. PATEL: You ___3___ rest for three days.
DR. PATEL: You ___4___ go to work.
DR. PATEL: Only water and ___5___.

| 空 | 选项 | 答案 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a) brings　b) bring　c) bringing　d) brought | a |
| 2 | a) hurt　b) hurting　c) hurts　d) is hurt | c |
| 3 | a) mustn't　b) must　c) don't　d) aren't | b |
| 4 | a) must　b) need　c) don't be　d) mustn't | d |
| 5 | a) soup　b) coffee　c) soda　d) juice | a |

## 关8 · 听对话理解题

1. Where is Kayla? — a) At the doctor's office ✓　b) At work　c) At home　d) At a store
2. How many days must Kayla rest? — a) Two　b) Five　c) Three ✓　d) Seven
3. What mustn't Kayla drink for now? — a) Water　b) Soup　c) Juice　d) Coffee ✓

## 关9 · 情景应答

1. 医生嘱咐病人必须多喝水 → You must drink a lot of water.
2. 告诉朋友这几天千万不能上班 → You mustn't go to work.
3. 劝人健康第一 → Health comes first.

## 关10 · 本课通关

A. 选择填空
1. You ___ rest today.（必须）　a) must　b) mustn't　c) can't　d) don't → **a**
2. You ___ drink coffee now.（禁止）　a) must　b) needn't　c) mustn't　d) aren't → **c**
3. must 后面接：　a) 动词-ing　b) 动词原形　c) to+动词　d) 动词三单 → **b**
4. mustn't 表示：　a) 不必　b) 可以　c) 建议　d) 禁止 → **d**

B. 句型转换
1. You go to work.（用 mustn't 表禁止）→ You mustn't go to work.
2. You rest for three days.（用 must 表必须）→ You must rest for three days.
3. You must drink coffee.（改为禁止）→ You mustn't drink coffee.

# Lesson 33  You don't need to worry
## 你不必担心

> 语法目标：need to / don't need to（不必） · Do I need to...? · 情态三分小结（must / mustn't / don't need to）
> 场景：青少年和爸爸谈周六外出条件

## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: What does Hailey need to clean?
（a) The kitchen　b) Her room ✓　c) The garage）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | HAILEY: Dad, my friends are going to the mall today. Can I go? | 海莉：爸，我朋友们今天去商场。我能去吗？ |
| 2 | DAD: Sure. But first—is your room clean? | 爸爸：可以。不过先问一句——你房间收拾了吗？ |
| 3 | HAILEY: ...Almost. Do I need to clean it right now? | 海莉：……差不多吧。非得现在打扫吗？ |
| 4 | DAD: Yes, you do. But you don't need to wash the dishes—I'm on it. | 爸爸：是的。不过碗你不用洗——我来。 |
| 5 | HAILEY: Deal! What about homework? | 海莉：成交！那作业呢？ |
| 6 | DAD: It's Saturday. You don't need to do it today. But you need to finish it by Sunday night. | 爸爸：今天周六，不必今天写。但周日晚上前必须写完。 |
| 7 | HAILEY: Fair. Anything else? | 海莉：合理。还有吗？ |
| 8 | DAD: One more thing: text me every hour. | 爸爸：最后一条：每小时给我发条短信。 |
| 9 | HAILEY: Every hour?! Dad, you don't need to worry so much. | 海莉：每小时？！爸，你不用这么操心。 |
| 10 | DAD: Ha! Okay—every two hours. Final offer. | 爸爸：哈哈！行——每两小时。最终条件。 |
| 11 | HAILEY: Deal! You're the best! | 海莉：成交！你最好了！ |

## 关2–4 · 核心词汇

| 词汇 | 美式音标 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 / 搭配 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mall | /mɔl/ | n. | 购物中心 | go to the mall |
| clean | /klin/ | adj./v. | 干净的；打扫 | clean my room |
| need to | /nid tu/ | 短语 | 需要做 | need to clean it |
| almost | /ˈɔlˌmoʊst/ | adv. | 差不多；几乎 | ...Almost. |
| finish | /ˈfɪnɪʃ/ | v. | 完成 | finish it |
| by | /baɪ/ | prep. | 在……之前（截止） | by Sunday night |
| one more thing | — | 短语 | 还有一件事 | One more thing: ... |
| text | /tɛkst/ | v. | 发短信 | text me |
| hour | /ˈaʊər/ | n. | 小时 | every hour |
| every hour | /ˈɛvri ˈaʊər/ | 短语 | 每小时 | text me every hour |
| so much | /soʊ mʌtʃ/ | 短语 | 这么多；那么 | worry so much |
| Final offer. | — | 短语 | 最终条件（谈判口吻） | Final offer. |

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① need to + 动词原形（需要做）**：You **need to clean** your room. / You **need to finish** it by Sunday.

**② don't need to（不必——没有必要）**：You **don't need to wash** the dishes. / You **don't need to worry**.

**③ Do I need to...? 问答**：Do I need to clean it now? — Yes, you **do**. / No, you **don't**.

**④ 情态三分小结（本单元核心表）**

| 情态 | 含义 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| must | 必须做 | You must rest. |
| mustn't | 禁止做 | You mustn't go to work. |
| don't need to | 不必做 | You don't need to worry. |

## 关6 · 美语点睛

| 美语 🇺🇸 | 英语 🇬🇧 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| don't need to / don't have to | needn't（偏书面） | "不必"的美语口语 |
| mall | shopping centre | 购物中心（美国青少年周末据点） |
| text（动词） | 同（手机时代新动词） | 发短信 |
| Final offer. | 同（谈判口吻） | 最终条件，就这样了 |

## 关7 · 对话填空

HAILEY: Do I ___1___ to clean it right now?
DAD: You ___2___ need to wash the dishes.
DAD: You need to ___3___ it by Sunday night.
DAD: ___4___ me every hour.
HAILEY: You don't need to ___5___ so much.

| 空 | 选项 | 答案 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a) need　b) needs　c) must　d) needing | a |
| 2 | a) mustn't　b) aren't　c) don't　d) can't | c |
| 3 | a) finishing　b) finish　c) finishes　d) finished | b |
| 4 | a) Call to　b) Talk　c) Say　d) Text | d |
| 5 | a) worry　b) worries　c) worrying　d) worried | a |

## 关8 · 听对话理解题

1. Where does Hailey want to go? — a) The mall ✓　b) The beach　c) The library　d) A wedding
2. What does Dad say Hailey doesn't need to do? — a) Clean her room　b) Wash the dishes ✓　c) Text him　d) Finish homework
3. How often must Hailey text Dad in the end? — a) Every hour　b) Every day　c) Every two hours ✓　d) Never

## 关9 · 情景应答

1. 说你今天不必上班 → I don't need to work today.
2. 问是不是现在就得打扫房间 → Do I need to clean it right now?
3. 谈判后给出最终条件 → Final offer.

## 关10 · 本课通关

A. 选择填空
1. You ___ need to bring anything.（不必）　a) don't　b) mustn't　c) aren't　d) can't → **a**
2. Do I ___ to finish it today?　a) needs　b) must　c) need　d) needing → **c**
3. "不必做"用：　a) mustn't　b) don't need to　c) must　d) can't → **b**
4. "你必须完成作业"用：　a) don't need to　b) needn't　c) mustn't　d) must → **d**

B. 句型转换
1. You need to clean your room.（变否定：不必）→ You don't need to clean your room.
2. I need to clean the room.（变一般疑问句）→ Do I need to clean the room?
3. You must wash the dishes.（改为"不必"）→ You don't need to wash the dishes.
