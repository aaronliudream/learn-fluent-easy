### s5 seq1 | ans: will laugh
单纯说将来会怎样,用一般将来时 will + 动词原形。✓ Some people will laugh. ✗ are laughing:现在进行;✗ laughed:过去;✗ have laughed:现在完成。

### s5 seq2 | ans: will never be
一般将来的否定,把 never 放在 will 之后。✓ You will never be too old to learn. ✗ are never:一般现在;✗ were never:过去;✗ have never been:现在完成。
