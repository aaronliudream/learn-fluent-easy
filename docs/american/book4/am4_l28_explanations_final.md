### s5 seq10 | ans: quicker
‘没有比…更快的办法’,quick 是单音节形容词,比较级加 -er:quicker。✓ no quicker way than … ✗ quickest(最高级)/ quick(原级)/ more quick:单音节用 -er。

### s9 seq1 | ans: Although our faith in old beliefs has weakened, our trust in medicine has not.
although 引导让步,‘尽管’;英语里 although 已表转折,句中不能再加 but(不写 …weakened, but…)、也不用 Despite + 从句、语序为 has weakened(不写 weakened has)。✓ Although …, … has not. 场合:说“尽管…,却…”。

### s9 seq2 | ans: Patients feel neglected unless they carry home a bottle of medicine.
unless 引导‘除非’的条件;从句用一般现在时 carry(不用 will carry、不用 -ing carrying)、且不能用 without + 从句替代。✓ … unless they carry home … 场合:说“除非…否则…”。

### s9 seq3 | ans: There is no quicker way to please patients than giving them pills.
