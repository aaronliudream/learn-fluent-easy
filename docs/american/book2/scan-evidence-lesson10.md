## 一、教材(新概念英语第二册 · WeLib 443pg)—— 定主考

- **PDF p50(印47)之后**:仍属 Lesson 9 收尾。
- **PDF p51(印48)**:`Lesson 10 Not for jazz 不适于演奏爵士乐` — First listen…(What happened to the clavichord?)+ 课文。核心句(整篇被动叙事):
  - It **is called** a clavichord. It **was made** in Germany in 1681. Our clavichord **is kept** in the living room. It **has belonged** to our family… The instrument **was bought** by my grandfather… it **was damaged** by a visitor… two of the strings **were broken**… Now we **are not allowed** to touch it. It **is being repaired** by a friend of my father's.
  - New words:jazz、musical、instrument、clavichord、recently、damage、key、string、shock、allow、touch(11 词)。
  - **Notes(考纲边界权威)**:① **本课的许多动词都是被动语态**(is called/was bought/it was damaged/two of the strings were broken);② **a friend of my father's = 双重所有格**('s 和 of 同用)。
- **PDF p52(印49)**:`Key structures 关键句型 · It was made in Germany in 1681.(一般过去时中的)被动语态`(**主考权威页**):
  - 主动 vs 被动:Who built this bridge? → This bridge **was built** in 1942(**by** prisoners of war)。
  - 主动↔被动配对:Workmen are building a road → A new road **is being built**;The newsagent delivers papers → Our papers **are delivered**;The postman delivered a letter → A letter **was delivered**。
  - 改写:The police arrested the thief → The thief **was arrested**;He gave me a present → I **was given** a present;The headmaster has punished the boy → The boy **has been punished**。

## 二、语法同步(新概念第二册语法同步 · 356pg)—— 定辅考(岔开主题)

- **PDF p75(印74)**:`LESSON 10 Not for jazz` 独立标题页,专题=**一、名词的属格('s / of 所有格)**(**与教材主题分歧**):
  - (一)所有格构成:单数 +'s / 复数 +' / 复合名词末词 +'s / and 并列名词共有末词'​s。
  - (二)所有格用法:有生命名词('s);无生命名词(时间距离 today's paper / 重量度量 two pounds' weight / 国家城市 China's population / 交通工具 the car's engine);省名词所有格(Michael's / at the St. Paul's / go to my sister's)。
- **PDF p76(印75)**:(三)of 属格(无生命 the name of the song;有生命+短语/从句 the name of the boy sitting next to her);(四)所有格意义(主谓关系 the teacher's request 等)。
- **裁决处置**:名词所有格辅考('s 基本 + of 属格 + 双重所有格 2-3题 + 语言点,因注释②"a friend of my father's"实际出现=①类);**同步所有格章深度细则(无生命属格 today's paper / two pounds' weight / of 属格进阶等)挂账 PROGRESS,并入后续合适课次**。

## 四、三源边界裁决(A+覆盖补丁)

- **主考(教材 Key structures)=被动语态**:be+过去分词 · 一般现在被动(is/are+pp)· 一般过去被动(was/were+pp,本课 title 主线)· by+动作执行者 · 主动↔被动转换 · 被动否定/疑问 · 被动辨识。题量大头(15题)。
- **辅考(同步岔题,注释②实际出现)=名词所有格**:'s 基本用法 · of 属格 · 双重所有格(a friend of my father's)。2-3题 + 关6/语言点(3题)。
- **展示不设题**:进行时被动(is being repaired)· 现在完成/将来被动(has been/will be + pp)——新思维八种时态里有,只展示。
- **挂账 PROGRESS**:同步所有格章深度细则(无生命属格 today's paper/two pounds' weight、of 属格进阶),并入后续课,单元2末汇报检查清零。
