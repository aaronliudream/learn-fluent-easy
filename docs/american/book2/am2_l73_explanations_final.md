### s5 seq1 | ans: who
指“人”并在后半句里作主语时,用 who 把两句连成一句。✓ Children who play truant are unimaginative. ✗ which:指物;✗ whose:表“谁的”;✗ whom:作宾格,此处需要主格。

### s5 seq2 | ans: who
指“人(a boy)”且在从句里作主语,用 who。✓ a boy who traveled 1,600 miles. ✗ which:指物;✗ whose:表所属;✗ where:指地点。

### s5 seq3 | ans: whose
表“他的书(book 属于 James)”用 whose。✓ James Sullivan, whose book was published recently… ✗ who:作主语,不表所属;✗ which:指物且不表所属;✗ who is:是“他是”,意思不对。

### s5 seq4 | ans: When
“当…的时候”用 When 引出时间。✓ When he woke up next morning, he discovered… ✗ Which:指物;✗ What:表“什么”;✗ Who:表“谁”。

### s5 seq5 | ans: as
“正当…的时候、一边…一边”用 as。✓ No one noticed the boy as he crept off. ✗ so:表“所以”;✗ or:表“或者”;✗ for:表“因为”。

### s5 seq6 | ans: After being
“被…之后”用 After being + 过去分词(被动的分词短语)。✓ After being picked up by a policeman, he was sent back. ✗ After he picked up:意思成了“他捡起”,不是“他被带走”;✗ After picking up:主动,意思反了;✗ After been:缺 being。

### s5 seq7 | ans: Although
“尽管、虽然”用 Although 引出让步。✓ Although the police searched everywhere, the boy could not be found. ✗ Because:表原因;✗ So:表结果;✗ Unless:表“除非”。

### s5 seq8 | ans: that
“In spite of the fact ___”后面用 that 引出要说明的事实。✓ In spite of the fact that fares have increased… ✗ which/what/why:都不能这样引出 fact 后的说明内容。

### s5 seq9 | ans: Though
“尽管…”用 Though,后面可直接接形容词(Though very tired)。✓ Though very tired, he kept on walking. ✗ Because:表原因;✗ So:表结果;✗ Unless:表“除非”。

### s5 seq10 | ans: not only
“不仅…而且…as well”的前半用 not only。✓ The climbers not only reached the top but spent the night there as well. ✗ only:少了 not,不成“不仅…而且”;✗ either / neither:与 but…as well 搭配不上。

### s5 seq11 | ans: such
后面接“a fright(名词短语)”时,“如此…以致”用 such…that。✓ He gave me such a fright that I knocked the teapot over. ✗ so:so 后接形容词/副词(so frightened),不接名词短语;✗ very / too:构不成“如此…以致”的结构。

### s5 seq12 | ans: before
“在…之前”用 before。✓ He fought the wolves off for three hours before help arrived. ✗ after:表“之后”;✗ since:表“自从”;✗ until:表“直到”,与此处“救援到来之前一直搏斗”语义不同。

### s5 seq13 | ans: was given
“(某人)被给了某物”用 was given + 物(被动)。✓ The boy was given a few cookies by the driver. ✗ gave:主动,意思成了“男孩给”;✗ was giving:进行,不合;✗ has given:现在完成主动。

### s5 seq14 | ans: was picked up
一般过去的被动 = was + 过去分词。✓ There he was picked up by a policeman. ✗ picked up:主动,意思成了“他抓住”;✗ was picking up:进行主动;✗ has picked up:现在完成主动。

### s5 seq15 | ans: wasn't noticed
“没被(任何人)注意到”用否定的被动 wasn't noticed(by anyone)。✓ He crept off and wasn't noticed by anyone. ✗ didn't notice:主动,意思成了“他没注意到”;✗ wasn't noticing:进行;✗ hasn't noticed:现在完成主动。

### s5 seq16 | ans: evading
“逃避、逃学”用 evade,其 -ing 形式 evading 作 of 的宾语。✓ Thousands of children dream of evading school. ✗ preventing:是“阻止”;✗ refusing:是“拒绝”;✗ denying:是“否认”。

### s5 seq17 | ans: ashamed
“(人)感到羞愧”用 ashamed(feel ashamed)。✓ They should feel ashamed. ✗ shameful:是“可耻的”,修饰“事”不修饰“人的感受”;✗ shame:是名词;✗ shyness:是“害羞、腼腆”,意思不同。

### s5 seq18 | ans: imagination
“想象力(名词)”用 imagination,haven't much + 名词。✓ They haven't much imagination. ✗ imaginary:是“虚构的(形容词)”;✗ imaginative:是“富有想象力的(形容词)”;✗ image:是“形象、图像”。
