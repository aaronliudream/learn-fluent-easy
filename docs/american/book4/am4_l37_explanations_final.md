### s5 seq1 | ans: its
‘最…’= at its most + 形容词;填形容词性物主代词 its。✓ at its most vigorous. ✗ it(主格/宾格)/ it's(= it is)/ their(复数):此处用 its。

### s5 seq2 | ans: least
‘最小’,little 的最高级是 least。✓ the likelihood of death is least. ✗ less(比较级)/ little(原级)/ lesser:最高级用 least。

### s5 seq3 | ans: most
‘最…之一’= one of the + 最高级;unpleasant 是多音节,最高级前加 most。✓ one of the most unpleasant discoveries. ✗ more(比较级)/ very / much:多音节最高级用 most。

### s5 seq4 | ans: yet
‘尚未…’= have yet to do;填副词 yet。✓ has yet to reach its full strength. ✗ still / ever / already:此结构用 yet。

### s5 seq5 | ans: yet

### s5 seq6 | ans: to
have yet + 不定式;填不定式符号 to。✓ has yet to be discovered. ✗ for / at / of:不定式用 to。

### s5 seq7 | ans: though
‘虽然…’的让步连词 though,可插在两逗号之间。✓ the loss of vigor, though imperceptible at first, … ✗ because / unless / since:让步用 though。

### s5 seq8 | ans: Though
‘虽然/尽管’的让步连词,置于句首用 Though。✓ Though lucky and robust, no one can live for ever. ✗ Because / Unless / So:让步用 Though。

### s5 seq9 | ans: though
