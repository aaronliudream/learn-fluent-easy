# AM2 L12 逐题解释(定稿) · 供 gen-book2-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: will arrive
将来要发生的事,用一般将来时 will + 动词原形。✓ They will arrive at noon tomorrow.

### s5 seq2 | ans: will sail
tomorrow(明天)是将来时间,用一般将来时 will + 动词原形 sail。✓ Captain Reed will sail tomorrow.

### s5 seq3 | ans: will meet
将来要做的事,用 will + 动词原形 meet。✓ We will meet him at the dock.

### s5 seq4 | ans: I'll see you tomorrow.
口语里 I will 常缩略成 I'll。✓ I'll see you tomorrow.

### s5 seq5 | ans: He'll come by train.
he will 口语缩略成 he'll,后面接动词原形 come。✓ He'll come by train.

### s5 seq6 | ans: will be
"离开(不在)"是将来的状态,用 will be。✓ He will be away for two months.

### s5 seq7 | ans: will be
"明天一早在码头"是将来在某个地方,用 will be。✓ We will be at the dock early tomorrow.

### s5 seq8 | ans: She won't be late.
一般将来时的否定 = won't(will not)+ 动词原形。✓ She won't be late.

### s5 seq9 | ans: Will he leave tomorrow?
一般将来时的疑问,把 will 提到句子最前面。✓ Will he leave tomorrow?

### s5 seq10 | ans: I will go to the party.
"将要去"用一般将来时 will + 动词原形 go(不是过去式 went)。✓ I will go to the party.

### s5 seq11 | ans: The play will be very successful.
只有 will be 表示将来;was/is/has been 分别是过去、现在、到现在为止。✓ The play will be very successful.

### s5 seq12 | ans: are going to
计划好、打算要做的事,用 be going to + 动词原形;主语 we 用 are going to。✓ We are going to visit our grandparents this weekend.

### s5 seq13 | ans: am going to
"打算下周买"用 be going to;主语 I 用 am going to + 动词原形 buy。✓ I am going to buy a new bike next week.

### s5 seq14 | ans: is going to
"乌云密布"是"要下雨"的迹象,用 be going to;It 用 is going to。✓ It is going to rain.

### s5 seq15 | ans: leaves
时刻表上确定的班次,用一般现在时也能表将来。✓ The train leaves at 8:20.

### s5 seq16 | ans: are having
已经安排好的事,可以用现在进行时表将来;We 用 are having。✓ We are having a party this Friday.

### s5 seq17 | ans: Shall
美语里 shall 只用于提议、征求意见:Shall I…? / Shall we…?。✓ Shall I carry your bag for you? 📌 表将来一律用 will,shall 表将来是英式/过时说法。

### s5 seq18 | ans: Shall we go?
提议"我们…好吗?"用 Shall we + 动词原形。✓ Shall we go? 这是 shall 在美语里最常见的活用法。

### s6 seq1 | ans: harbor
美语"港口"拼 harbor;英式拼 harbour。✓ sail from the harbor.

### s6 seq2 | ans: neighbor
美语"邻居"拼 neighbor;英式拼 neighbour。✓ Our neighbor, Captain Reed.

### s6 seq3 | ans: sailboat
美语"帆船"常说 sailboat;英式说 sailing boat。✓ his little sailboat.

### s6 seq4 | ans: 启程,出发
set out = 启程、动身出发。✓ He will set out at eight o'clock.
顺带记:停下 = stop;返回 = return / go back;到达 = arrive / get to.

### s6 seq5 | ans: 参加
take part in = 参加(活动/比赛)。✓ take part in a race.
顺带记:离开 = leave;负责 = be in charge of;放弃 = give up.

### s6 seq6 | ans: 为…感到自豪
be proud of = 为…感到自豪、骄傲。✓ We are proud of him.
顺带记:害怕… = be afraid of;厌倦… = be tired of;担心… = be worried about.

### s7 seq1 | ans: will sail
tomorrow → 一般将来时 will sail。✓ Captain Reed will sail from the harbor tomorrow.
✗ sails:tomorrow 是将来,不用一般现在 sails;✗ sailed:tomorrow 不是过去;✗ is sailing:这里陈述明天将起航这件将来的事,用 will,不用现在进行.

### s7 seq2 | ans: will meet
将来要做的事 → will meet。✓ We will meet him at the dock early.
✗ meet:将来的事不用一般现在 meet;✗ met:不是过去发生的;✗ are meeting:此处是泛说将来的送行安排,用 will 更贴切.

### s7 seq3 | ans: will be
将来的状态(不在) → will be。✓ He will be away for two months.
✗ is:将来的状态不用现在 is;✗ was:不是过去;✗ be:be 不能单独作谓语,前面缺 will.

### s7 seq4 | ans: will watch
将来要做的事 → will watch。✓ We will watch him win the race.
✗ watch:将来的事不用一般现在 watch;✗ watched:不是过去;✗ have watched:现在完成表"已经看过",与"将要看"矛盾.
