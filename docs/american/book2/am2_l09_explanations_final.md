具体钟点前用 at。✓ The clock stopped at five to twelve.（at + 几点几分）

### s5 seq2 | ans: At
at that moment(在那一刻)是固定说法,时刻点用 at。✓ At that moment, everybody laughed.

### s5 seq3 | ans: in
年份前用 in。✓ The war ended in 1945.

### s5 seq4 | ans: in
季节前用 in。✓ in winter / in summer.

### s5 seq5 | ans: in
"上午、下午、晚上"(the morning/afternoon/evening)前用 in。✓ in the morning.

### s5 seq6 | ans: in
in the morning / in the afternoon / in the evening 都用 in(这是要记住的固定搭配)。

### s5 seq7 | ans: on
星期几前用 on。✓ We went to the Town Hall on Wednesday.

### s5 seq8 | ans: on
具体日期(几月几号)前用 on。✓ My birthday is on November 7th.

### s5 seq9 | ans: on
具体某一天——星期几、几月几号、生日那天——前面都用 on。

### s5 seq10 | ans: in
"再过十分钟 / 十分钟后"这种"一段时间之后"用 in(常见于将来)。✓ The train is leaving in ten minutes.

### s5 seq11 | ans: in
in twenty minutes' time = 20分钟之后,同样用 in 表示"…之后"。✓

### s5 seq12 | ans: I saw him on Monday morning.
