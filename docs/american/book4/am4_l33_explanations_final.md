### s5 seq1 | ans: What
名词性从句作主语,从句里缺宾语‘意味着什么’,用 What。✓ What true education means is misunderstood. ✗ That(从句不缺成分才用)/ Which / Whether(表‘是否’,意思不符)。

### s5 seq2 | ans: that
同位语从句,补充说明 belief 的内容,用 that(从句完整不缺成分)。✓ the belief that knowledge alone makes us wise. ✗ which / what(要作成分)/ whose(表所属)。

### s5 seq3 | ans: that
宾语从句,作 mean 的宾语,用 that(陈述内容)。✓ does not mean that discipline has no place. ✗ what(要作成分)/ which / whether(表‘是否’,意思不符)。

### s5 seq4 | ans: in
‘介词 + 关系词’,‘在其中’= in which;填 which 前的介词 in。✓ a school in which children ask questions. ✗ on / at / of:表‘在(某环境)之中’用 in。

### s5 seq5 | ans: whose
定语从句表‘其目标’这种所属关系,用 whose。✓ a teacher whose goal is understanding. ✗ who(作主语)/ which / that:表所属用 whose。

### s5 seq6 | ans: that
限定性定语从句,先行词 mistake 指物、在从句里作主语,用 that。✓ a mistake that has lasted for centuries. ✗ who/whom(指人)/ what(不引导定语从句)。

### s5 seq7 | ans: be

### s5 seq8 | ans: think

### s5 seq9 | ans: be

### s5 seq10 | ans: Encouraged

### s5 seq11 | ans: filling

### s5 seq12 | ans: teaching

### s5 seq13 | ans: as

### s5 seq14 | ans: that
强调句 It is … that …,强调状语 through curiosity;连接词用 that。✓ It is only through curiosity that real learning happens. ✗ which(强调状语不用)/ what / when:强调句连接词用 that。

### s5 seq15 | ans: than
