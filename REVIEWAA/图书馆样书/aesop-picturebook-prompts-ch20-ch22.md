| p3 | 3 | 公牛眼皮都懒得抬："你落下来我没察觉，你飞走我也不会知道" |

**本章角色（三页都贴）**
```
the bull: ONE large brown bull standing in a green field, with TWO curved
pale horns, a broad muzzle, dark hooves and calm heavy-lidded eyes. He is
big, solid and unbothered.
the gnat: ONE small round cartoon gnat with a blue-grey body, TWO clear
round wings, thin legs and big round friendly eyes. The gnat is small but
clearly visible, about the size of the bull's eye.
```

### p1（句1）
```
A wide green field under a pale blue sky. ONE large brown bull stands
side-on, calm and still, chewing slowly. ONE small blue-grey gnat sits
comfortably on the TIP OF THE BULL'S LEFT HORN, settled down with its wings
folded, looking pleased with itself. The bull's eyes are half closed and he
takes no notice of the gnat at all. There is exactly ONE bull and exactly
ONE gnat in the picture.
```

### p2（句2）
```
The same green field, closer view of the bull's head. ONE small blue-grey
gnat has lifted off the tip of the bull's left horn and hovers just above
it with its TWO wings spread and blurred, one thin leg raised in a polite
little wave, its mouth open as if speaking. The bull's head fills much of
the frame. There is exactly ONE bull and exactly ONE gnat in the picture.
```

### p3（句3）
```
The same green field, close view of the bull's face from the side. The
bull's eyes stay HALF CLOSED and heavy-lidded, not turning toward the gnat
at all, his mouth open in a slow bored reply. ONE small blue-grey gnat
hovers in the air a little way from the bull's horn, wings spread, looking
slightly deflated. The bull is clearly paying no attention. There is exactly
ONE bull and exactly ONE gnat in the picture.
```

| p3 | 5–6 | 树上那个下来问"熊说了什么"；地上那个回敬一句 |

**本章角色（三页都贴）**
```
the bear: ONE large brown bear with a rounded head, small round ears, a
broad muzzle and calm dark eyes. He is big but gentle-looking, never fierce.
the climber: ONE young man with SHORT BROWN HAIR, wearing a GREEN TUNIC,
brown trousers and brown boots
the other traveler: ONE older man with GREY HAIR and a SHORT GREY BEARD,
wearing a BLUE TUNIC, brown trousers and brown boots
```

**配色锁（三页都贴）**
```
COLOUR PALETTE — the picture uses only these colours: green grass and
leaves, brown earth and tree trunks, pale blue sky, brown bear, green tunic,
blue tunic. There is no red anywhere in the picture.
```

### p1（句1–2）
```
A brown dirt road running through a green wood. ONE large brown bear walks
into view from the far end of the road, still some distance away, looking
ahead. Beside the road stands ONE tall tree with a thick brown trunk. The
young man in the GREEN TUNIC has already climbed up into the branches and
crouches among the green leaves, holding the trunk and peering down. The
older man in the BLUE TUNIC stands alone on the road below, turning toward
the bear with his hands raised in surprise. There is a wide stretch of open
road between the bear and the men.
```

### p2（句3–4）
```
The same brown dirt road. The older man in the BLUE TUNIC lies FLAT ON HIS
BACK on the road, completely still, arms straight down at his sides, eyes
closed, holding his breath. ONE large brown bear stands over him and lowers
its nose gently toward the man's shoulder, sniffing, its eyes calm and
curious, its mouth CLOSED. High above in the tree, the young man in the
GREEN TUNIC crouches in the leaves watching, both hands gripping a branch.
The bear is calm and gentle, simply sniffing.
```

### p3（句5–6）
```
The same brown dirt road, now empty of any bear. The young man in the GREEN
TUNIC has climbed down and stands on the road with one hand raised, smiling
and asking a question. The older man in the BLUE TUNIC stands up again,
brushing dust off his sleeve, and looks sideways at his companion with a dry
knowing expression, one eyebrow raised. The tall tree stands beside them.
There are exactly TWO people and no animals in the picture.
```

## 四、ch22 《橡树与芦苇》 The Oak and the Reeds — 3 句 → **1 / 1 / 1**

| 页 | 句 | 内容 |
|---|---|---|
| p1 | 1 | 河岸上的橡树被大风连根拔起，横倒在水面上 |
| p2 | 2 | 它落进一丛芦苇里，问"你们是怎么挺过来的" |
| p3 | 3 | 芦苇答："你跟它硬扛，我们来一阵风就弯一次腰" |

**本章角色（三页都贴）**
```
the oak: ONE big oak tree with a THICK BROWN TRUNK, spreading branches and
round green leaves, and a SIMPLE FRIENDLY CARTOON FACE on its trunk with two
round eyes and a mouth
the reeds: a clump of TALL SLENDER GREEN REEDS growing at the water's edge.
THREE of the reeds have SMALL SIMPLE CARTOON FACES with round eyes and small
mouths. The reeds are thin and flexible.
the river: calm light-blue water with a grassy green bank
```

### p1（句1）
```
A strong gale blows across a river bank. ONE big oak tree with a thick brown
trunk is being torn out of the green bank, its brown roots pulling up out of
the earth, its leafy branches bending hard to the right. Green leaves fly
through the air and the light-blue water below is rippled by the wind. The
oak's cartoon face looks alarmed, eyes wide. At the water's edge on the right
grows a clump of tall slender green reeds, all bending far over in the same
wind but still rooted.
```

### p2（句2）
```
The gale has passed and the air is calm. ONE big oak tree now lies ON ITS
SIDE across the light-blue river, its thick brown trunk stretched over the
water and its brown roots torn up and bare at the bank. The oak's leafy crown
has come down among a clump of tall slender green reeds at the water's edge.
The oak's cartoon face, turned sideways, looks up at the reeds and asks a
question, its mouth open. THREE of the green reeds stand UPRIGHT and
undamaged beside it, their small faces looking down at the fallen oak.
```

### p3（句3）
```
The same calm river bank. ONE big oak tree still lies on its side across the
light-blue water with its bare brown roots at the bank, listening. Beside it
THREE tall slender green reeds stand upright, and the nearest reed BENDS
GENTLY TO ONE SIDE in a light breeze, its small face turned toward the oak,
mouth open as it answers. The other two reeds also lean slightly in the same
breeze. A few soft white wind-lines curve through the air above them to show
the light breeze passing over the tops of the reeds.
```
