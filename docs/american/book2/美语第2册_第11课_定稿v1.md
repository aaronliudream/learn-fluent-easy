## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,时态语态满篇混用 + 连词 + ask sb to do)

> I was having dinner at a downtown restaurant when Tony Steele walked in. Tony worked in a lawyer's office years ago, but he is now working at a bank. He gets a good salary, but he always borrows money from his friends and never pays it back. Tony saw me, and he came over and sat down at my table. He has never borrowed a single dollar from me before. While he was eating, I asked him to lend me twenty dollars. To my surprise, he handed me the money immediately. "I have never borrowed any money from you," Tony said with a smile. "So now you can pay for my dinner!" I looked at the check on the table and could not help laughing. One good turn deserves another, I guess.
