### s5 seq1 | ans: were not
“直到1860年才……”用 not ... until;拳套“被引入”是被动,过去用 were not (introduced)。✓ Gloves were not introduced until 1860. ✗ were / are not / had:配不出“直到…才”的被动。

### s5 seq2 | ans: not until
强调“直到…才”用 It was not until … that …。✓ It was not until 1790 that he won. ✗ until not / not till until / no until:固定说 not until。

### s5 seq3 | ans: until
“直到…才”用 not ... until。✓ did not become champion until his third try. ✗ since / before / from:表“直到”用 until。

### s5 seq4 | ans: rose
“成名、地位上升”用 rise(过去式 rose),后面不接一个东西。✓ Mendoza rose to fame. ✗ raised(要接一个东西)/ rise / raise:这里用 rose。

### s5 seq5 | ans: raised
“举起(拳头)”后面接了一个东西,用 raise(过去式 raised)。✓ raised his fists. ✗ rose(后面不接东西)/ rise / raise:这里用 raised。

### s5 seq6 | ans: rose
“太阳升起”用 rise(过去式 rose),后面不接东西。✓ The sun rose early. ✗ raised(要接东西)/ raise / rises(时态不对):这里用 rose。

### s5 seq7 | ans: ago
从“现在”往前数多少年,用 ago。✓ two hundred years ago. ✗ before(从过去某点往前)/ since / past:距今用 ago。

### s5 seq8 | ans: before
从“过去某个时间点”往前数,用 before。✓ they had not been used before. ✗ ago(距今才用)/ then / later:过去往前用 before。

### s5 seq9 | ans: before
引导“在……之前”这个时间,用 before。✓ years before he met Humphries. ✗ ago / since / until:“在…之前”用 before。

### s5 seq10 | ans: could
“过去的一般可能/能力(有可能受伤)”用 could。✓ a fighter could get badly hurt. ✗ was able to(表某一次成功)/ can 现在 / is able to 现在。

### s5 seq11 | ans: was able to
“某一次终于成功做成”用 was able to,不用 could。✓ was finally able to beat him in 1790. ✗ could(表一般能力)/ can / is able to:一次成功用 was able to。

### s5 seq12 | ans: could
“过去的一般能力(一向跑得快)”用 could。✓ he could run faster than anyone. ✗ was able to(一次成功)/ can / be able:一般能力用 could。

### s5 seq13 | ans: by
被动语态里,动作的发出者用 by 引出。✓ admired by rich and poor. ✗ with / from / of:施动者用 by。

### s5 seq14 | ans: to learn
形容词 pleased 后接“to + 动词”:pleased to learn。✓ was pleased to learn so much. ✗ learning / learn / learned:pleased 后用不定式 to learn。

### s5 seq15 | ans: to have spent
形容词 sorry 后接不定式;对已经做过的事表示后悔,用 to have done。✓ sorry to have spent his money. ✗ spending / spend / spent:这里用 to have spent。
