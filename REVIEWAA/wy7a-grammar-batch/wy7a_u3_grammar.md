# 外研社七上 U3 语法(名词所有格)— 待审 v1

> code=`wy7-u3-possessive-nouns` · junior_fltrp/wy7A/U3/grade7 · 20题 · 答案分布[5, 5, 5, 5] · 难度{1: 9, 2: 8, 3: 3}

**summary**:表示“……的”:单数名词及不以 s 结尾的名词加 's(Tom's, children's);以 s 结尾的复数名词只加撇号 '(students', boys')。无生命的事物多用 of 结构(the door of the room)。两人共有一物,只在最后一个名词后加 's(Lucy and Lily's room);各有其物,则各自加 's(Tom's and Sam's bags)。

**examples**:This is Lily's bag. / These are the students' books. / the legs of the table

## 题目

**1. [d1]** This is ____ bag. It's not mine.
  - A)Lilys  B)Lilys'  C)Lily's  D)Lily  →**C**
  - 解析:单数名词表所属加 's。
**2. [d1]** ____ pencil is on the desk.
  - A)Tom's  B)Toms'  C)Toms  D)Tom  →**A**
  - 解析:单数名词加 's。
**3. [d1]** I have only one sister. That is my ____ room.
  - A)sister  B)sister's  C)sisters  D)sisters'  →**B**
  - 解析:只有一个姐姐,单数名词加 's。
**4. [d1]** There are many students in our class. These are the ____ books, not the teachers'.
  - A)student's  B)studentss  C)students  D)students'  →**D**
  - 解析:许多学生,以 s 结尾的复数名词只加撇号。
**5. [d1]** The ____ toys are new.
  - A)childrens  B)children  C)children's  D)childrens'  →**C**
  - 解析:不规则复数 children 加 's。
**6. [d1]** I have two brothers. These are my ____ bikes.
  - A)brothers  B)brothers'  C)brother's  D)brotherss  →**B**

**8. [d1]** The ____ room is over there.

**9. [d1]** Is this ____ ruler?

**10. [d2]** The door ____ the room is red.

**11. [d2]** There are five boys in this room. These are the ____ desks.
  - A)boyss  B)boys  C)boy's  D)boys'  →**D**

**12. [d2]** Do you like ____ new dress?

**13. [d2]** The colour ____ the car is black.
