## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,现在完成时叙事)

> My brother Tom has moved abroad. He is now living in Canada, and he has been there for six months. I have just received a long letter from him. Tom is an engineer, and he has already gotten a good job at a big firm. He works hard, but he has not felt tired yet. Since he arrived, he has traveled to a great number of different cities. He has recently bought a used car. Right now he has driven to a small town in the center of the country. Have you ever been to Canada? Tom has never lived abroad before. He is finding this trip really exciting!
