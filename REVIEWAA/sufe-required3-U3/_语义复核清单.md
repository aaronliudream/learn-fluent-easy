### u3.01 并列句/简单句省略(避免重复)
- "Xiaohan is going on a diet, but I don't think Xiaosong ____."
  **will✓** / does so / goes / is
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:省去重复的 go on a diet,用助动词 will 收尾即可。
- "My brother likes swimming, and ____ jogging in the morning."
  me / **I✓** / mine / myself
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:并列结构省去重复的动词 like,后半句主语用主格 I。
- "Yihui likes reading, Lingyun likes football and Shanshan ____ gardening."
  to like / liking / **likes✓** / like
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:三项并列重复同一动词 like,各分句保留 likes(主语第三人称单数)。
- "You'd better drink fat-free milk or ____ low-fat milk."
  drank / to drink / drinking / **drink✓**
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:or 连接的并列,省去重复的情态结构,保留原形动词 drink 与前一致。
- "She eats a lot of vegetables, but her brother ____."
  **doesn't✓** / isn't / won't / hasn't
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:省去重复的 eat a lot of vegetables,一般现在时否定用 doesn't。
- "Too much sugar is harmful to physical and ____ health."
  the mental / **mental✓** / a mental / mentally
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:physical and mental health 共用一个 health,省去重复名词,形容词 mental 直接并列。
- "I can run five kilometres, but my sister ____."
  doesn't / isn't / won't / **can't✓**
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:省去重复的 run five kilometres,情态动词 can 的否定用 can't。
- "Dark green, red ____ orange vegetables all have high levels of nutrients."
  **and✓** / but / so / that
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:三个并列形容词共用一个名词 vegetables,用 and 连接,省去重复的 vegetables。
- "Some students skip meals, others eat very little, ____ a few simply fast."
  that / **and✓** / which / what
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:并列分句用 and 连接,后句省去重复成分,只保留新信息。
- "My friends have different hobbies: Tom likes cycling and Ann ____ yoga."
  is / has done / **does✓** / will
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:do yoga 的 do 不能省,省去的是重复信息;此处保留 does(第三人称单数)接 yoga。
- "He looks much healthier than his cousin ____."
  is / has / done / **does✓**
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:比较句省去重复的 looks healthy,用助动词 does 收尾。
- "We can eat out tonight, or we ____ cook something simple at home."
  **can✓** / are / do / have
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:or 连接的并列省去重复的 can,后句保留情态动词 can。
- "Liz drinks soda while studying, but she knows she ____."
  isn't / **shouldn't✓** / doesn't / won't
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:省去重复的 drink soda,情态结构否定用 shouldn't。
- "Routine chores don't get your heart rate up the way jogging ____."
  is / has / **does✓** / done
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:比较中省去重复的 gets your heart rate up,用 does 替代。
- "He has already exercised today, but I ____ yet."
  didn't / wasn't / don't / **haven't✓**
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:省去重复的 exercised,现在完成时否定用 haven't(配 yet)。
- "Mike runs faster than John ____."
  **does✓** / is / run / ran
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:比较句省去重复的 runs,用助动词 does 收尾。
- "You should reduce salt, ____ leave it out altogether if you can."
  that / **or✓** / which / what
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:or 连接两个并列建议,后句省去重复的主语 you 与 should。
- "My sister got eight hours of sleep last night, but I only ____ six."
  get / getting / **got✓** / to get
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:并列句省去重复的 hours of sleep,保留过去式 got 接 six。
- "Some people love morning workouts, ____ others prefer evening walks."
  for / so / that / **but✓**
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:前后形成对比,用 but 连接两个并列分句。
- "She wanted to join the dance club, and her friends ____ too."
  **did✓** / were / have / are
  考点:并列句/简单句省略(避免重复) ｜ 解析:省去重复的 wanted to join the dance club,用助动词 did 收尾。

### u3.02 状语从句省略(主语与主句一致且含 be)
- "____ weak and tired, Yutong still went jogging in the afternoon."
  Because / **Though✓** / Unless / So

### u3.03 不定式 to 的省略 & 答语/对话省略
- "He wanted to go to the gym, but he was too tired ____."

  do / it / **to✓** / so
