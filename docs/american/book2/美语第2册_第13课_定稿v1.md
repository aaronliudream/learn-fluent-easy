## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,将来进行时密集)

> The Silver Hawks are a group of pop singers. At present, they are traveling all across the country. They will be arriving in our town tomorrow. They will be coming by train, and most of the young fans will be meeting them at the station. Tomorrow evening, they will be singing downtown at the Community Club. The Silver Hawks will be staying for five days, and they will give five performances. Hundreds of fans will be lining up outside the club for hours. As usual, the police will have a difficult time. They will be trying hard to keep order. It is always the same on these big occasions. What will you be doing when the Silver Hawks arrive?
