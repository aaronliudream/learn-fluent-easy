# 外研社七上 Starter 语法(综合基础(be动词·指示代词·冠词·名词复数))— 待审 v1

> code=`wy7-starter-basics` · junior_fltrp/wy7A/Starter/grade7 · 20题 · 答案分布[5, 5, 5, 5] · 难度{1: 11, 2: 6, 3: 3}

**summary**:be 动词随主语变化:I 用 am,单数用 is,复数或 you 用 are。指示代词:近指单数 this、复数 these,远指单数 that、复数 those。不定冠词:辅音音素前用 a,元音音素前用 an。名词复数:一般加 -s,以 s/x/ch/sh 结尾加 -es。There be 表存在:单数用 There is,复数用 There are。

**examples**:I am ready. / These are my books. / It's an orange. / There are three boxes.

## 题目

**1. [d1]** I ____ a middle school student.
  - A)be  B)are  C)is  D)am  →**D**
  - 解析:主语 I 用 be 动词 am。
**2. [d1]** This ____ my English teacher.
  - A)is  B)are  C)be  D)am  →**A**
  - 解析:单数主语用 is。
**3. [d1]** My parents ____ teachers.
  - A)is  B)are  C)be  D)am  →**B**
  - 解析:复数主语用 are。
**4. [d1]** You ____ my good friend.
  - A)is  B)be  C)are  D)am  →**C**
  - 解析:主语 you 用 are。
**5. [d1]** ____ is my new schoolbag.
  - A)They  B)This  C)These  D)Those  →**B**
  - 解析:近指单数用 this。
**6. [d1]** ____ are my classmates.
  - A)That  B)This  C)These  D)It  →**C**
  - 解析:近指复数用 these。
**7. [d1]** Look at ____ bird in the tree over there.
  - A)these  B)this  C)those  D)that  →**D**
  - 解析:远处的单只鸟,远指单数用 that。
**8. [d1]** This is ____ apple.
  - A)an  B)a  C)and  D)am  →**A**
  - 解析:元音音素开头的 apple 前用 an。
**9. [d1]** I have ____ new bike.
  - A)and  B)am  C)an  D)a  →**D**
  - 解析:辅音音素开头的 new 前用 a。
**10. [d1]** There are three ____ on the desk.
  - A)a book  B)bookes  C)books  D)book  →**C**
  - 解析:可数名词复数一般加 -s。
**11. [d1]** I can see two ____ in the room.
  - A)boxes  B)boxies  C)box  D)boxs  →**A**
  - 解析:以 x 结尾的名词复数加 -es。
**12. [d2]** ____ some students in the classroom.
  - A)There is  B)There are  C)They are  D)It is  →**B**
  - 解析:复数 students 用 There are 表存在。
**13. [d3]** ____ those your books on the floor? Yes, ____ are.
  - A)Are; it  B)Is; they  C)Are; they  D)Am; they  →**C**
  - 解析:复数主语 those 用 are;简答用复数代词 they。
**14. [d2]** This is not a pen. It's ____ eraser.
  - A)two  B)the one  C)a  D)an  →**D**
  - 解析:元音音素开头的 eraser 前用 an。
**15. [d2]** My father and mother ____ doctors.
  - A)is  B)are  C)be  D)am  →**B**
  - 解析:and 连接的复数主语用 are。
**16. [d2]** There are many ____ in my school.
  - A)buses  B)bus  C)buss  D)busies  →**A**
  - 解析:以 s 结尾的名词复数加 -es。
**17. [d2]** ____ a big tree near my school.
  - A)It is  B)There are  C)There is  D)They are  →**C**
  - 解析:单数 a big tree 用 There is 表存在。
**18. [d2]** Kate and I ____ in the same class.
  - A)are  B)is  C)am  D)be  →**A**
  - 解析:Kate and I 是复数,用 are。
**19. [d3]** This is ____ egg and ____ old box, not ____ new bag.
  - A)a; an; a  B)an; an; a  C)a; a; a  D)an; a; a  →**B**
  - 解析:egg、old 以元音音素开头用 an;new 以辅音音素开头用 a。
**20. [d3]** ____ that boy your brother? No, ____ isn't.
  - A)Is; it  B)Is; she  C)Are; he  D)Is; he  →**D**
  - 解析:单数主语 that boy 用 is;指男孩用主格 he。
