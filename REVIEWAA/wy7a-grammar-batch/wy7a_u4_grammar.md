# 外研社七上 U4 语法(频度副词)— 待审 v1

> code=`wy7-u4-adverbs-of-frequency` · junior_fltrp/wy7A/U4/grade7 · 20题 · 答案分布[5, 5, 5, 5] · 难度{1: 8, 2: 9, 3: 3}

**summary**:频度副词表示动作发生的频繁程度:always(总是)、usually(通常)、often(经常)、sometimes(有时)、never(从不)。位置:放在实义动词之前(He often plays.),但放在 be 动词之后(He is never late.)。提问频率用 how often。never 本身表否定,不再与 not 连用。表频率的短语(every day, twice a week)通常放在句末。

**examples**:She always helps her mum. / He is never late. / How often do you play sports?

## 题目

**1. [d1]** Ann ____ walks to school. She does it every day.
  - A)usually  B)never  C)sometimes  D)always  →**D**
  - 解析:“每天都”表示频率最高,用 always。
**2. [d1]** Ben ____ drinks coffee. He doesn't drink it at all.
  - A)never  B)often  C)always  D)usually  →**A**
  - 解析:“一点也不喝”表示从不,用 never。
**3. [d1]** We ____ go swimming, only now and then.
  - A)always  B)never  C)sometimes  D)usually  →**C**
  - 解析:“偶尔”用 sometimes。
**4. [d1]** My father ____ gets home at six; he does so almost every day.
  - A)often  B)usually  C)sometimes  D)never  →**B**
  - 解析:“几乎每天”用 usually。
**5. [d1]** Lily ____ reads English books; she reads them many times a week.
  - A)always  B)never  C)often  D)sometimes  →**C**
  - 解析:“一周很多次”用 often。
**6. [d1]** Kate ____ goes to bed at nine; she does so almost every night.
  - A)sometimes  B)usually  C)never  D)always  →**B**
  - 解析:“几乎每晚”用 usually。
**7. [d1]** My mother ____ cooks nice food. We enjoy it every day.
  - A)always  B)often  C)sometimes  D)never  →**A**
  - 解析:“每天都”用 always。
**8. [d1]** He ____ plays computer games, maybe once a month.
  - A)usually  B)often  C)always  D)sometimes  →**D**
  - 解析:“大约一个月一次”用 sometimes。
**9. [d2]** Tom is a good student. He is ____ late for class; he always comes on time.
  - A)always  B)never  C)often  D)usually  →**B**
  - 解析:“总是准时”即从不迟到,be 动词后用 never。
**10. [d2]** — ____ do you play sports? — Twice a week.
  - A)How often  B)How many  C)How long  D)How much  →**A**
  - 解析:提问频率用 how often。
**11. [d2]** My English teacher is ____ kind to us; she helps everyone every day.
  - A)never  B)often  C)sometimes  D)always  →**D**
  - 解析:be 动词后接频度副词;“每天都帮”用 always。
**12. [d2]** They ____ help others; they do good things many times a week.
  - A)always  B)sometimes  C)often  D)never  →**C**
  - 解析:实义动词前接频度副词;“一周很多次”用 often。
**13. [d2]** My uncle works far away. He is ____ at home from Monday to Friday.
  - A)usually  B)never  C)always  D)often  →**B**
  - 解析:离家远,周一到五几乎不在家,be 动词后用 never。
**14. [d2]** — How often does your sister go swimming? — She goes ____.
  - A)at home  B)by bus  C)twice a week  D)very well  →**C**
  - 解析:how often 提问频率,答句用频率短语 twice a week。
**15. [d2]** My grandma ____ watches TV in the evening; she watches it many times a week.
  - A)often  B)always  C)sometimes  D)never  →**A**
  - 解析:实义动词前接频度副词;“很多次”用 often。
**16. [d2]** We have an English lesson ____.
  - A)always  B)very  C)never  D)every day  →**D**
  - 解析:表频率的短语 every day 放在句末。
**17. [d2]** I visit my uncle ____.
  - A)never week  B)always day  C)twice a week  D)often a  →**C**
  - 解析:表频率短语 twice a week 放句末。
**18. [d3]** — Does Lily ever eat fast food? — No, she ____ eats it.
  - A)not ever  B)never  C)doesn't never  D)never not  →**B**
  - 解析:never 本身表否定,不再加 doesn't。
**19. [d3]** — How often does Ben go to the library? — He ____ goes there, about three times a week.
  - A)often  B)often is  C)is often  D)never  →**A**
  - 解析:often 放实义动词 goes 之前;一周三次频率高,不用 never。
**20. [d3]** Mr Li is a busy doctor. He is ____ free from Monday to Friday, but he ____ takes us to the park on Sundays.
  - A)always; sometimes  B)never; never  C)sometimes; never  D)never; sometimes  →**D**
  - 解析:周一到周五忙,be 动词后用 never;周日“有时”带我们去,实义动词前用 sometimes。
