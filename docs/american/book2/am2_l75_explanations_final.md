### s5 seq1 | ans: held
hold 是不规则动词,过去式是 held。✓ Last Saturday our school held its sports day. ✗ holded:不规则动词不加 -ed;✗ holds:一般现在;✗ holding:现在分词。

### s5 seq2 | ans: shot
shoot 是不规则动词,过去式是 shot(此处“猛冲”)。✓ the runners shot forward. ✗ shooted:不规则动词不加 -ed;✗ shoot:原形;✗ shooting:现在分词。

### s5 seq3 | ans: won
win 是不规则动词,过去式是 won。✓ We won by half a second. ✗ winned:不规则动词不加 -ed;✗ win:原形;✗ winning:现在分词。

### s5 seq4 | ans: entered
enter 是规则动词,过去式加 -ed = entered。✓ My class entered the relay race. ✗ enter:原形;✗ entering:现在分词;✗ enters:一般现在。

### s5 seq5 | ans: passed
pass 是规则动词,过去式 passed(超过)。✓ Our third runner passed two others. ✗ past:是介词/形容词,不是动词过去式;✗ passing:现在分词;✗ passes:一般现在。

### s5 seq6 | ans: shouted
shout 是规则动词,过去式 shouted。✓ The crowd shouted. ✗ shout:原形;✗ shouting:现在分词;✗ shouts:一般现在。

### s5 seq7 | ans: took
take 是不规则动词,过去式是 took。✓ I took a deep breath. ✗ take:原形;✗ taken:过去分词;✗ taking:现在分词。

### s5 seq8 | ans: fell
fall 是不规则动词,过去式是 fell(此处“落后”)。✓ At first we fell behind. ✗ fall:原形;✗ fallen:过去分词;✗ falling:现在分词。

### s5 seq9 | ans: came
come 是不规则动词,过去式是 came。✓ Hundreds of families came to watch. ✗ come:原形;✗ comes:一般现在;✗ coming:现在分词。

### s5 seq10 | ans: took
与过去时间 last Saturday 搭配,用一般过去 took(take place=举行)。✓ The race took place last Saturday. ✗ takes:一般现在;✗ will take:将来;✗ is taking:进行。

### s5 seq11 | ans: ago
“(一段时间)以前”用“时段 + ago”。✓ two weeks ago 两周前。✗ later:是“…之后”;✗ since:接时间点;✗ before:不这样直接接时段表“…前”。

### s5 seq12 | ans: ran
与 yesterday 搭配用一般过去;run 的过去式是 ran。✓ Yesterday I ran in the relay. ✗ run:原形;✗ will run:将来;✗ am running:现在进行。

### s5 seq13 | ans: didn't
一般过去的否定用 didn't + 动词原形。✓ I didn't expect to win. ✗ don't:一般现在;✗ wasn't:后面不接动词原形;✗ haven't:现在完成。

### s5 seq14 | ans: Did
一般过去的一般疑问用 Did 开头,后面的动词一律用原形。✓ Did your class win the relay race? ✗ Was:后面不接动词原形 win;✗ Do:一般现在;✗ Has:现在完成。

### s5 seq15 | ans: didn't
一般过去的否定用 didn't + 原形。✓ The other team didn't finish in time. ✗ wasn't:后面不接原形 finish;✗ hasn't:现在完成;✗ doesn't:一般现在。

### s5 seq17 | ans: whistle
