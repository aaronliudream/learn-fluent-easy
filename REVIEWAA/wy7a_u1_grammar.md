# 外研社七上 U1 语法(人称代词和物主代词)— 待审

> code=`wy7-u1-pronouns` · publisher=junior_fltrp · volume=wy7A · unit=U1 · grade=7 · 20题 · 高中同款mcq结构
> 素材:附录 Guide to the language use 印136-137 + 单元Grammar板块;题目来源标 source_type(textbook/authored)

**讲解 summary**:人称代词:作主语用主格(I/we/he…),作宾语或表语用宾格(me/us/him…);单独作答或带 not 的简短回答常用宾格(—Who is it? —Me.);it 可表时间/天气/距离;并列顺序 you, he and I。物主代词:形容词性(my/our/your/his/her/its/their)用在名词前;名词性(mine/ours/yours/his/hers/its/theirs)独立使用,代替“形容词性+名词”。

## 题目

**1. [d1·authored]** ____ am a middle school student now.
  - A)Mine  B)Me  C)I  D)My  →**C**
  - 解析:作主语用主格 I。
**2. [d1·authored]** My grandpa often tells ____ interesting stories.
  - A)I  B)me  C)mine  D)my  →**B**
  - 解析:作宾语用宾格 me。
**3. [d1·authored]** ____ school uniform is blue and white.
  - A)My  B)Me  C)I  D)Mine  →**A**
  - 解析:名词 school uniform 前用形容词性物主代词 my。
**4. [d1·authored]** Tom is my classmate. ____ is good at maths.
  - A)He  B)Him  C)His  D)He's  →**A**
  - 解析:作主语用主格 He；He's=He is。
**5. [d2·authored]** This blue pen isn't yours. It's ____.
  - A)my  B)I  C)mine  D)me  →**C**
  - 解析:名词性物主代词 mine 代替 my pen。
**6. [d1·authored]** Our teacher helps ____ with English every day.
  - A)we  B)our  C)us  D)ours  →**C**
  - 解析:作宾语用宾格 us。
**7. [d2·authored]** ____ is sunny and warm today.
  - A)This  B)He  C)That  D)It  →**D**
  - 解析:it 可表天气。
**8. [d2·authored]** — Is this schoolbag ____? — Yes, it's mine.
  - A)you  B)you're  C)your  D)yours  →**D**
  - 解析:名词性物主代词 yours 代替 your schoolbag；you're=you are。
**9. [d1·authored]** The students are cleaning ____ classroom.
  - A)they  B)their  C)them  D)theirs  →**B**
  - 解析:名词 classroom 前用形容词性物主代词 their。
**10. [d2·authored]** — Who broke the window? — ____.
  - A)Mine  B)I  C)Me  D)My  →**C**
  - 解析:单独作答常用宾格 Me；主格一般不单独使用。
**11. [d1·authored]** Lily loves ____ new bike very much.
  - A)hers  B)she  C)she's  D)her  →**D**
  - 解析:名词 bike 前用形容词性物主代词 her。
**12. [d2·authored]** The little dog is wagging ____ tail happily.
  - A)its  B)it's  C)his  D)it  →**A**
  - 解析:its 表示“它的”；it's=it is。
**13. [d2·authored]** These gloves are ____, not mine.
  - A)hers  B)her  C)her's  D)she  →**A**
  - 解析:名词性物主代词 hers 代替 her gloves；无 her's 写法。
**14. [d1·authored]** We are all proud of ____ school.
  - A)our  B)us  C)ours  D)we  →**A**
  - 解析:名词 school 前用形容词性物主代词 our。
**15. [d3·textbook]** When several pronouns are used together, English usually says ____.
  - A)I, he and you  B)he, I and you  C)I, you and he  D)you, he and I  →**D**
  - 解析:英语人称代词并列顺序为 you, he and I。
**16. [d2·authored]** It's about 20 kilometres from my home to school. ____ takes an hour by bus.
  - A)It  B)There  C)That  D)This  →**A**
  - 解析:it 可表距离，且 It takes… 表花费时间。
**17. [d1·authored]** The books are heavy. Can you help me carry ____?
  - A)them  B)theirs  C)their  D)they  →**A**
  - 解析:作宾语用宾格 them。
**18. [d2·authored]** This is Peter's eraser. The eraser is ____.
  - A)her  B)his  C)he  D)him  →**B**
  - 解析:his 既可作形容词性也可作名词性物主代词,此处名词性,代替 his eraser。
**19. [d2·authored]** My sister and I share a room. That bed by the window is ____.
  - A)me  B)my  C)mine  D)I  →**C**
  - 解析:名词性物主代词 mine 代替 my bed。
**20. [d1·authored]** Welcome to our school! Nice to meet ____!
  - A)your  B)you  C)yours  D)you're  →**B**
  - 解析:meet 后作宾语用宾格 you。
