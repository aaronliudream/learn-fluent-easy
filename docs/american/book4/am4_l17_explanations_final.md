# AM4 L17 逐题解释(定稿·原创承载·By+动名词+情态被动/turn out to do/while对比/非限定which/过去完成·人为的疾病) · 供 gen-book4-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: By
‘通过做…’表方式,用介词 By + 动名词。✓ By infecting a few rabbits, epidemics could be started. ✗ With / On / In:表方式‘通过’用 By。

### s5 seq2 | ans: be
情态词被动 could + be + 过去分词;中间那个词是 be。✓ could be spread by releasing sick rabbits. ✗ is / been / being:could 后用原形 be + 过去分词。

### s5 seq3 | ans: releasing
By + 动名词;release 的 -ing 是 releasing。✓ By releasing them into the burrows. ✗ released / release / to release:介词 By 后用动名词。

### s5 seq4 | ans: turned
turn out to do,‘结果成了…’;一般过去时用 turned。✓ A certain mosquito turned out to act as the carrier. ✗ turns(现在)/ turning / turn(原形):此处用过去式 turned。

### s5 seq5 | ans: to be
be unlikely to do:unlikely 后接不定式 to be。✓ unlikely ever to be completely exterminated. ✗ be / being / been:be unlikely 后用不定式 to be。

### s5 seq6 | ans: turned
turn out that …,‘结果发现’;一般过去时用 turned。✓ It turned out that only this rabbit was susceptible. ✗ turns / turning / turn:此处用过去式 turned。

### s5 seq7 | ans: While
‘当…时/然而’引导对比,用 While。✓ While the world was killing mosquitoes, Australia bred them. ✗ Because / Unless / Since:表对比用 While。

### s5 seq8 | ans: while
‘正当…时’引导同时对比,用 while。✓ multiplied while the settlers were watching helplessly. ✗ unless / although / whether:表‘正当…时’用 while。

### s5 seq9 | ans: was
过去进行时 was + 现在分词;主语 the rest of the world 单数用 was。✓ While the world was getting rid of the pest. ✗ is(时态)/ been / being:单数过去进行用 was doing。

### s5 seq10 | ans: which
非限定定语从句(逗号后)指前面的 Europe,用 which(不用 that)。✓ Europe, which had given the rabbit to Australia, … ✗ that(非限定不用)/ what / who(此处指物/国家用 which):用 which。

### s5 seq11 | ans: which
非限定定语从句,指物(the virus),用 which。✓ The virus, which spread far beyond one estate, … ✗ that / what / whose:非限定指物用 which。

### s5 seq12 | ans: which
非限定定语从句,指物(the rabbits),用 which。✓ The rabbits, which had no natural enemies, … ✗ that / what / whom:非限定指物用 which。

### s5 seq13 | ans: had
过去完成时 had + 过去分词;第一个助动词用 had。✓ By then the rabbits had overrun the continent. ✗ have / has / were:过去完成用 had done。

### s5 seq14 | ans: had

### s5 seq15 | ans: had

### s5 seq16 | ans: pest
损害庄稼、令人厌恶的动植物是 pest(害虫、有害生物)。✓ an animal that harms crops is a pest. ✗ pet 宠物;✗ guest 客人;✗ guide 向导——都不对。

### s5 seq17 | ans: susceptible
容易被伤害或感染的是 susceptible(易感的)。✓ easily infected means susceptible. ✗ immune 免疫的;✗ blind 盲的;✗ equal 相等的——都相反或不对。

### s5 seq18 | ans: exterminate
把某物种彻底消灭是 exterminate(灭绝)。✓ to wipe out completely is to exterminate. ✗ protect 保护;✗ feed 喂养;✗ raise 饲养——都相反或不对。

### s9 seq1 | ans: By infecting a few rabbits, local epidemics could be started.
