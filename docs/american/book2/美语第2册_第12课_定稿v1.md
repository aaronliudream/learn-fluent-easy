## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,一般将来时密集 + be going to)

> Our neighbor, Captain David Reed, will sail from the harbor tomorrow morning. We are going to meet him at the dock bright and early. He will be on his little sailboat, the Seabird. The Seabird is a famous little boat, and it has crossed the Atlantic many times. Captain Reed will set out at eight o'clock sharp, so we'll have plenty of time. We'll watch his boat leave, and then we'll say goodbye to him. "Good luck!" we will all shout from the dock. He is going to be away for about two months. We are all very proud of him. He will take part in an important race across the ocean. I think he will win, and we will welcome him home like a hero.
