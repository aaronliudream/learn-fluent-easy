### s5 seq1 | ans: had
间接引语里主句是过去时,“已经…”的从句常用过去完成 had + 过去分词。✓ He said that justice had been done. ✗ has(现在完成)/ have / is:配合过去主句用 had。

### s5 seq2 | ans: if
间接的一般疑问(是否)用 if 引导。✓ wants to know if the man was caught. ✗ that(引导陈述)/ what / does:是否用 if。

### s5 seq3 | ans: that
间接陈述用 that 引导(常可省略,但四选一时选 that)。✓ believes that the thief got what he deserved. ✗ if(是否)/ whether / what:陈述用 that。

### s5 seq4 | ans: The moment

### s5 seq5 | ans: Now that

### s5 seq6 | ans: The moment
