# 外研社七上 U2 语法(一般现在时)— 待审 v1

> code=`wy7-u2-simple-present` · junior_fltrp/wy7A/U2/grade7 · 20题 · 答案分布[5, 5, 5, 5] · 难度{1: 9, 2: 8, 3: 3}

**summary**:一般现在时表习惯或事实。主语为第三人称单数时,动词加 -s/-es(watch→watches, go→goes, study→studies, have→has);其余主语用动词原形。否定与疑问借助助动词 do/does:三单用 does/doesn't,其余用 do/don't,助动词后动词一律用原形。常与 every day、on Sundays 等状语连用。

**examples**:He plays football every day. / She doesn't like bananas. / Does your sister like music?

## 题目

**1. [d1]** He ____ football every day.
  - A)playing  B)to play  C)play  D)plays  →**D**
  - 解析:第三人称单数,动词加 -s。
**2. [d1]** She ____ TV after dinner.
  - A)watches  B)watch  C)watching  D)watchs  →**A**
  - 解析:watch 以 ch 结尾,三单加 -es。
**3. [d1]** My father ____ to work by bus.
  - A)gos  B)goes  C)go  D)going  →**B**
  - 解析:go 三单加 -es。
**4. [d1]** Lucy ____ a nice bike.
  - A)have  B)haves  C)has  D)having  →**C**
  - 解析:have 的三单形式是 has。
**5. [d1]** They ____ English at school.
  - A)study  B)studys  C)studying  D)studies  →**A**
  - 解析:复数主语用动词原形。
**6. [d1]** I ____ up at six every morning.
  - A)gets  B)got  C)get  D)getting  →**C**
  - 解析:主语 I 用动词原形。
**7. [d1]** We ____ our classroom every day.
  - A)cleaning  B)clean  C)cleans  D)to clean  →**B**
  - 解析:复数主语用动词原形。
**8. [d1]** Tom ____ his homework in the evening.
  - A)do  B)dos  C)doing  D)does  →**D**
  - 解析:do 的三单形式是 does。
**9. [d1]** The girl ____ her hands before lunch.
  - A)wash  B)washs  C)washes  D)washing  →**C**
  - 解析:wash 以 sh 结尾,三单加 -es。
**10. [d2]** ____ your sister like music?
  - A)Is  B)Are  C)Do  D)Does  →**D**
  - 解析:三单一般疑问用 Does + 动词原形。
**11. [d2]** ____ they go to school on Sundays?
  - A)Do  B)Are  C)Does  D)Is  →**A**
  - 解析:复数主语一般疑问用 Do。
**12. [d2]** My brother doesn't ____ milk in the morning.
  - A)drank  B)drink  C)drinks  D)drinking  →**B**
  - 解析:doesn't 后接动词原形。
**13. [d2]** — Does Anna live near here? — Yes, she ____.
  - A)lives  B)do  C)is  D)does  →**D**
  - 解析:三单一般疑问的肯定简答用 does。
**14. [d2]** Mike and I ____ swimming after school.
  - A)goes  B)to go  C)go  D)going  →**C**
  - 解析:并列主语为复数,用原形 go。
**15. [d2]** She ____ have breakfast at home.
  - A)isn't  B)doesn't  C)not  D)don't  →**B**
  - 解析:三单否定用 doesn't + 原形。
**16. [d2]** My friend ____ like sports at all.
  - A)doesn't  B)not  C)isn't  D)don't  →**A**
  - 解析:三单主语否定用 doesn't。
**17. [d2]** Lily ____ her grandma every weekend.
  - A)visiting  B)visit  C)visits  D)visites  →**C**
  - 解析:三单动词加 -s。
**18. [d3]** My friend Ben ____ like fish, but he ____ chicken very much.
  - A)doesn't; likes  B)isn't; likes  C)don't; like  D)doesn't; like  →**A**
  - 解析:三单否定 doesn't + 原形;肯定谓语 likes 加 -s。
**19. [d3]** Anna ____ in a small town. Every day she ____ to school and ____ her friends there.
  - A)lives; go; meets  B)live; go; meet  C)lives; goes; meet  D)lives; goes; meets  →**D**
  - 解析:三单主语,三个谓语都加 -s/-es。
**20. [d3]** — How often ____ Tom read books? — He ____ books every evening.
  - A)does; read  B)does; reads  C)is; reads  D)do; reads  →**B**
  - 解析:疑问句用助动词 does + 原形;陈述句谓语 reads 加 -s。
