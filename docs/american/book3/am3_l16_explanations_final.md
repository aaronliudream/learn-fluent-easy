### s5 seq1 | ans: of
accuse sb of doing sth“指控某人做了某事”,介词固定用 of。✓ accused Earl of stealing. ✗ for / with / about:accuse 搭配的介词是 of。

### s5 seq2 | ans: of
suspect sb of doing sth“怀疑某人做了某事”,介词也用 of。✓ suspected him of taking the money. ✗ for / to / with:suspect 搭配的介词是 of。

### s5 seq3 | ans: borrowing
介词 of 后面接动词要用 -ing 形式:of borrowing。✓ accused me of borrowing his tools. ✗ borrow / to borrow / borrowed:介词后要用 -ing。

### s5 seq4 | ans: taking

### s5 seq5 | ans: breaking

### s5 seq6 | ans: taking

### s5 seq7 | ans: of

### s5 seq8 | ans: for
apologize for doing“为做…而道歉”,介词用 for。✓ apologized for jumping to conclusions. ✗ of / to / in:apologize 后接 for + doing(to 用在人前面:apologize to sb)。

### s5 seq9 | ans: being
介词 for 后面接动词要用 -ing 形式:for being。✓ apologized for being so rude. ✗ to be / be / been:介词后要用 -ing。

### s5 seq10 | ans: had better
“最好…”的忠告用 had better + 动词原形。✓ You had better return the lamb. ✗ would better / had to better / better had:固定说 had better。

### s5 seq11 | ans: had better
“最好…”的忠告用 had better。✓ we had better leave now. ✗ would rather better / had best to / better to:固定说 had better。

### s5 seq12 | ans: tell

### s5 seq13 | ans: had better not
“最好别…”用 had better not + 原形,not 放在 better 后面。✓ You had better not accuse people. ✗ had not better / better not had / would not better:语序错误。

### s5 seq14 | ans: go
had better not 后面同样接动词原形:had better not go。✓ We had better not go out. ✗ to go / going / went:后用原形。

### s5 seq15 | ans: not call
“最好别做某事”用 had better not + 原形:'d better not call。✓ You'd better not call him now. ✗ not to call / don't call / no call:had better 的否定是 not + 原形。
