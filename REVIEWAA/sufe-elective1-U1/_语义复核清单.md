### u1.01 过去完成时(had done:过去的过去)
- By the time the visitors arrived, Curie ____ the long poem.
  **had already recited✓** / recites / has recited / will recite
  考点:过去完成时(had done:过去的过去) ｜ 解析:by the time + 过去动作之前已完成 → 过去完成时 had recited。
