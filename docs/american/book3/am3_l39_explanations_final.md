### s5 seq1 | ans: were
be able to 的过去式,主语复数用 were able to。✓ We were able to cross the plain. ✗ are(现在)/ will be(将来)/ being:过去用 were able to。

### s5 seq2 | ans: was
be able to 的过去式,主语单数用 was able to。✓ he was able to keep the car moving. ✗ were(复数)/ is(现在)/ being:单数过去用 was able to。

### s5 seq3 | ans: will be
be able to 的将来式用 will be able to。✓ we will be able to cross faster next time. ✗ are(现在)/ were(过去)/ being:将来用 will be able to。

### s5 seq4 | ans: managed
“设法做成(克服困难)”用 manage to do。✓ Tom managed to steer around every boulder. ✗ could(一贯能力)/ must / ought:设法做成用 managed to。
