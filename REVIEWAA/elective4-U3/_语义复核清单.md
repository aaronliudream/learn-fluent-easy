### u3.01 长句中的从句连接词(定语/状语/名词性从句:which/where/when/that/because/although/so that 等)

- I will never forget the moment ____ the manned submersible finally touched the deepest part of the sea.

  考点:长句中的从句连接词(定语/状语/名词性从句:which/where/when/that/because/although/so that 等) ｜ 解析:前后让步转折(虽被禁,仍有人捕猎)→ Although。

  考点:长句中的从句连接词(定语/状语/名词性从句:which/where/when/that/because/although/so that 等) ｜ 解析:表目的(为了日后能辨认)→ so that。

  which / that / what / **where✓**
  考点:长句中的从句连接词(定语/状语/名词性从句:which/where/when/that/because/although/so that 等) ｜ 解析:先行词 database 视作抽象地点、从句完整 → where(= in which)。

  which / where / that / **when✓**
  考点:长句中的从句连接词(定语/状语/名词性从句:which/where/when/that/because/although/so that 等) ｜ 解析:先行词 year 表时间、从句完整 → when。

  考点:长句中的从句连接词(定语/状语/名词性从句:which/where/when/that/because/although/so that 等) ｜ 解析:宾语从句缺主语、表'什么'→ what。

  考点:长句中的从句连接词(定语/状语/名词性从句:which/where/when/that/because/although/so that 等) ｜ 解析:先行词 possession、从句缺宾语(use up 的宾语)→ that(或 which)。

### u3.02 长句中的非谓语动词(doing / done / to do 作定语/状语/宾补)

- The deep ocean is too dark and too cold ____ without special equipment and powerful lights.

- ____ the most advanced research vessel in the country, Kexue draws the attention of scientists worldwide.

### u3.03 长句中的并列、强调与倒装(not only…but also;it is…that 强调;Only…倒装;neither/so 倒装)
- Sea exploration not only helps us find new medicines ____ also leads us to new sources of food and energy.
  **but✓** / and / or / so
  考点:长句中的并列、强调与倒装(not only…but also;it is…that 强调;Only…倒装;neither/so 倒装) ｜ 解析:not only … but also 固定并列结构 → but。
- It was the writing of the ships' logs ____ allowed later sailors to follow the very same routes safely.
  which / **that✓** / what / who
  考点:长句中的并列、强调与倒装(not only…but also;it is…that 强调;Only…倒装;neither/so 倒装) ｜ 解析:强调句 It was … that 强调主语 the writing → that。
- Only after the storm finally passed ____ the exhausted sailor able to see the distant shore.
  be / is / **was✓** / being
  考点:长句中的并列、强调与倒装(not only…but also;it is…that 强调;Only…倒装;neither/so 倒装) ｜ 解析:Only + 状语置句首引起部分倒装,主句用 was the sailor able → was。
- We can explore the sea to learn from it, ____ we can exploit it carelessly until nothing is left.
  and / but / for / **or✓**
  考点:长句中的并列、强调与倒装(not only…but also;it is…that 强调;Only…倒装;neither/so 倒装) ｜ 解析:两种相反选择(学习它 / 或耗尽它)→ or。
- The ocean is huge, ____ it is far more sensitive than most people are willing to believe.

  考点:长句中的并列、强调与倒装(not only…but also;it is…that 强调;Only…倒装;neither/so 倒装) ｜ 解析:前后转折(很大,然而脆弱)→ yet。
- It is climate change ____ this melting Arctic ice is warning us about so clearly and so urgently.
  who / **that✓** / which / what
  考点:长句中的并列、强调与倒装(not only…but also;it is…that 强调;Only…倒装;neither/so 倒装) ｜ 解析:强调句 It is … that 强调宾语 climate change → that。
