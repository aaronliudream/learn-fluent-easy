# L15《Good news》三源扫描凭证(间接引语 主考)

> 凭证纪律:凡"某书没有 X"结论必附逐页页眉清单。**本课=同主题讲深(非分歧)**:教材=间接引语,同步 L15=宾语从句(that/if/whether)+ 过去将来时(would)——正是间接引语的底层机制(that 从句 + will→would 后移)。

## 一、教材(新概念英语第二册 · WeLib 443pg)—— 定主考

- **PDF p71(印68)**:`Lesson 15 Good news 佳音` — First listen…(What was the good news?)+ 课文。核心(**满篇间接引语 told/said + that 从句 + 时态后移**):
  - The secretary **told me that** Mr. Harmsworth **would see** me.(will→would 后移)
  - I **felt** very nervous when I **went** into his office. He **did not look up** from his desk when I **entered**.
  - **After I had sat down**, he **said that** business **was** very bad.(过去完成 + 间接引语)
  - He **told me that** the firm **could not afford** to pay such large salaries.(can→could 后移)
  - Twenty people **had already left**. I **knew that** my turn **had come**.
  - 'Mr. Harmsworth,' I **said** in a weak voice. 'Don't interrupt,' he **said**.
  - Then he **smiled** and **told me** I **would receive** an extra thousand pounds a year!(will→would 后移)
  - New words(官方5):secretary(l.1)/ˈsekrətəri/、nervous(l.2)/ˈnɜːvəs/、afford(l.4)/əˈfɔːd/、weak(l.7)/wiːk/、interrupt(l.8)/ˌɪntəˈrʌpt/。
  - **Notes**:① look up=抬头看;② could not afford to=不可能支付(afford 常和 can/could 连用);③ I knew that my turn had come=我知道该轮到我了(指被解雇)。
- **PDF p72(印69)**:`Key structures 关键句型 · **He said that… He told me… 间接引语**`(**主考权威页**):
  - **直接→间接引语,say/tell + that + 从句,时态后移**:
    - 'I am busy,' he said. → He **said that** he **was** busy. / He **told me that** he was busy.(am→was)
    - 'I never work on Sundays,' she said. → She said that she **worked**…(work→worked)
    - 'I have just finished work.' → He said that he **had** just **finished**…(have finished→had finished)
    - 'I broke that plate.' → He said that he **had broken**…(broke→had broken)
    - 'Mr. Jones **will** see you now.' → She said that Mr. Jones **would** see you now.(will→would)
    - 'You **can** go now.' → The teacher said that you **could** go now.(can→could)
  - **say 后不接人;tell 后必须接人(间接宾语)**。
  - Exercise A:What did the secretary tell me? / What were the secretary's exact words?(间接↔直接互转)。
  - **裁决**:教材 Key structures 明标间接引语 → **本课主考=间接引语**(say/tell + that + 从句;say 不接人 tell 接人;时态后移 am→was/do→did/will→would/can→could/have done→had done;人称变化)。

## 二、语法同步(新概念第二册语法同步 · 356pg)—— 同主题讲深(宾语从句 + 过去将来时)

- **PDF p99(印98)**:`Lesson 15 Good news` 章,专题(**间接引语的底层机制,同主题**):
  - **一、宾语从句(that/if/whether)**:(二)that 在宾语从句中可省略(并列宾语从句时 that 不可省);(三)**if 还是 whether**——if 一般只引导宾语从句,whether 可引导其他名词从句;if 不与 or not 直接连用(不说 if or not),whether 无此限制。
  - **二、过去将来时**:(一)构成 would/should + 动词原形(would 通用/should 第一人称,缩写 'd);(二)用法——**从过去看将来(He said he would go / I told her I should return / She said she wouldn't borrow)= 间接引语里 will→would**;was going to;was to;was about to。
- **裁决处置**(同主题讲深):讲深辅考——宾语从句 that 可省 + if vs whether(转述疑问句 asked if/whether)2题 + 过去将来时 would/should(间接引语里 will→would 后移)2题;was going to/was to/was about to 过于细,只作理解不单设题(be about to 已在 L12 挂账)。

## 四、三源边界裁决(同主题讲深)

- **主考(教材 Key structures)= 间接引语**:say/tell + that + 从句 · say 不接人 tell 接人 · 时态后移(am→was/do→did/have done→had done/will→would/can→could)· 人称变化 · 转述陈述句改写。题量大头(14题)。

- **认知题 ≤3**:间接引语构成(G1)· say 不接人 tell 接人(G2)· 时态往过去移(G3)。
