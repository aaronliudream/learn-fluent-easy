# AM4 L47 逐题解释(定稿·原创承载·Not only倒装/whether主语从句/find oneself doing/far from doing·far less than/现在分词作后置定语·大逃亡) · 供 gen-book4-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: Not
‘不但…而且…’置于句首用 Not only;填句首的否定副词 Not。✓ Not only are the means within one's keeping, but escape is easy. ✗ No / Nor / None:与 only 搭配、领起倒装的是 Not。

### s5 seq2 | ans: is
Not only 置于句首,主句要部分倒装,把 be 动词提到主语 the car 前;填 is。✓ Not only is the car a means of arrival… ✗ it is(未倒装)/ does(此处是 be 动词句)/ being:倒装后 be 动词提前用 is。

### s5 seq3 | ans: but
not only … but (also) … 关联结构;与 not only 呼应的是 but。✓ not only Germans but also the Dutch. ✗ and / or / so:与 not only 搭配的是 but。

### s5 seq4 | ans: Whether
‘是否…’引导主语从句、置于句首作主语,只能用 Whether;if 不能引导主语从句。✓ Whether it will kill the freer kind is hard to say. ✗ If(不能引导主语从句)/ That(不表‘是否’)/ What。

### s5 seq5 | ans: Whether
与 or not 搭配、表‘无论…是否…’,只能用 Whether;if 一般不与 or not 连用于句首。✓ Whether the beach is crowded or not… ✗ If / That / Which。

### s5 seq6 | ans: whether
say 后接‘是…还是…’,与 or 搭配表选择,用 whether。✓ hard to say whether it is good or bad. ✗ that(不表选择)/ what / how。

### s5 seq7 | ans: himself
find + 反身代词 + 分词;主语是 he,反身代词用 himself。✓ he may find himself parked beside a car. ✗ him(宾格非反身)/ his(所有格)/ he(主格):此处要反身代词。

### s5 seq8 | ans: standing
find oneself + 现在分词,表‘发现自己正在…’;填 stand 的 -ing 形式 standing。✓ find yourself standing door to door. ✗ stand / stood / to stand:find oneself 后用现在分词。

### s5 seq9 | ans: sharing
find oneself + 现在分词;填 share 的 -ing 形式 sharing。✓ found themselves sharing a site. ✗ share / shared / to share:find oneself 后用现在分词。

### s5 seq10 | ans: from
‘远非…’= far from + 动名词;far 之后接介词 from。✓ far from being the only motive. ✗ of / than / off:此结构是 far from doing。

### s5 seq11 | ans: less
‘远比…少’= far + 比较级 + than;little 的比较级是 less。✓ far less than a run of hotel bills. ✗ little(原级)/ least(最高级)/ lesser(用于抽象‘次要的’):比较 than 用 less。

### s5 seq12 | ans: being
far from 后接动名词;形容词短语前加 being;填 be 的动名词 being。✓ far from being as dull as critics claim. ✗ be / been / to be:far from 后用动名词 being。

### s5 seq13 | ans: forbidding
现在分词作后置定语,表主动‘禁止…的告示’(= notices that forbid);填 forbid 的 -ing 形式 forbidding。✓ notices forbidding the drying of clothes. ✗ forbidden(过去分词表被动,意思变‘被禁止的告示’)/ forbid / to forbid。

### s5 seq14 | ans: inviting
现在分词作后置定语,‘邀请…的告示’;填 invite 的 -ing 形式 inviting。✓ signs inviting campers to a dance. ✗ invited(被动)/ invite / to invite:主动的后置定语用现在分词。

### s5 seq15 | ans: hoping
现在分词作后置定语修饰 traveler,‘渴望…的旅行者’;填 hope 的 -ing 形式 hoping。✓ a traveler hoping to get away from it all. ✗ hoped / hope / to hope:主动的后置定语用现在分词。

### s5 seq16 | ans: assumption
