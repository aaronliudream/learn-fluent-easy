### s5 seq1 | ans: overtake
与现在相反的虚拟:if we kept…(过去式)时主句用 might + 动词原形。✓ misfortune might overtake us. ✗ overtakes / overtook / to overtake:might 后接动词原形 overtake。

### s5 seq2 | ans: could
Without…(=If it were not for…)引导与现在相反的虚拟,主句用情态词的过去式 could + 原形。✓ we could not make a living. ✗ can(不是虚拟)/ will / may:此处用 could。

### s5 seq3 | ans: rose
与现在相反的虚拟,if 从句用一般过去式。✓ If the river rose higher… ✗ rises(不是虚拟)/ has risen / will rise:虚拟条件从句用过去式 rose。

### s5 seq4 | ans: stood
与过去相反的虚拟,主句用 would have + 过去分词。✓ it would have stood. ✗ stand / stood up / standing:would have 后用过去分词 stood。

### s5 seq5 | ans: had been
与过去相反的虚拟,if 从句用过去完成式 had + 过去分词。✓ even if it had been almost submerged. ✗ was / were(现在相反才用 were)/ has been:过去相反用 had been。

### s5 seq6 | ans: might not
与过去相反的虚拟,主句用 would/might + have + 过去分词。✓ they might not have panicked. ✗ may not(要用过去式 might)/ will not / do not:此处用 might not。

### s5 seq7 | ans: must
对过去的肯定推测“一定…了”用 must + have + 过去分词。✓ They must have become frightened. ✗ can(疑问/否定才用)/ would / need:表肯定推测用 must。

### s5 seq8 | ans: must
对过去的肯定推测,填 must。✓ The river must have risen very fast. ✗ shall / ought(须加 to)/ dare:表推测“一定”用 must。

### s5 seq9 | ans: have
must + have + 过去分词构成对过去的推测;此处填中间的助动词 have。✓ must have built. ✗ has / had / having:must 后一律用 have + 过去分词。

### s5 seq10 | ans: crowning
现在分词短语作伴随状语,主动“给…戴花冠”。✓ crowning the youngest child with flowers. ✗ crowned(被动)/ crown / to crown:表主动伴随用 -ing。

### s5 seq11 | ans: throwing
现在分词短语作伴随状语,主动“把…抛入”。✓ throwing a green wreath into the current. ✗ thrown(被动)/ throw / to throw:主动伴随用 -ing。

### s5 seq12 | ans: Giving
现在分词短语置于句首作状语,主动“为…致谢”。✓ Giving thanks for the harvest, we gather… ✗ Given(被动)/ Give / To giving:主动用现在分词 Giving。

### s5 seq13 | ans: carrying

### s5 seq14 | ans: swimming
现在分词修饰名词 horse,表主动“正在游”。✓ a horse swimming bravely. ✗ swum(过去分词)/ swim / to swim:表主动进行用 -ing。
