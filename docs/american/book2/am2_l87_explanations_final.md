### s5 seq1 | ans: had been training
主句是过去时(said),转述时时态后移:现在完成进行 have been training → 过去完成进行 had been training。✓ He said that they had been training hard. ✗ have been training:未后移;✗ are training:未后移;✗ train:未后移。

### s5 seq2 | ans: were
主句是过去时(told),转述时 are → were(时态后移)。✓ He told her that the players were ready. ✗ are:未后移;✗ will be:未后移;✗ have been:与直接引语 are 不对应。

### s5 seq3 | ans: sounded
主句是过去时(wrote),转述时 sounds → sounded(时态后移)。✓ The reporter wrote that the coach sounded very positive. ✗ sounds:未后移;✗ will sound:未后移;✗ has sounded:与直接引语 sounds 不对应。

### s5 seq4 | ans: told
后面直接跟人(her)时,用 tell。✓ He told her that it would begin at seven. ✗ said:后面不直接跟人;✗ spoke:不接 that 从句转述内容;✗ talked:不接 that 从句转述内容。

### s5 seq5 | ans: said
后面不跟人、直接接 that 从句,用 say。✓ He said that they had trained hard. ✗ told:后面要跟人;✗ told to:错;✗ spoke:不接 that 从句转述内容。

### s5 seq6 | ans: added
“补充说”用 add(过去式 added),后接 that 从句。✓ He added that he hoped the fans would cheer. ✗ told:后面要跟人;✗ spoke to:后面跟人不跟 that;✗ said to:错搭。

### s5 seq7 | ans: whether
转述一般疑问(是否),用 whether(或 if)。✓ The reporter asked whether the star player would join. ✗ that:用于陈述内容,不用于疑问;✗ what:引导“什么”;✗ how:引导“如何”。

### s5 seq8 | ans: if
转述一般疑问,可用 if(等于 whether)。✓ She asked if the team was ready. ✗ that:不用于疑问;✗ what:引导“什么”;✗ so:不引导疑问。

### s5 seq9 | ans: whether
转述一般疑问(是否),用 whether。✓ He asked me whether I had enjoyed the game. ✗ that:不用于疑问;✗ which:引导“哪一个”;✗ so:不引导疑问。

### s5 seq10 | ans: how
转述“如何”这类特殊疑问,用 how,从句用陈述语序。✓ She asked him how the team had prepared. ✗ that:不引导疑问;✗ whether / if:引导“是否”,与“如何”不符。

### s5 seq11 | ans: when

### s5 seq12 | ans: trained
