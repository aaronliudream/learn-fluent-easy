### u4.01 作定语:doing(主动/进行) vs done(被动/完成)
- From the window we watched the train ____ west across the open prairie.
  **heading✓** / headed / to head / head

- The road ____ with deep snow was hard for the bus to climb.
  covering / **covered✓** / to cover / covers
  考点:作定语:doing(主动/进行) vs done(被动/完成) ｜ 解析:road 被雪覆盖、是承受者(被动)→ 过去分词 covered。
