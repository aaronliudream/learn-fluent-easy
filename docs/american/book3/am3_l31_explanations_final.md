### s5 seq1 | ans: being
介词 without 后面接动名词 -ing。✓ without being conscious of it. ✗ be / to be / been:介词后用动名词 being。

### s5 seq2 | ans: using
介词 instead of 后面接动名词 -ing。✓ Instead of using the car… ✗ use / to use / used:介词后用 -ing。

### s5 seq3 | ans: collecting
介词 in 后面接动名词 -ing;be interested in doing。✓ interested in collecting paintings. ✗ collect / to collect / collected:介词后用 -ing。

### s5 seq4 | ans: Preferring
用现在分词短语作状语,表原因“因为偏爱…”。✓ Preferring always to go on foot, he… ✗ Prefer / Preferred to / He preferred:此处用分词 Preferring。

### s5 seq5 | ans: Recognizing
用现在分词短语作状语,表时间“认出…后”。✓ Recognizing who the customer was, the manager… ✗ Recognize / He recognized / Recognized by:用分词 Recognizing。

### s5 seq6 | ans: After being
表“被…之后”,用 After being + 过去分词(分词的完成被动)。✓ After being caught in a shower, he… ✗ After be / After been / For being:固定用 After being + 过去分词。

### s5 seq7 | ans: attention

### s5 seq8 | ans: care

### s5 seq10 | ans: who the customer was
间接疑问句用陈述句的正常语序(不倒装)。✓ knew who the customer was. ✗ who was the customer(倒装了)/ who the customer is he / was who the customer:间接疑问不倒装。

### s5 seq11 | ans: why he did it
间接疑问句不倒装、不加 do。✓ don't know why he did it. ✗ why did he do it(倒装+do)/ why he did do it / did why he do it:间接疑问用正常语序。

### s5 seq12 | ans: if the watch was expensive
间接的一般疑问用 if 引导,后面用陈述句语序。✓ asked if the watch was expensive. ✗ was the watch expensive(倒装)/ if was the watch expensive / the watch if expensive:if 后不倒装。

### s5 seq13 | ans: the clerk's
insist on 后接动名词,做这动作的人用物主代词(名词加's)。✓ insisted on the clerk's counting. ✗ the clerk to / to the clerk / that the clerk:用 the clerk's + 动名词。

### s5 seq14 | ans: my
