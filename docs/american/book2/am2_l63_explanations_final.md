### s5 seq1 | ans: asked
请求别人做某事,转间接引语用 ask sb to do。✓ She asked me to open the door for her. ✗ said:say 后不接 sb + 不定式;✗ suggested:suggest 后用 that…;✗ insisted:insist 后用 that…。

### s5 seq2 | ans: told
"告诉某人不要做某事"转间接用 tell sb not to do。✓ She told them not to make so much noise. ✗ said:say 后不接 sb + 不定式;✗ spoke:speak 不这样搭配;✗ talked:talk 不这样搭配。

### s5 seq3 | ans: to go
祈使句请求转间接用 ask sb to do;说话人视角从 come 变为 go。✓ He asked me to go with him. ✗ to come:视角未转换;✗ going:ask 后用不定式;✗ go:缺 to。

### s5 seq4 | ans: to wait
请求转间接用 ask sb to do;here 相应变 there。✓ He asked me to wait there. ✗ wait:缺 to;✗ waiting:ask 后用不定式;✗ waited:形式不对。

### s5 seq5 | ans: wanted
陈述句转间接,主句为过去(told),从句时态相应后移,want→wanted。✓ Jenny told him she wanted to go home. ✗ wants:未后移;✗ want:未后移;✗ has wanted:与过去不一致。

### s5 seq6 | ans: didn't
陈述句转间接,时态后移,don't→didn't。✓ She said she didn't like it. ✗ doesn't:未后移;✗ hadn't:like 是一般动作,不用完成;✗ won't:表将来。

### s5 seq7 | ans: had
陈述句转间接,时态后移,have finished→had finished。✓ He said he had finished. ✗ has:未后移;✗ have:未后移;✗ was:构不成完成。

### s5 seq8 | ans: should
insist 后的从句用 that … should do。✓ He insisted that I should stay to lunch. ✗ will:不与 insist 从句搭配;✗ must have / would have:意思和形式都不对。

### s5 seq9 | ans: should
suggest 后的从句用 that … should do。✓ He suggested that I should go with him. ✗ will / am / would:不与 suggest 从句搭配。

### s5 seq10 | ans: if
一般疑问句(可用 yes/no 回答)转间接,用 if 或 whether 引导。✓ He asked her if she had enjoyed the speech. ✗ that:that 引导陈述句转述;✗ did:间接疑问不用 do/did;✗ what:此处不是特殊疑问。

### s5 seq11 | ans: if
间接一般疑问用 if/whether 引导。✓ I wonder if it will rain tomorrow. ✗ that:引导陈述转述;✗ will:不引导从句;✗ does:间接疑问不用 do/does。

### s5 seq12 | ans: can
间接疑问一律用陈述语序(he can),不倒装、不加 do/does。✓ I wonder if he can wait a few minutes. ✗ can he:疑问语序;✗ could he:疑问语序;✗ does:多余。

### s5 seq13 | ans: hadn't
特殊疑问转间接,保留疑问词并用陈述语序,时态后移 didn't enjoy→hadn't enjoyed。✓ He asked her why she hadn't enjoyed it. ✗ didn't:未后移;✗ doesn't:未后移;✗ hasn't:与过去不一致。

### s5 seq14 | ans: will arrive
间接特殊疑问用陈述语序(he will arrive)。✓ I wonder when he will arrive. ✗ will he arrive:疑问语序;✗ does arrive:多余的 do;✗ arrive will:词序错。

### s5 seq15 | ans: lives
间接疑问用陈述语序(he lives),不倒装、不加 do/does。✓ I wonder where he lives. ✗ does he live:疑问语序;✗ does live:多余的 do;✗ live he:词序错。

### s5 seq16 | ans: me
转间接引语时人称相应变化:原话里的 you(指听话人 me)变成 me。✓ He said he had seen me the day before. ✗ you:未随人称变化;✗ him / us:指代错。

### s5 seq17 | ans: then
转间接引语时时间词相应变化:now→then。✓ She said she would do it then. ✗ now:未变化;✗ today / soon:与原意不符。

### s5 seq18 | ans: there
转间接引语时地点/视角相应变化:here→there,come→go。✓ He asked me to go there. ✗ here:未变化;✗ home / back:与原意不符。
