## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**:Listen and answer — Why was the writer unhappy at the theater?
（选项:a) The play was boring　b) A couple behind kept talking ✓　c) His seat was bad　d) The actors were too quiet）

**美语版课文**（原创叙事,11 句,现代美语）

> Last Friday I went to the theater downtown. I had a great seat, right in the center. The play was a comedy, and it was really funny. But I didn't enjoy it at all. A young couple was sitting behind me, and they were talking loudly the whole time. I got angry because I couldn't hear the actors. I turned around and looked at them, but they didn't pay any attention. In the end I couldn't bear it. I turned around again. "I can't hear a word!" I said angrily. "It's none of your business," the young man said rudely. "This is a private conversation!"

## 关5 · 语法专项(G1–G11,共 22 题;**认知题仅 3 道 [Q4/Q8/Q11]**,其余用法题;题前置折叠卡)

> 📖 **关5 前置「语法小知识」折叠卡「句子的骨架」**:用本课句子现场拆解 主语/谓语/系动词/表语/宾语/双宾/宾补(存 `american_lessons.grammar_card`,`GrammarTipsView` 渲染)。**教了再考**(规范见 specs/terminology_spec.md)。

### G1 句子成分与语序(3 题·用法)
1. 选出语序正确的句子:
   a) I went yesterday to the theater.　b) Yesterday I went to the theater. ✓　c) I yesterday went to the theater.　d) To the theater I went yesterday.
2. 时间状语可放句首或句尾。下列哪句**也**正确(与"Every morning she reads the news"同义)?
   → She reads the news every morning. ✓ / She every morning reads the news. / Reads she the news every morning. / She reads every morning the news.
3. 排序:the actors / couldn't / I / hear → **I couldn't hear the actors.** ✓(主+谓+宾)

### G2 动词分类(1 题·**认知①**)
4. "She **can** swim well." 句中 **can** 是:a) 实义动词　b) 助动词　c) 情态动词 ✓　d) 系动词

### G3 系动词与表语(2 题·用法)
6. The pizza smells ___.　a) good ✓　b) well　c) nicely　d) badly(副词不作表语)
7. He looked ___ when he heard the news.　a) angrily　b) angry ✓　c) anger　d) angers(look 后接形容词作表语)

### G4 五种基本句型 + 及物/不及物(3 题)
8. [**认知②**] "The play was really funny." 属于哪种句型?　a) 主谓　b) 主系表 ✓　c) 主谓宾　d) 主谓双宾
9. [用法·句子完整性] 下列哪句**不完整**,动词后面还缺"什么"(缺宾语)?
   a) He arrived yesterday.　b) I really enjoyed. ✓　c) They were talking loudly.　d) She smiled.
10. [用法·去术语] 哪句的 play 后面**不接任何东西**,意思也完整?
    a) They play soccer.　b) The kids play in the park. ✓　c) She plays the piano.　d) We played a game.

### G5 双宾语 vs 复合宾语(2 题)
5. [用法·双宾改介词] "She gave me a great seat." 换一种说法:She gave a great seat ___ me.
   a) to ✓　b) for　c) at　d) with
11. [**认知③**] 哪句是**复合宾语**?(判别法:宾语后加 be 能成句 → him **is** captain ✓)
    a) They made him captain. ✓　b) She gave him a ticket.　c) I told her the truth.　d) He bought me coffee.

### G6 陈述句的否定(2 题)
12. 把 "He likes the play." 变否定:→ He **doesn't** like the play. ✓ / He don't like… / He not likes… / He isn't like…
13. 下列哪个否定式**没有**缩写形式?　a) is not→isn't　b) am not ✓　c) do not→don't　d) cannot→can't

### G7 一般疑问句(2 题)
14. 把 "You saw the play." 变一般疑问句:→ **Did you see the play?** ✓ / Do you saw…? / Saw you…? / Are you see…?
15. "Is this your seat?" 的正确肯定回答:→ **Yes, it is.** ✓ / Yes, this is. / Yes, it's. / Yes, is it.

### G8 特殊疑问(不接名词,2 题)
16. "___ do you go to the theater?"(答:By subway.)→ **How** ✓ / Where / When / Who
17. 对 "The couple sat **behind me**" 划线部分提问:→ **Where** did the couple sit? ✓ / When / How / Why

### G9 特殊疑问(接名词 which/whose,1 题)
18. "___ seat is this? It's mine."　a) Who　b) Whose ✓　c) Which　d) What

### G10 how 用法与频率(2 题)
19. "___ do you go to the theater?"(答:Three times a year.)→ **How often** ✓ / How far / How long / How much
20. "How ___ is the theater from here?"(答:About two miles.)→ **far** ✓ / tall / old / many

### G11 课文语言点(2 题,宾格 / pay no attention=not any)
21. I looked at ___ angrily.(指那对情侣)　a) they　b) them ✓　c) their　d) theirs
22. "pay **no** attention" = "**not** pay ___ attention"　a) some　b) any ✓　c) much　d) a

3. 语块 get angry 的意思 → **生气** ✓ / 高兴 / 害怕 / 惊讶
4. "in the end" 的意思 → **最后,终于** ✓ / 在结尾的位置 / 立刻,马上 / 从头开始

## 关9 · 情景应答(3 题)

1. 剧院里有人身后大声聊天,你想让他们安静,礼貌的说法 →
   a) It's none of your business!　b) Excuse me, could you please keep it down? ✓　c) Shut up!　d) I can't hear a word!
2. 别人问起你不想透露的私事,你想婉拒 →
   a) I'd rather not say. ✓　b) Pay attention!　c) Have a good one.　d) Turn around.
3. 你没听清对方的话,想请他再说 →
   a) It's none of your business.　b) Sorry, I couldn't hear you — could you say that again? ✓　c) I got angry.　d) This is a private conversation.
