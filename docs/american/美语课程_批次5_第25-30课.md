# Lesson 25  Let's order pizza!
## 咱们点披萨吧！

> 语法目标：like / love / don't like（好恶程度） · not ... at all · Let's + 动词原形 · What do you like?
> 场景：室友商量披萨配料

## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: Emily says "I don't like ___ at all."
（a) Olives　b) Mushrooms ✓　c) Pepperoni）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | JOSH: Let's order pizza! What do you like? | 乔什：咱们点披萨吧！你喜欢什么口味？ |
| 2 | EMILY: I love pepperoni. Do you like it? | 艾米丽：我超爱意式辣香肠。你喜欢吗？ |
| 3 | JOSH: Yes, I do. But I really love mushrooms. | 乔什：喜欢。不过我最爱的是蘑菇。 |
| 4 | EMILY: Ugh, mushrooms? I don't like them at all! | 艾米丽：呃，蘑菇？我一点都不喜欢！ |
| 5 | JOSH: Ha! Okay. What about olives? | 乔什：哈哈！好吧。橄榄呢？ |
| 6 | EMILY: I like olives. And I love extra cheese! | 艾米丽：橄榄可以。而且我爱加倍芝士！ |
| 7 | JOSH: Me too. So—pepperoni, olives, and extra cheese. | 乔什：我也是。那就——辣香肠、橄榄、加倍芝士。 |
| 8 | EMILY: Perfect. Oh wait—do you like thin crust? | 艾米丽：完美。等等——你喜欢薄底吗？ |
| 9 | JOSH: Yes! Thin crust is the way to go. | 乔什：喜欢！薄底才是正解。 |
| 10 | EMILY: Great. I'm calling Tony's now. | 艾米丽：好。我这就给托尼披萨店打电话。 |
| 11 | JOSH: Tell them: no pineapple! | 乔什：跟他们说：不要菠萝！ |

## 关2–4 · 核心词汇

| 词汇 | 美式音标 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 / 搭配 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| order | /ˈɔrdər/ | v. | 点（餐）；订购 | order pizza |
| pizza | /ˈpitsə/ | n. | 披萨 | Let's order pizza! |
| love | /lʌv/ | v. | 热爱；超爱 | I love pepperoni. |
| pepperoni | /ˌpɛpəˈroʊni/ | n. | 意式辣香肠 | pepperoni pizza |
| mushroom | /ˈmʌʃrum/ | n. | 蘑菇 | I love mushrooms. |
| at all | /æt ɔl/ | 短语 | 一点也（不） | not ... at all |
| olive | /ˈɑlɪv/ | n. | 橄榄 | I like olives. |
| extra | /ˈɛkstrə/ | adj. | 额外的；加倍的 | extra cheese |
| thin crust | /θɪn krʌst/ | n. | 薄底（披萨） | thin crust pizza |
| the way to go | — | 短语 | 正解；就该这样 | ... is the way to go. |

| pineapple | /ˈpaɪnˌæpəl/ | n. | 菠萝 | No pineapple! |

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① 好恶程度阶梯**

| 程度 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| like 喜欢 | I **like** olives. |
| really love / love 超爱 | I **love** pepperoni. |
| don't like 不喜欢 | I **don't like** mushrooms. |
| don't like ... at all 一点都不喜欢 | I don't like them **at all**! |

**② What do you like? 特殊疑问**：— What do you like? — I like olives.

**③ Let's + 动词原形（提议"咱们……吧"）**：**Let's order** pizza! / **Let's watch** a movie.

**④ 宾格复现**：Do you like **it**? / I don't like **them**.

## 关6 · 美语点睛

| 美语 🇺🇸 | 英语 🇬🇧 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 菠萝披萨之争 | —（网络文化梗） | 美国人对"披萨放不放菠萝"争论不休 |
| thin crust | 同（另有 deep dish 厚底） | 披萨薄底 |
| ... is the way to go. | 同（口语） | ……才是正解 |
| Ugh | Eww | 呃（嫌弃拟声） |

|---|---|---|

| 2 | a) loving　b) loves　c) love　d) am love | c |

## 关8 · 听对话理解题

1. Emily says "I love ___." — a) Mushrooms　b) Pepperoni ✓　c) Pineapple　d) Onions
2. Emily doesn't like ___ at all. — a) Olives　b) Cheese　c) Mushrooms ✓　d) Thin crust
3. What do they NOT want on the pizza? — a) Olives　b) Extra cheese　c) Pepperoni　d) Pineapple ✓

## 关9 · 情景应答

1. 提议大家一起点披萨 → Let's order pizza!
2. 说你一点都不喜欢某种食物 → I don't like it at all!
3. 附和对方的选择"这才对嘛" → That's the way to go!

## 关10 · 本课通关

A. 选择填空
1. I ___ pepperoni pizza.　a) love　b) loves　c) loving　d) am love → **a**
2. ___ order tacos tonight!　a) Let is　b) Lets be　c) Let's　d) Doing → **c**
3. I don't like olives ___ all.　a) in　b) at　c) on　d) for → **b**
4. What ___ you like?　a) are　b) is　c) can　d) do → **d**

B. 句型转换
1. You like mushrooms.（变否定句）→ You don't like mushrooms.
2. We order pizza.（变 Let's 提议）→ Let's order pizza.
3. I like olives.（程度加强为"超爱"）→ I love olives.

# Lesson 26  I usually get up at six
## 我通常六点起床

> 语法目标：频度副词 always / usually / sometimes / never 及位置 · every + 时间 · 一般现在时表习惯
> 场景：同事聊晨间习惯

## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: Ken usually gets up at ___.
（a) Five　b) Six ✓　c) Seven）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | LAUREN: Ken, you're always early! What's your secret? | 劳伦：肯，你总是来这么早！有什么秘诀？ |
| 2 | KEN: Ha! I usually get up at six. | 肯：哈哈！我通常六点起床。 |
| 3 | LAUREN: Six?! I never get up at six. | 劳伦：六点？！我从来没六点起过。 |
| 4 | KEN: I run every morning. Then I make coffee. | 肯：我每天早晨跑步，然后煮咖啡。 |
| 5 | LAUREN: Wow. I usually check my phone in bed... for an hour. | 劳伦：哇。我通常躺在床上刷手机……刷一个小时。 |
| 6 | KEN: Ha! Do you eat breakfast? | 肯：哈哈！你吃早饭吗？ |
| 7 | LAUREN: Sometimes. I usually just grab a coffee. | 劳伦：偶尔吃。通常就是随手买杯咖啡。 |
| 8 | KEN: I always eat breakfast. Eggs and toast. | 肯：我每天都吃早饭。鸡蛋加烤面包片。 |
| 9 | LAUREN: Okay, okay. New plan: I'm going to get up early! | 劳伦：行行行。新计划：我要早起！ |
| 10 | KEN: Ha! You always say that. | 肯：哈！你每次都这么说。 |
| 11 | LAUREN: ...Fair. | 劳伦：……有道理。 |

## 关2–4 · 核心词汇

| 词汇 | 美式音标 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 / 搭配 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| always | /ˈɔlweɪz/ | adv. | 总是 | You're always early. |
| usually | /ˈjuʒuəli/ | adv. | 通常 | I usually get up at six. |
| never | /ˈnɛvər/ | adv. | 从不 | I never get up at six. |
| sometimes | /ˈsʌmˌtaɪmz/ | adv. | 有时 | Sometimes. |
| early | /ˈɜrli/ | adj./adv. | 早的；早 | get up early |
| secret | /ˈsikrɪt/ | n. | 秘诀 | What's your secret? |
| get up | /ɡɛt ʌp/ | 短语 | 起床 | get up at six |
| every morning | /ˈɛvri ˈmɔrnɪŋ/ | 短语 | 每天早晨 | I run every morning. |
| breakfast | /ˈbrɛkfəst/ | n. | 早餐 | eat breakfast |
| grab | /ɡræb/ | v. | 随手买/拿 | grab a coffee |
| toast | /toʊst/ | n. | 烤面包片 | eggs and toast |
| Fair. | /fɛr/ | int. | 有道理；我认 | ...Fair. |

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① 频度副词阶梯**

| 频度 | 副词 |
|---|---|
| 100% | **always** 总是 |
| 大多数 | **usually** 通常 |
| 有时 | **sometimes** 有时 |
| 0% | **never** 从不 |

**② 频度副词的位置**

| 规则 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| be 动词**后** | You're **always** early. |
| 实义动词**前** | I **usually** get up at six. / I **never** drink soda. |

**③ every + 时间**：every morning / every day / every Friday。

**④ 一般现在时表习惯**：说的是"平常一贯如此"，不是此刻。

## 关6 · 美语点睛

| 美语 🇺🇸 | 英语 🇬🇧 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| grab a coffee | get a coffee | 随手买杯咖啡 |
| toast | 同（美式早餐标配） | 烤面包片 |
| Fair. | Fair enough. | 有道理，我认 |
| You always say that. | 同（调侃） | 你每次都这么说 |

## 关7 · 对话填空

LAUREN: You're ___1___ early!
KEN: I ___2___ get up at six.
LAUREN: I ___3___ get up at six.
KEN: I run ___4___ morning.
LAUREN: I usually just ___5___ a coffee.

| 空 | 选项 | 答案 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a) always　b) never　c) every　d) all | a |
| 2 | a) never　b) every　c) usually　d) very | c |
| 3 | a) always　b) never　c) usually　d) every | b |
| 4 | a) all　b) very　c) most　d) every | d |
| 5 | a) grab　b) grabs　c) grabbing　d) grabbed | a |

## 关8 · 听对话理解题

1. Ken usually gets up at ___. — a) Five　b) Six ✓　c) Seven　d) Eight
2. Ken says "I ___ every morning." — a) Run ✓　b) Swim　c) Cook　d) Read
3. Lauren usually checks her phone ___. — a) At work　b) In the car　c) In bed ✓　d) At breakfast

## 关9 · 情景应答

1. 说你通常七点起床 → I usually get up at seven.
2. 说你从不喝汽水 → I never drink soda.
3. 别人说得在理，你认了 → Fair.

## 关10 · 本课通关

A. 选择填空
1. I ___ eat breakfast.（总是）　a) always　b) never　c) very　d) most → **a**
2. She's ___ late.（从不）　a) no　b) don't　c) never　d) not ever be → **c**
3. I run ___ morning.　a) all　b) every　c) very　d) most → **b**
4. 频度副词在实义动词的：　a) 后面　b) 句尾　c) 句首必须　d) 前面 → **d**

B. 句型转换
1. I get up at six.（加"通常"）→ I usually get up at six.
2. You are early.（加"总是"）→ You are always early.
3. I drink soda.（改为"从不"）→ I never drink soda.

# Lesson 27  Does he cook?
## 他会做饭吗？

> 语法目标：第三人称单数动词 -s / -es · have → has · Does he/she...? — Yes, he does. · doesn't + 动词原形
> 场景：聊新室友

## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: Marco works at ___.
（a) A coffee shop　b) An animal hospital ✓　c) A middle school）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | BELLA: So, Zach—what's your new roommate like? | 贝拉：说说，扎克——你的新室友怎么样？ |
| 2 | ZACH: Marco? He's great. He works at an animal hospital. | 扎克：马可？人特好。他在一家动物医院工作。 |
| 3 | BELLA: Cool! Does he like animals? | 贝拉：酷！他喜欢动物吗？ |
| 4 | ZACH: He loves them! He has two cats. | 扎克：爱得不行！他养了两只猫。 |
| 5 | BELLA: Aww. Does he cook? | 贝拉：可爱。他做饭吗？ |
| 6 | ZACH: Yes, he does. He makes amazing pasta every Friday. | 扎克：做的。他每周五做超好吃的意面。 |
| 7 | BELLA: Lucky you! Does he play music at night? | 贝拉：你可真幸运！他晚上放音乐吗？ |
| 8 | ZACH: No, he doesn't. He goes to bed at ten. | 扎克：不放。他十点就睡了。 |
| 9 | BELLA: Wow. He sounds perfect. | 贝拉：哇，听起来完美啊。 |
| 10 | ZACH: Almost. He never washes the dishes! | 扎克：差一点。他从来不洗碗！ |
| 11 | BELLA: Ha! Nobody's perfect. | 贝拉：哈哈！人无完人。 |

## 关2–4 · 核心词汇

| 词汇 | 美式音标 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 / 搭配 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| roommate | /ˈrumˌmeɪt/ | n. | 室友 | my new roommate |
| animal hospital | /ˈænəməl ˈhɑspɪtl/ | n. | 动物医院 | works at an animal hospital |
| has | /hæz/ | v. | 有（have 的三单） | He has two cats. |
| cat | /kæt/ | n. | 猫 | two cats |
| cook | /kʊk/ | v. | 做饭 | Does he cook? |
| pasta | /ˈpɑstə/ | n. | 意面 | amazing pasta |
| Friday | /ˈfraɪˌdeɪ/ | n. | 星期五 | every Friday |
| go to bed | /ɡoʊ tu bɛd/ | 短语 | 上床睡觉 | goes to bed at ten |
| sound | /saʊnd/ | v. | 听起来 | He sounds perfect. |
| wash the dishes | /wɑʃ ðə ˈdɪʃɪz/ | 短语 | 洗碗 | never washes the dishes |
| Lucky you! | — | 短语 | 你真幸运 | Lucky you! |
| Nobody's perfect. | — | 短语 | 人无完人 | Nobody's perfect. |

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① 第三人称单数动词加 -s**

| 规则 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| 一般加 **-s** | work → work**s**, make → make**s**, love → love**s** |
| s/x/ch/sh/o 结尾加 **-es** | go → go**es**, wash → wash**es** |
| 特殊 | have → **has** |

**② Does he/she...? 问答**：**Does he** cook? — Yes, he **does**. / No, he **doesn't**.

**③ 否定：doesn't + 动词原形**：He **doesn't play** music.（⚠️ 不说 ~~doesn't plays~~）

**④ never + 三单**：He never wash**es** the dishes.（never 不影响 -s）

## 关6 · 美语点睛

| 美语 🇺🇸 | 英语 🇬🇧 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| roommate | flatmate | 室友（美国大学文化） |
| Lucky you! | 同（口语） | 你真幸运 |
| Nobody's perfect. | 同（俗语） | 人无完人 |
| Aww | 同（拟声） | 可爱（心都化了） |

## 关8 · 听对话理解题

## 关9 · 情景应答

2. 问对方的室友做不做饭 → Does he cook? / Does she cook?

## 关10 · 本课通关

A. 选择填空

2. He ___ two dogs.　a) have　b) haves　c) is have　d) has → **d**
3. ___ he like animals? — Yes, he does.　a) Do　b) Does　c) Is　d) Can → **b**
4. She ___ play music at night.　a) don't　b) isn't　c) doesn't　d) not → **c**

B. 句型转换
1. I work at a hospital.（主语改 He）→ He works at a hospital.
2. He cooks pasta.（变一般疑问句）→ Does he cook pasta?
3. She washes the dishes.（变否定句）→ She doesn't wash the dishes.
