# AM2 L3 逐题解释(定稿) · 供 gen-book2-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: visited
规则动词过去式:visit 直接加 -ed → visited。✓ I visited three museums yesterday. yesterday 提示这是过去发生的事。

### s5 seq2 | ans: cried
cry 以"辅音字母+y"结尾,变过去式把 y 改成 i 再加 -ed → cried。✓ She cried when she heard the news.

### s5 seq3 | ans: went
go 是不规则动词,过去式是 went(不加 -ed)。✓ Last summer I went to Italy.(L1、L2 课文里也见过 went)。

### s5 seq4 | ans: lent
lend(借出)是不规则动词,过去式 lent。✓ He lent me a book. 别写成 lended。

### s5 seq5 | ans: bought
buy 是不规则动词,过去式 bought。✓ I bought thirty-seven cards. 别写成 buyed。

### s5 seq6 | ans: didn't understand
过去时否定用 didn't + 动词原形,understand 要还原成原形。✓ I didn't understand a word. 不是 didn't understood。

### s5 seq7 | ans: 动词原形
过去时否定 didn't 本身已经表示过去,后面的动词一律用原形,不再变过去式。✓ didn't send / didn't write。

### s5 seq8 | ans: Did you see the play?
过去时一般疑问:Did + 主语 + 动词原形。see 要还原原形。✓ Did you see the play? 不是 Did you saw。

### s5 seq9 | ans: Yes, I did.
Did…? 的肯定回答用 Yes, 主语 + did。✓ Yes, I did.

### s5 seq10 | ans: How many
问"多少张卡"(卡可数),用 How many + 可数名词复数。✓ How many cards did he buy?

### s5 seq11 | ans: Where did he stay?
对地点提问:Where + did + 主语 + 动词原形。✓ Where did he stay? 不是 Where did he stayed。

### s5 seq12 | ans: Last summer
"去年夏天"直接说 Last summer,前面不加介词。✓ Last summer I went to Italy. 不能说 in last summer。

### s5 seq13 | ans: Last summer I went to Italy.
"已经发生过、结束了"的事用一般过去时。只有 Last summer I went to Italy 是过去时(went);其它几句是一般现在时(说习惯/事实)或现在进行时。

### s5 seq14 | ans: was / were
be 动词过去式:单数主语(the weather)用 was,复数主语(the people)用 were。✓ The weather was warm, and the people were friendly.

### s5 seq15 | ans: Were
回答是 No, I wasn't,主语 you 对应 were;疑问句把 Were 提到主语前。✓ Were you afraid?

### s5 seq16 | ans: He lent me a book.
lend = 借出(把自己的东西给别人),过去式 lent。✓ He lent me a book. borrow 是"借入",方向正相反。

### s5 seq17 | ans: I borrowed a book from the library.
borrow = 借入(从别处借来),常用 borrow sth from sb。✓ I borrowed a book from the library.

### s5 seq18 | ans: spent
spend 是不规则动词,过去式 spent。✓ I spent the whole day in my room.

### s6 seq1 | ans: 借出(把东西借给别人)
lend = 借出:把自己的东西借给别人。✓ He lent me a book. 和 borrow(借入)方向相反。
顺带记:借入(从别人处借) = borrow;购买 = buy;出售 = sell.

### s6 seq2 | ans: 做决定
make a decision = 做决定、下决心。✓ I made a big decision.
顺带记:犯错误 = make a mistake;改主意 = change one's mind;提问题 = ask a question.

### s6 seq3 | ans: 几个(修饰可数名词)
a few = 几个,修饰可数名词复数。✓ a few words / a few cards. 修饰不可数名词要用 a little。
顺带记:很多 = many / a lot of;一点点(修饰不可数) = a little;没有 = no / none.

### s6 seq4 | ans: mail
美语"寄(信/卡)"用 mail;英式用 post。✓ I'll mail the card tomorrow.

### s6 seq5 | ans: vacation
美语"假期"说 vacation;英式说 holiday。✓ We had a great vacation.

### s6 seq6 | ans: spoiled
spoil 的过去式,美式规则拼写 spoiled;英式 spoilt。✓ The rain spoiled our trip.

### s7 seq1 | ans: went
"去年夏天去意大利"用一般过去时,go→went。✓ Last summer I went to Italy.
✗ go:Last summer 是过去,用过去式 went;✗ goes:过去的事不用一般现在,主语 I 也不用 goes;✗ going:缺助动词,单独不能作谓语.

### s7 seq2 | ans: taught
teach 是不规则动词,过去式 taught。✓ A waiter taught me some Italian.
✗ teached:teach 是不规则动词,过去式是 taught 不是 teached;✗ teaches:过去的事不用一般现在;✗ teaching:缺助动词,单独不能作谓语.

### s7 seq3 | ans: thought
think 是不规则动词,过去式 thought。✓ Every day I thought about postcards.
✗ think:过去的事用过去式 thought;✗ thinks:过去的事不用一般现在,主语 I 也不用 thinks;✗ thinking:缺助动词.

### s7 seq4 | ans: didn't
"却一张也没寄"用过去否定 didn't + 动词原形 send。✓ but I didn't send any.
✗ don't:过去的否定用 didn't,不用 don't;✗ wasn't:send 是实义动词,否定借 didn't,不用 wasn't;✗ weren't:主语 I 不配 weren't,实义动词否定也该用 didn't.

### s9 seq1 | ans: I went to the movies.
说昨晚做过、已结束的事,用一般过去时,go→went。✓ I went to the movies. 场合:回答别人问你昨晚干了什么。

### s9 seq2 | ans: Where did you go yesterday?
问别人昨天去了哪儿,用 Where did you go yesterday?——问过去的事,前面加 did,go 保持原形。✓ Where did you go yesterday? 场合:问对方昨天的行踪。

### s9 seq3 | ans: Thank you for lending me this book.
感谢对方"借给我"(借出=lend),这里用动名词 lending。✓ Thank you for lending me this book. 用 borrow 就说反方向了。

### s10 seq1 | ans: museum
"博物馆"对应 museum。✓ I visited a lot of museums.

### s10 seq2 | ans: 唯一的,一张也(没写)

### s10 seq4 | ans: got
