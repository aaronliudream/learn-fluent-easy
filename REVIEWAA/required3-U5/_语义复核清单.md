### u5.01 表推测可能性(must / can't / may / might / could)
- "Henry has his own boat and once worked for a mining company, so he ____ be completely poor — yet he is."
  **can't✓** / must / should / would
  考点:表推测可能性(must / can't / may / might / could) ｜ 解析:前提给出他有船有工作,推断"不可能完全是穷光蛋"——否定推测用 can't。
- "The owner stared at the note. 'This ____ be the million-pound note I read about in the papers last week!'"
  can't / **must✓** / need / ought
  考点:表推测可能性(must / can't / may / might / could) ｜ 解析:有报纸为证、惊讶认出,强肯定推测"一定是"——must。
- "Nobody answered the door, so the brothers ____ be out for the afternoon."
  can't / would / **might✓** / had better
  考点:表推测可能性(must / can't / may / might / could) ｜ 解析:只凭没人应门,证据不足的可能推测"也许出门了"——might。
- "He is wearing a worn coat and has no small change, so he ____ be a very rich man," thought the clerk.
  must / should / need / **can't✓**
  考点:表推测可能性(must / can't / may / might / could) ｜ 解析:破外套、没零钱,推断"不可能是富翁"——否定推测 can't。

  考点:表推测可能性(must / can't / may / might / could) ｜ 解析:灯还亮着是有力证据,"一定还有人在"——must。

  考点:表推测可能性(must / can't / may / might / could) ｜ 解析:"不确定"表明证据不足,弱推测"也许回酒店了"——may。
