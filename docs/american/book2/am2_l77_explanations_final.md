### s5 seq1 | ans: was built
过去某年建成、且是“被建造”,用一般过去的被动 was + 过去分词。✓ The bridge was built in 1850. ✗ has been built:现在完成,不配 in 1850 这种明确过去时间;✗ is built:一般现在;✗ was building:进行,意思成了“正在建”。

### s5 seq2 | ans: appeared
过去某时“出现了”,用一般过去时。✓ Recently, cracks appeared in the old stone. ✗ have appeared:现在完成;✗ were appearing:进行;✗ appear:一般现在。

### s5 seq3 | ans: discovered
过去“发现了”,用一般过去时。✓ The experts discovered that the bridge was partly wooden. ✗ have discovered:现在完成;✗ were discovering:进行;✗ discover:一般现在。

### s5 seq4 | ans: has stood
从1850年一直矗立到现在,用现在完成时。✓ The bridge has stood over the river since 1850. ✗ stood:一般过去,不含“延续到现在”;✗ is standing:进行,表此刻;✗ had stood:过去完成,时间点不对。

### s5 seq5 | ans: have crossed
这些年来无数人走过、经历延续至今,用现在完成时。✓ Thousands of people have crossed it. ✗ crossed:一般过去;✗ are crossing:进行;✗ had crossed:过去完成。

### s5 seq6 | ans: has remained
设计一直保持不变到现在,用现在完成时。✓ The design has remained the same for centuries. ✗ remained:一般过去;✗ is remaining:进行;✗ had remained:过去完成。

### s5 seq7 | ans: have been examining
从过去一直考察到现在、强调持续过程,用现在完成进行时。✓ Experts have been examining the bridge for six months. ✗ examined:一般过去;✗ are examining:现在进行,不含“已持续六个月”;✗ have examined:现在完成,不强调过程。

### s5 seq8 | ans: have been working
从去年春天一直研究到现在,用现在完成进行时。✓ We have been working on this since last spring. ✗ worked:一般过去;✗ are working:现在进行;✗ have worked:现在完成,不强调持续过程。

### s5 seq9 | ans: has been looking for
最近一直在找、强调持续,用现在完成进行时。✓ The city has been looking for ways to protect the bridge. ✗ looked for:一般过去;✗ is looking for:现在进行;✗ has looked for:现在完成,不强调过程。

### s5 seq10 | ans: for
接“一段时间(six months)”用 for。✓ Experts have examined the bridge for six months. ✗ since:接时间点;✗ ago:用在“时段+ago”结构末尾;✗ at:接钟点。

### s5 seq11 | ans: since

### s5 seq13 | ans: ago
