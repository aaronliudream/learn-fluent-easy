### u2.01 主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语)
- The meaning of the word ____ greatly with the context.
  **changes✓** / change / changing / to change
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:主谓结构,主语 meaning 第三人称单数,谓语用 changes。
- At first the foreigner ____ fluent Spanish but still felt confused.
  talked / **spoke✓** / said / told
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:及物动词+宾语 Spanish,speak a language 才地道,talk/say/tell 搭配不对。
- After waiting too long, she finally ____ for the nearest bus.
  rushed quickly / did a run / **made a rush✓** / went a hurry
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:make a rush for 是地道搭配,构成主谓宾;其余搭配不成立。
- The strange experience slowly ____ from my memory.
  removed / deleted / erased / **faded✓**
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:fade 为不及物动词,fade from memory(逐渐消逝),构成主谓;其余多为及物且搭配不当。
- I ____ to the seller that I could not wait any longer.
  **signalled✓** / spoke / told / talked
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:signal to sb 示意,构成主谓;told 需直接宾语 sb,spoke/talked 搭配不当。
- She ____ a portion of French fries for us to share.
  asked / **ordered✓** / wished / hoped
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:及物动词 order+宾语 a portion,构成主谓宾;其余搭配不对。
- When native speakers talk fast, I can ____ only a few words.
  listen / hear of / **catch✓** / look
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:catch+宾语 a few words(听清),主谓宾;listen 不及物需 to,look 也不及物。
- My attitude towards time ____ a lot since I moved abroad.
  has been changed / changes it / has changing / **has changed✓**
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:change 此处不及物表'变化',主谓结构,用现在完成 has changed;被动 has been changed 语义不通。
- The waiter ____ us a plate of chips, not French fries.
  **brought✓** / came / arrived / appeared
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:bring 及物可带宾语,构成主谓宾(后又接双宾);come/arrive/appear 不及物,不能直接带宾语。
- Gestures ____ from country to country in expressing ideas.
  different / **differ✓** / difference / differently
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:需要谓语动词,differ from(不同于)为不及物动词,主谓结构;其余为形容词/名词/副词。
- He laughed and ____ to the aubergines on the pizza.
  showed / watched / **pointed✓** / saw
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:point to/at 指向,不及物需介词,搭配地道;show/watch/see 为及物且语义不合。
- Part of the excitement of learning a language ____ discovering the differences.
  lies on / stays at / puts in / **lies in✓**
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:lie in(在于)是地道搭配,主谓;其余介词搭配错误。
- I couldn't ____ what "French fries" really were.
  **imagine✓** / think / guess of / dream
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:imagine+宾语从句,及物,主谓宾;think 后多需 about/of,guess of 不成立。
- People in some countries ____ their hands to show their feelings.
  do / **use✓** / make / play
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:use+宾语 their hands,及物,主谓宾;其余搭配不地道。
- When the visitor said this, the meaning ____ completely in Chinese culture.
  changed it / was changing it / **changed✓** / changed them
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:此处 change 不及物表'改变',主谓;加宾语 it/them 多余且无指代对象。
- The two kinds of English ____ in spelling, grammar and pronunciation.
  compare / match / equal / **differ✓**
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:differ in(在……方面不同),不及物,主谓;compare/match/equal 为及物且语义相反。
- A few short words often ____ enough between close friends.
  **are✓** / have / do / make
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:此处需系动词构成判断;但本题考主语+谓语成立,are 让主干完整;have/do/make 缺宾语或语义不通。
- She ____ at her wrist to show that time was running out.
  looked / **pointed✓** / watched / saw
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:point at 指向,主谓(带介词短语);look 需 at 但 look at one's wrist 语义不如 point 自然,且其余为及物。
- My dad ____ this pizza, and it was covered in aubergines.
  asked / requested for / **ordered✓** / wished
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:order+宾语 this pizza,及物,主谓宾;request 后不接 for,ask/wish 语义不合。
- The host smiled and ____, "Where? Where?"
  spoke / talked / told / **said✓**
  考点:主谓 SV(不及物动词)/ 主谓宾 SVO(及物动词+宾语) ｜ 解析:say+引语,及物,主谓宾;speak/talk 不及物不能直接接引语,tell 需 sb。

### u2.02 主系表 SVC(系动词后用形容词作表语)/ There be 存在句

  **confused✓** / confusedly / confuse / confusing him
  考点:主系表 SVC(系动词后用形容词作表语)/ There be 存在句 ｜ 解析:系动词 was 后用形容词作表语,confused(感到困惑的);副词 confusedly 不能作表语。

  考点:主系表 SVC(系动词后用形容词作表语)/ There be 存在句 ｜ 解析:系动词 wasn't 后接形容词 pleasant 作表语;副词/动词/名词都不行。

  strangely / stranger  / **strange✓** / strangeness
  考点:主系表 SVC(系动词后用形容词作表语)/ There be 存在句 ｜ 解析:sound 为系动词,后接形容词 strange 作表语;副词 strangely 不能作表语。

  考点:主系表 SVC(系动词后用形容词作表语)/ There be 存在句 ｜ 解析:There be 存在句表'有/存在'某段时间;It was 无法引出 a time when 的存在含义。

- The new word seemed ____ until I learned the culture behind it.

- In England, gravy is ____ and made from meat.

- ____ likely to be many differences even in the same language.

### u2.03 主谓双宾 SVOO(give sb sth)/ 主谓宾补 SVOC(make us happy / call it…)
- She gave ____ to show that she had to leave.
  **him a signal✓** / a signal him / to him a signal / him to a signal
  考点:主谓双宾 SVOO(give sb sth)/ 主谓宾补 SVOC(make us happy / call it…) ｜ 解析:give sb sth 双宾结构,间接宾语 him 在前、直接宾语 a signal 在后。
- The cultural difference made the answer ____ to the American.
  puzzle / **puzzling✓** / to puzzle / puzzlingly
  考点:主谓双宾 SVOO(give sb sth)/ 主谓宾补 SVOC(make us happy / call it…) ｜ 解析:make+宾语+宾补,形容词 puzzling 作宾语补足语;副词/动词原形不可。
- My friend asked me ____ I wanted to order French fries.
  that / what about / **whether✓** / for
  考点:主谓双宾 SVOO(give sb sth)/ 主谓宾补 SVOC(make us happy / call it…) ｜ 解析:ask sb whether... 双宾+宾语从句;that 不引导'是否',其余不合语法。
- Americans call ____ this purple vegetable on the menu.
  "eggplant" it / to it "eggplant" / for it "eggplant" / **it "eggplant"✓**
  考点:主谓双宾 SVOO(give sb sth)/ 主谓宾补 SVOC(make us happy / call it…) ｜ 解析:call sb/sth+名称,宾补结构,宾语 it 在前、补足语 "eggplant" 在后。
- The waiter brought ____ instead of the French fries I expected.
  **us a plate of chips✓** / a plate of chips to we / to us a plate of chips / us a plate of chips to
  考点:主谓双宾 SVOO(give sb sth)/ 主谓宾补 SVOC(make us happy / call it…) ｜ 解析:bring sb sth 双宾,间接宾语 us 在前、直接宾语 a plate of chips 在后。
- What we call "biscuits", Americans ____ "cookies".
  say / **call✓** / speak / tell
  考点:主谓双宾 SVOO(give sb sth)/ 主谓宾补 SVOC(make us happy / call it…) ｜ 解析:call sth+名称,宾补结构;say/speak/tell 不能接'宾语+名称'作宾补。
- The strange experience taught ____ about cultural differences.
  a lot me / to me a lot / **me a lot✓** / for me a lot
  考点:主谓双宾 SVOO(give sb sth)/ 主谓宾补 SVOC(make us happy / call it…) ｜ 解析:teach sb sth 双宾,间接宾语 me 在前、直接宾语 a lot 在后。
- Living in Mexico has made my idea of time much more ____.
  relax / to relax / relaxing it / **relaxed✓**
  考点:主谓双宾 SVOO(give sb sth)/ 主谓宾补 SVOC(make us happy / call it…) ｜ 解析:make+宾语 idea+宾补,形容词 relaxed 作宾语补足语;动词原形/不定式不可。
- A linguist once told ____ that "ahorita" could mean almost anything.
  **me a fact✓** / a fact me / to me a fact / for me a fact
  考点:主谓双宾 SVOO(give sb sth)/ 主谓宾补 SVOC(make us happy / call it…) ｜ 解析:tell sb sth 双宾,间接宾语 me 在前、直接宾语 a fact 在后。
