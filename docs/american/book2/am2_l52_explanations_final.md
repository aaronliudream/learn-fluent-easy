### s5 seq1 | ans: have
表示"刚刚做完、对现在有影响"用现在完成时 have/has + 过去分词;主语 We 用 have。✓ We have just moved into a new house. ✗ are:be 动词不构成完成时;✗ did:一般过去;✗ will:将来。

### s5 seq2 | ans: seen
现在完成时用 have + 过去分词;see 的过去分词是 seen。✓ the prettiest carpet I have ever seen. ✗ saw:过去式不能跟在 have 后;✗ see:原形;✗ seeing:现在分词。

### s5 seq3 | ans: rung
现在完成时用 has + 过去分词;ring 的过去分词是 rung。✓ He has rung me up five times. ✗ rang:过去式不能跟 has;✗ ring:原形;✗ ringing:现在分词。

### s5 seq4 | ans: hasn't

### s5 seq5 | ans: have been

### s5 seq6 | ans: been

### s5 seq7 | ans: ringing

### s5 seq8 | ans: has

### s5 seq9 | ans: has rung

### s5 seq10 | ans: has been ringing

### s5 seq11 | ans: beautifully
sing(唱)是动词,修饰动词要用副词 beautifully。✓ She sings beautifully. ✗ beautiful:形容词,修饰名词(a beautiful voice);✗ beauty:名词;✗ more beautiful:比较级形容词。

### s5 seq12 | ans: slowly

### s5 seq13 | ans: carefully

### s5 seq14 | ans: hard
"努力地"这个词形容词、副词同形,都是 hard(没有 -ly)。✓ He works very hard. ✗ hardly:意思是"几乎不",词义相反;✗ hardness:名词;✗ more hardly:词义和形式都不对。

### s5 seq15 | ans: hardly
"几乎不、几乎没"用 hardly(它不是 hard 的副词形式)。✓ He hardly ever does any work. ✗ hard:意思是"努力地",词义相反;✗ nearly:差点;✗ lately:最近。

### s5 seq16 | ans: lately
"最近、近来"用 lately(不是 late)。✓ Have you travelled by train lately? ✗ late:意思是"晚";✗ later:以后;✗ lastly:最后(列举时用)。
