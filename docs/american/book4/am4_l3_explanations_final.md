### s5 seq1 | ans: It
It 作形式主语,真正主语是后面的不定式 to realize…。✓ It is hard for us to realize… ✗ That / This / There:形式主语用 It。

### s5 seq2 | ans: to
it 形式主语句,真正主语是不定式;填不定式符号 to。✓ It is hard for us to realize how… ✗ for / of / that:此处用 to realize。

### s5 seq3 | ans: for
it 形式主语句,动作的执行者用 for sb 引出(不用 of sb)。✓ It was hard for the pioneers to find an inn. ✗ of / to / with:此结构用 for。

### s5 seq4 | ans: must
对过去的肯定推测“一定…了”用 must + have + 过去分词。✓ must have been hard for them. ✗ can / would / need:表推测用 must。

### s5 seq5 | ans: must
对过去的肯定推测,填 must。✓ The first climbers must have suffered greatly. ✗ shall / ought(须加 to)/ dare:表推测“一定”用 must。

### s5 seq6 | ans: have
must + have + 过去分词构成对过去的推测;填助动词 have。✓ must have felt like a triumph. ✗ has / had / having:must 后一律用 have + 过去分词。
