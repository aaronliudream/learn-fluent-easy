### U4《Study, but play too》—— 全文重写

比较级是原文的骨架(6 处),换词改不动,按 Aaron 方向重写:保留"劳逸结合"立意,改用并列与转折表达对比。**U4 是祈使句单元,老师建议那几句祈使句是考点,原样保住。**

**改后正文(190 词,原 189):**

> Last term, my friend Ben studied all the time. Every day after school, he did his homework until late at night. He never played sports and seldom saw his friends. "I must work hard," he always said. But soon Ben looked tired and unhappy, and his marks did not go up. One day our teacher gave him some good advice. "Ben," she said, "work hard, but don't forget to rest. Play some sport. Meet your friends. A tired mind cannot learn well." Ben decided to try. He began to play basketball twice a week. On weekends, he went out with his friends. At first he worried about losing study time, but something surprising happened. He felt happy, and his mind became clear and quick. His marks even went up. He slept well at night and woke up happy. He also made new friends when he played, and school did not feel boring any more. Now Ben knows the secret: don't just study all day. Take a break, have some fun, and then go back to your books. Work and play are like two legs — you need both to walk well.

**逐句改动:**

| 改前 | 改后 |
|---|---|
| his marks did not get **better** | his marks did not **go up** |
| He felt **happier**, and his mind became **clearer**. | He felt **happy**, and his mind became **clear and quick**. |
| He slept **better** at night | He slept **well** at night |
| school felt **less boring than before** | school **did not feel boring any more** |

**题目改动:**

| | 改前 | 改后 |
|---|---|---|
| 第3题选项C(答案) | Balancing work and play helps you do **better**. | Balancing work and play helps you do **well**. |
| 第3题解析 | 结尾点题:劳逸结合让你做得更好。 | 结尾点题:劳逸结合,学得好也玩得好。 |
| 第4题选项B | Because the tests were **easier**. | Because the tests were **not hard**. |
| 第4题选项D(答案) | Because rest made his mind **clearer** and he learned **better**. | Because rest made his mind **clear** and he learned **well**. |
| 第4题解析 | "his mind became **clearer**"→学得更好;靠推断。 | "his mind became **clear and quick**"→学得好;靠推断。 |
| 第5题选项D | study **harder** | study **all night** |
| 第5题解析 | 由上下文"have some fun, then go back"可推断 | 由上下文"have some fun, **and** then go back"可推断(补 and,原解析漏字) |
