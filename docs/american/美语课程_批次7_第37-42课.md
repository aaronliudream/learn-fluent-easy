# Lesson 37  We drove to the Grand Canyon!
## 我们开车去了大峡谷！

> 语法目标：不规则动词过去式第一批（went / took / drove / saw / ate / had） · 过去叙事串联
> 场景：公路旅行归来分享

## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: Derek drove to ___.
（a) Miami　b) The Grand Canyon ✓　c) The beach）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | FAITH: Derek! You're back! How was the road trip? | 费丝：德里克！你回来啦！公路旅行怎么样？ |
| 2 | DEREK: Incredible. We drove to the Grand Canyon! | 德里克：太震撼了。我们开车去了大峡谷！ |
| 3 | FAITH: No way! How long was the drive? | 费丝：不会吧！开了多久？ |
| 4 | DEREK: Twelve hours. We took Route 66 for a while. | 德里克：十二个小时。有一段我们走的是 66 号公路。 |
| 5 | FAITH: Classic! Tell me everything. | 费丝：太经典了！快全讲给我听。 |
| 6 | DEREK: We saw wild horses on the way. And we ate at a tiny diner in Arizona. | 德里克：路上我们看到了野马。还在亚利桑那一家小餐馆吃了饭。 |
| 7 | FAITH: A real diner? I love that. | 费丝：正宗的美式小餐馆？我太爱了。 |

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① 不规则动词过去式第一批（逐个记，没有 -ed）**

| 原形 | 过去式 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| go | **went** | We **went** at sunrise. |
| take | **took** | We **took** Route 66. |
| drive | **drove** | We **drove** to the canyon. |
| see | **saw** | We **saw** wild horses. |
| eat | **ate** | We **ate** at a diner. |
| have | **had** | I **had** a huge burger. |

**② 不规则 ≠ 规则**：这批动词不能加 -ed（~~goed / eated~~ ✗）。

**③ 过去式无人称变化复现**：I went / She went / They went。

**④ 过去叙事串联**：We drove... We took... We saw... We ate...（连续动作按顺序讲）。

# Lesson 38  They're too small
## 太小了

> 语法目标：不规则过去式第二批（bought / wore / got / paid） · too + 形容词（过度） · not too...
> 场景：网购球鞋到店退换

## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: Tessa's sneakers are too ___.
（a) Big　b) Small ✓　c) Old）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | RAY: Hi! How can I help you today? | 雷：您好！有什么可以帮您？ |
| 2 | TESSA: Hi. I bought these sneakers online last week. | 泰莎：你好。这双运动鞋是我上周网上买的。 |
| 3 | RAY: Okay. Is there a problem? | 雷：好的。有什么问题吗？ |
| 4 | TESSA: Yes—they're too small. I wore them once, and my feet hurt all day. | 泰莎：有——太小了。我就穿了一次，脚疼了一整天。 |
| 5 | RAY: Ouch. What size do you need? | 雷：哎哟。您需要什么码？ |
| 6 | TESSA: I got a nine, but I need a ten. | 泰莎：我买的九码，但我需要十码。 |
| 7 | RAY: Got it. Do you have the receipt? | 雷：明白。小票带了吗？ |
| 8 | TESSA: Yes, right here. I paid with my card. | 泰莎：带了，在这儿。我是刷卡付的。 |
| 9 | RAY: Perfect. One second... Okay, here's the ten. Try them on! | 雷：好的。稍等……十码的来了，试试！ |
| 10 | TESSA: These are perfect. Not too big, not too small. | 泰莎：完美。不大不小正合适。 |
| 11 | RAY: Great! Have a good one! | 雷：太好了！祝您愉快！ |

## 关2–4 · 核心词汇

| 词汇 | 美式音标 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 / 搭配 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| bought | /bɔt/ | v. | 买（buy 的过去式） | I bought these online. |
| sneakers | /ˈsnikərz/ | n. | 运动鞋 | a pair of sneakers |
| online | /ˌɑnˈlaɪn/ | adv. | 在网上 | bought them online |
| too | /tu/ | adv. | 太……（过度） | too small |
| wore | /wɔr/ | v. | 穿（wear 的过去式） | I wore them once. |
| once | /wʌns/ | adv. | 一次 | I wore them once. |
| feet | /fit/ | n. | 脚（复数，特殊） | My feet hurt. |
| Ouch! | /aʊtʃ/ | int. | 哎哟（疼） | Ouch! |

| receipt | /rɪˈsit/ | n. | 收据；小票 | Do you have the receipt? |
| paid | /peɪd/ | v. | 付款（pay 的过去式） | I paid with my card. |
| card | /kɑrd/ | n. | 银行卡 | pay with a card |

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① 不规则动词过去式第二批**

| 原形 | 过去式 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| buy | **bought** | I **bought** these online. |
| wear | **wore** | I **wore** them once. |
| get | **got** | I **got** a nine. |
| pay | **paid** | I **paid** with my card. |

**② too + 形容词（太……，带"有问题"的负面含义）**
- They're **too small**.（小到没法穿）/ The coffee is **too hot**.

⚠️ too ≠ very：very small（很小，中性描述）；**too** small（小得有问题）。

**③ not too...（不……，刚刚好）**：Not too big, not too small.

**④ 两批不规则动词小结（累计 10 个）**：went / took / drove / saw / ate / had / bought / wore / got / paid。

## 关6 · 美语点睛

|---|---|---|

| receipt | 同（p 不发音） | 小票 |
| Ouch! | Ow! | 哎哟（疼） |

# Lesson 39  Did you call a mechanic?
## 你叫修车师傅了吗？

> 语法目标：Did you...? — Yes, I did. / No, I didn't. · didn't + 动词原形 · 疑问中动词回原形
> 场景：给牙医诊所打电话改约

## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: Why did Victor miss his appointment?
（a) His car didn't start ✓　b) He was sick　c) He forgot）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | GLORIA: Lakeside Dental, this is Gloria. How can I help you? | 格洛丽亚：湖畔牙科，我是格洛丽亚。有什么可以帮您？ |
| 2 | VICTOR: Hi, this is Victor Cruz. I missed my appointment this morning. I'm so sorry. | 维克多：你好，我是维克多·克鲁兹。我今早的预约错过了，非常抱歉。 |
| 3 | GLORIA: Let me check... Did you have the nine o'clock? | 格洛丽亚：我查一下……您约的是九点那场吗？ |
| 4 | VICTOR: Yes, I did. My car didn't start. | 维克多：是的。我的车打不着火了。 |
| 5 | GLORIA: Oh no. Did you call a mechanic? | 格洛丽亚：哎呀。您叫修车师傅了吗？ |
| 6 | VICTOR: I did, but he didn't come until noon. | 维克多：叫了，但他中午才来。 |
| 7 | GLORIA: That's rough. Well, don't worry—these things happen. | 格洛丽亚：真够呛。不过别担心——这种事难免。 |
| 8 | VICTOR: Thanks. Can I reschedule? | 维克多：谢谢。我能改约吗？ |
| 9 | GLORIA: Sure. Did Dr. Kim see you last time, or Dr. Lopez? | 格洛丽亚：当然。上次给您看诊的是金医生还是洛佩兹医生？ |

| 11 | GLORIA: Perfect. How about Thursday at ten? | 格洛丽亚：好的。周四十点怎么样？ |
| 12 | VICTOR: Thursday works. Thank you so much! | 维克多：周四可以。太感谢了！ |

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① Did you + 动词原形...?（过去时一般疑问句）**

| 问 | 肯定答 | 否定答 |
|---|---|---|
| **Did you have** the nine o'clock? | Yes, I **did**. | No, I **didn't**. |

**② didn't + 动词原形（过去时否定）**：My car **didn't start**.（⚠️ 不说 ~~didn't started~~）

**③ 疑问中动词回原形**：Did Dr. Kim **see** you?（不是 ~~Did she saw~~）

**④ 应答简式**：I did, but...（叫了，但是……）。
