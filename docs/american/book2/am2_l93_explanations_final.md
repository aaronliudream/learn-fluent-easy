### s5 seq1 | ans: was given
过去“被赠予”,用一般过去被动 was + 过去分词。✓ A clock was given to our town long ago. ✗ gave:主动;✗ is given:一般现在被动,与 long ago 不符;✗ has given:现在完成主动。

### s5 seq2 | ans: is admired
经常性地“被赞赏”,用一般现在被动 is + 过去分词。✓ It is admired by everyone. ✗ admires:主动;✗ was admired:一般过去;✗ is admiring:进行主动。

### s5 seq3 | ans: was made

### s5 seq4 | ans: has been kept
