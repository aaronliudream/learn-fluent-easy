### s5 seq1 | ans: 动词 + -ing,当名词用
动名词 = 动词 + -ing,把动作当名词来用。✓ Fishing(钓鱼)、Swimming(游泳)都是动名词。

### s5 seq2 | ans: Swimming is fun.
Swimming is fun 里的 Swimming 是动名词(当名词用);he swims / is swimming / Swim! 里的都不是动名词。

### s5 seq3 | ans: 单数(is / was)
动名词作主语时看作单数,后面的谓语动词用 is / was。✓ Swimming is good for you.

### s5 seq4 | ans: Fishing
"钓鱼"作主语,用动名词 Fishing。✓ Fishing is my favorite sport. Fish / Fished / Fishes 都不能表示"钓鱼这件事"。

### s5 seq5 | ans: Watching
"看电视"作主语,用动名词 Watching。✓ Watching television is fun.

### s5 seq6 | ans: 动名词(-ing)
介词(in / on / of / without…)后面接动词时,一律用动名词。✓ interested in fishing, keen on cycling.

### s5 seq7 | ans: fishing
be interested in 后面接动名词,"对钓鱼感兴趣"= interested in fishing。✓ I'm interested in fishing.

### s5 seq8 | ans: cycling
介词 on 后面用动名词,keen on cycling = 热衷骑车。✓ He is keen on cycling.

### s5 seq9 | ans: 这个动作比句子的动作更早发生
动名词完成式 having done,强调它比句子里的动作更早发生。✓ After having spent the morning, I went home.

### s5 seq10 | ans: having written
"写完信"在"出去"之前,用动名词完成式 having written。✓ After having written the letter, he went out.

### s5 seq11 | ans: saying
without 是介词,后面用动名词 saying,"没说再见"= without saying goodbye。✓ He left without saying goodbye.

### s5 seq12 | ans: leaving
before 后面接动名词 leaving,"离开房间前"= before leaving the room。✓ Before leaving the room, he turned off the light.

### s5 seq13 | ans: reading
enjoy 后面只能接动名词,enjoy reading = 喜欢看书。✓ I enjoy reading in bed.

### s5 seq14 | ans: helping
for 是介词,后面用动名词 helping,"谢谢你帮我"= Thank you for helping me。✓

### s5 seq15 | ans: enjoy(enjoy doing)
enjoy 后面只能接动名词 enjoy doing;want / hope / decide 后面接 to do,不接动名词。

### s5 seq16 | ans: doing
finish 后面只能接动名词,finish doing = 做完。✓ I finished doing my homework.

### s5 seq17 | ans: 动名词(instead of doing)
instead of 是介词短语,后面接动名词。✓ instead of catching fish 而不是钓鱼。

### s5 seq18 | ans: staying
be afraid of 后面接动名词,"害怕独自待着"= afraid of staying alone。✓ She is afraid of staying alone.

### s6 seq1 | ans: favorite
美语"最喜欢的"拼 favorite;英式拼 favourite。✓ my favorite sport.

### s6 seq2 | ans: trash / garbage
美语"垃圾"常说 trash 或 garbage;英式说 rubbish。✓ old boots and trash.

### s6 seq3 | ans: 放弃;戒掉
give up = 放弃;也指戒掉(烟酒等)。✓ give up fishing 别再钓鱼。
顺带记:举起 = lift up;赠送 = give away;追上 = catch up.

### s6 seq4 | ans: 浪费时间
a waste of time = 浪费时间。✓ It's a waste of time,这是浪费时间。
顺带记:很花钱 = cost a lot;很值得 = worth it;省时间 = save time.

### s6 seq5 | ans: 对…感兴趣
be interested in = 对…感兴趣。✓ interested in fishing 对钓鱼感兴趣。
顺带记:对…厌烦 = be tired of;负责… = be in charge of;擅长… = be good at.

### s6 seq6 | ans: 而不是;代替
instead of = 而不是、代替。✓ instead of catching fish 没钓到鱼(而是别的)。
顺带记:除…之外 = besides;为了 = for;由于 = because of.

### s7 seq1 | ans: catching
without 是介词,后面用动名词 catching。✓ without catching anything 什么也没钓到。
✗ catch:介词后不能用动词原形;✗ caught:介词后不用过去式;✗ to catch:介词后不接不定式.

### s7 seq2 | ans: going
instead of 后面用动名词 going。✓ instead of going home 而不是回家。
✗ go:介词后不能用原形;✗ went:介词后不用过去式;✗ to go:介词后不接不定式.

### s7 seq3 | ans: Sitting
"坐在船里"作主语,用动名词 Sitting。✓ Sitting in a boat is my pleasure.
✗ Sit:原形不能作主语;✗ Sat:过去式不能作主语;✗ Sits:第三人称形式不能作主语.

✗ fish:give up 后不接原形;✗ to fish:give up 后不接不定式;✗ fished:give up 后不接过去式.
