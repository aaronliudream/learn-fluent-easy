### s5 seq1 | ans: were
过去进行时 = was/were + 动词-ing。主语 The detectives 是复数,用 were。✓ The detectives were waiting.

### s5 seq2 | ans: was
主语 A guard 是单数,用 was。✓ A guard was opening the parcel.

### s5 seq3 | ans: running
run 以"重读闭音节+单辅音"结尾,-ing 要双写 n → running。✓ He was running to the gate.

### s5 seq4 | ans: having
have 以 e 结尾,变 -ing 去掉 e → having。✓ They were having dinner.（别写成 haveing）

### s5 seq5 | ans: sitting
sit 以"短元音+单辅音"结尾,-ing 要双写 t → sitting。✓ I was sitting on the grass.

### s5 seq6 | ans: was watching
"昨晚十点那个时刻正在看",用过去进行时 was watching。✓ At ten last night, I was watching a movie.

### s5 seq7 | ans: 过去某个时刻正在进行的动作
过去进行时表示"过去某个时刻正在进行"的动作,强调"当时正在做"。✓ Everyone was holding their breath.

### s5 seq8 | ans: when
"飞机降落"是突然发生的一下,用 when 引出;正在进行的背景用过去进行时。✓ The detectives were watching when the plane landed.

### s5 seq9 | ans: when
"电话突然响了"是突发的短暂动作,用 when 连接。✓ I was having breakfast when the phone rang.

### s5 seq10 | ans: While
两件事在同一段时间同时进行,用 While 连接,两个动作都用过去进行时。✓ While one guard was keeping watch, another was opening the box.

### s5 seq11 | ans: 过去进行时(正在进行的动作)
while 意思是"在…期间",后面常接过去进行时,表示"正在进行"的动作。✓ while she was listening to the radio.

### s5 seq12 | ans: I was watching TV at ten last night.
"十点那个时刻正在看"用过去进行时 was watching。✓ I was watching TV at ten last night.

### s5 seq13 | ans: was watching 强调当时正在进行;watched 只说'做过'
was watching(过去进行时)强调"当时那一刻正在进行";watched(一般过去时)只表示"做过"这件事、不强调进行。

### s5 seq14 | ans: He wasn't sleeping.
过去进行时的否定 = was/were + not + V-ing。✓ He wasn't sleeping.（不是 didn't sleeping,也不是 wasn't slept）

### s5 seq15 | ans: What were you doing?
过去进行时的疑问句把 was/were 提到主语前;you 用 were。✓ What were you doing?

### s5 seq16 | ans: 守卫,站岗
keep guard / keep watch = 守卫、站岗。✓ One guard was keeping watch at the door.

### s5 seq17 | ans: 令他们吃惊的是

### s5 seq18 | ans: 装满…,充满…
be full of = 装满、充满。✓ The parcel was full of stones and sand.
