What was she like?
（课本原文写的是真实人物赵一曼,并附 1936 年真实家书。改写件不复述任何真实生平与书信内容。）

As a little girl, I was often told, "Your grandmother Zhao Yiman was a hero." However, to me, she was just a black-and-white picture on the wall. She died long before I was born. So, I never got to know her. According to my great-aunt, she was a kind person who always thought of others first.

In my teenage years, Grandma appeared in my textbooks as a great fighter. My teacher said that she was a brave soldier who never gave in. She didn't say a word even when she was badly hurt by the enemy.

Years later, I followed Grandma's footsteps across China. She fought in many places. I saw Yiman Street, Yiman Park and Yiman Primary School, all named after her. Even now, people speak of her with great respect. I realised that Zhao Yiman was a hero who would live forever in Chinese people's hearts.

After I became a mother myself, I started to wonder, "How could a woman who loved her young son leave him behind?" In a letter from Grandma to my father, I found the answer. Between the lines, I saw a gentle mother who chose to give her life for her country.
