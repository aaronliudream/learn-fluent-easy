## 一、教材(新概念英语第二册 · WeLib 443pg)—— 定主考

- **PDF p63(印60)**:`Lesson 13 The Greenwood Boys 绿林少年` — First listen…(Why will the police have a difficult time?)+ 课文。核心(**满篇将来进行时**):
  - The Greenwood Boys **are** a group of pop singers. At present, they **are visiting** all parts of the country.(现在进行,对比铺垫)
  - They **will be arriving** here tomorrow. They **will be coming** by train and most of the young people **will be meeting** them at the station.
  - Tomorrow evening they **will be singing** at the Workers' Club. The Greenwood Boys **will be staying** for five days.
  - During this time, they **will give** five performances.(一般将来,对比)
  - As usual, the police **will have** a difficult time. They **will be trying** to keep order. It **is** always the same on these occasions.
  - New words(官方5):group(l.1)/gruːp/、pop singer(l.1)/ˈpɒp-ˌsɪŋə/、club(l.5)/klʌb/、performance(l.7)/pəˈfɔːməns/、occasion(l.9)/əˈkeɪʒən/。
  - **Notes(考纲边界权威)**:② **will be arriving = 将来进行时**,表示最近的将来正在进行的动作或说话人设想已安排好的事;③ by train(以此类推 by air/by sea/by bus);④ as usual=和往常一样;⑤ keep order=维持秩序。
- **PDF p64(印61)**:`Key structures 关键句型 · **What will you be doing tomorrow? 将来进行时**`(**主考权威页**):
  - **现在进行(Now)vs 将来进行(Tomorrow)**对照:I am writing letters now. / I shall be writing letters all day tomorrow. · He is working in the garden. / He will be working in the garden tomorrow. · Are you washing your car? / Will you be washing your car tomorrow? · They are playing football. / They will be playing football tomorrow.
  - **(b)一般将来时 ≈ 将来进行时(同义可换)**:I'll come tomorrow. / I'll be coming tomorrow. · He'll arrive in a minute. / He'll be arriving in a minute. · He'll catch the 4 o'clock train. / He'll be catching the 4 o'clock train. · She'll meet him. / She'll be meeting him.
  - Exercise A:划出课文所有将来进行时动词。
  - **裁决**:教材 Key structures 明标将来进行时 → **本课主考=将来进行时**(will/shall be + V-ing;现进 vs 将进;各人称;疑问 Will you be doing…?;≈ 一般将来时)。

## 四、三源边界裁决(单一主题)

- **主考(教材 Key structures)= 将来进行时**:will/shall be + V-ing · 现在进行 vs 将来进行(now vs tomorrow) · 'll be / shall be 各人称 · 疑问 Will you be doing…? / 否定 won't be doing · ≈ 一般将来时(同义) · 用法(安排好的/最近将来正在进行)。题量大头。
- **辅考语言点**:by + 交通工具(by train/air/sea/bus,Note③)· at present/as usual/keep order 固定表达 · 现在进行时(at present 正在)复习对照。
- **无挂账新增**(L13 反而兑现 L12 的将来进行时挂账)。
- **认知题 ≤3**:将来进行时含义(G1)· ≈一般将来时可换(G5)· 表安排好的将来(G6)。
