### s5 seq1 | ans: stopped
叙事里“突然发生”的一件事,用一般过去时。✓ the train suddenly stopped. ✗ was stopping:过去进行,表“正在停”不合“突然停”;✗ stops:一般现在;✗ had stopped:过去完成。

### s5 seq2 | ans: cleared
“把树清走”是发生并完成的动作,用一般过去时。✓ The workers cleared the tree away. ✗ were clearing:过去进行,强调过程,此处说结果;✗ clear:原形;✗ had cleared:过去完成。

### s5 seq3 | ans: started

### s5 seq4 | ans: were traveling

### s5 seq9 | ans: used to
表示“过去有、现在没了”的状态,用 used to(would 不表状态)。✓ There used to be a small station here. ✗ would:不表状态;✗ was used to / is used to:意思是“习惯于”，不同。

### s5 seq10 | ans: would
