# AM3 L51 逐题解释(定稿·原创承载·过去将来 would·when 定语从句/could have done/Nor 倒装/as…as·enough to do/take over·be capable of·预测未来) · 供 gen-book3-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: when
先行词 a time 指时间,关系词在从句中作时间状语,用关系副词 when。✓ a time when computers would be small. ✗ which / that(此处需关系副词)/ where(指地点)。

### s5 seq2 | ans: would
从过去的角度看未来,用过去将来时 would + 动词原形。✓ He predicted that computers would be used … ✗ will(从现在看未来)/ are / have:过去将来用 would。

### s5 seq3 | ans: would
从过去(70年代)看未来,用过去将来时 would。✓ computers would be in every home. ✗ will / are / do:过去将来用 would。

### s5 seq4 | ans: could
“谁能想到”是对过去可能性的推测,用 could have done;此处填情态动词 could。✓ Who could have imagined this? ✗ can(现在)/ must(必然)/ need:表“(过去)能否”用 could。

### s5 seq5 | ans: have
could not have + 过去分词,“过去不可能做过”;情态动词 could 后接 have + 过去分词。✓ could not have foreseen. ✗ has / had / having:情态动词后用 have。

### s5 seq6 | ans: foreseen
could have + 过去分词;foresee 的过去分词是 foreseen。✓ could not have foreseen. ✗ foresee(原形)/ foresaw(过去式)/ foreseeing:此处用过去分词 foreseen。

### s5 seq7 | ans: Nor
“他也无法…”用否定词 Nor 置句首引起倒装。✓ Nor could he have foreseen … ✗ So(用于肯定“也…”)/ And / But:表“也不”置句首用 Nor。

### s5 seq8 | ans: could
Nor 置句首,分句要倒装,把情态动词 could 提到主语前。✓ Nor could he imagine … ✗ he could(未倒装)/ did he(与原句情态不符)/ he:倒装后用 could。

### s5 seq9 | ans: nor
“也不”接在否定句后并引起倒装,用 nor。✓ …, nor did he foresee smartphones. ✗ so / and / but:表“也不”用 nor。

### s5 seq10 | ans: as
as + 形容词 + as,“与…一样”;第二个比较词用 as。✓ as common as TV sets. ✗ than(用于比较级)/ like / so:同级比较第二处用 as。

### s5 seq11 | ans: enough
形容词 + enough + to do,“足够…以至于能”;enough 放形容词之后。✓ small enough to hold. ✗ too(too…to 表否定结果)/ very / so:此处用 enough。

### s5 seq12 | ans: as
否定同级比较 not as/so + 形容词 + as,“不如…那么大”;结尾用 as。✓ not as large as the old mainframes. ✗ more / than / such:同级比较结尾用 as。

### s5 seq13 | ans: over

### s5 seq14 | ans: of

### s5 seq15 | ans: of
