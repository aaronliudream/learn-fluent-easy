## L83《The Election Results》· am2_l83 · U11 · 时态综合复习(KS74-82)
考点:一般现在/现在进行/一般过去/过去进行/现在完成/现在完成进行/过去完成——各时态综合运用辨析。(原课涉 elections 政治,改"校园社团选举结果揭晓"中性情节。)
1. Every year, our school **holds** an election for club president.（一般现在)
2. This year, three students **were running**, and the whole school **was watching**.（过去进行)
3. Last Friday, everyone **gathered** in the hall to hear the results.（一般过去)
4. By the time I **arrived**, the votes **had already been counted**.（过去完成被动)
5. "The students **have been waiting** all week for this," the teacher said.（现在完成进行)
6. Right now, the principal **is announcing** the winner.（现在进行)
7. Mia **has won** by just five votes!（现在完成)
8. She **was** so surprised that she **couldn't** speak for a moment.（一般过去)
9. "I **have never felt** this happy," she finally said.（现在完成)
10. Now she **is planning** all the fun events the club **will hold** this year.（进行+将来)
词表:election, president, run(参选), vote, count, gather, announce, winner, principal, event

## L85《Never Too Old to Learn》· am2_l85 · U11 · 将来进行/将来完成(KS13/37/61)
考点:G1 一般将来 will do · G2 将来进行 will be doing · G3 将来完成 will have done · G4 将来完成进行 will have been doing。(原课干净:老人上学温情故事,照搬美语化。)
1. Next month, my grandfather **will be starting** college at the age of seventy.（将来进行)
2. By this time next year, he **will have finished** his first year.（将来完成)
3. "By my next birthday, I **will have been studying** for a whole year," he says proudly.（将来完成进行)
4. Everyone **will be watching** to see how he does.（将来进行)
5. "Some people **will laugh**," he admits, "but I don't mind."（一般将来)
6. "In five years, I **will have earned** my degree."（将来完成)
7. He **will be sitting** in class with students sixty years younger.（将来进行)
8. "You **will never be** too old to learn something new," he tells me.（一般将来)
9. By the time he graduates, he **will have proved** everyone wrong.（将来完成)
10. I think he **will be** the most inspiring student the college **has** ever seen.（将来+现在完成)
词表:college, degree, graduate, admit, proudly, inspiring, prove, earn, mind, retire

## L88《Lost and Found》· am2_l88 · U11 · 条件句 if(KS16/40/64)
考点:G1 真实条件(if + 现在时, will do)· G2 非真实条件(if + 过去式, would do)· G3 if 从句时态规则。(原课《Trapped in a mine》涉矿难被困,改"丢失又找回背包"日常情节。)
1. If you **lose** something at school, you **should** check the lost-and-found box.
2. Last week, if I **hadn't checked** it, I **would have lost** my backpack forever.
3. "If I **were** you," my friend said, "I **would** put my name on everything."
4. If everyone **labeled** their things, the box **wouldn't be** so full.
5. "If you **find** my bag," I told the office, "please **call** me."
6. If I **had been** more careful, this **wouldn't have happened**.
7. "If it **turns** up, we **will let** you know," the secretary said.
8. If you **ask** politely, people usually **help** you.
9. The next day, if you **can** believe it, my bag **was** on my desk!
10. If I **ever lose** something again, I **will know** exactly what to do.
词表:lost-and-found, backpack, label, forever, careful, turn up, secretary, politely, believe, exactly

## L89《A Slip of the Tongue》· am2_l89 · U12 · 情态 must/have to/need/should(KS17/41/65)
考点:G1 must(必须/推测)· G2 have to(客观必须)· G3 needn't/don't have to(不必)· G4 should(应该)。(原课干净:口误闹笑话,照搬美语化。)
1. "You **must** stay calm on stage," the teacher told the new host.
2. "You **don't have to** be perfect, but you **should** speak clearly."
3. That evening, the host **had to** introduce a famous writer.
4. He was so nervous that he **must have** mixed up the names.
5. "You **needn't** worry," the writer whispered kindly. "It **happens** to everyone."
6. "I **should have** practiced more," the host said afterward.
7. "You **mustn't** be so hard on yourself," she replied with a smile.
8. "Next time, I **must** remember to breathe first," he laughed.
9. "You **should** be proud — you kept going!" she said.
10. Everyone agreed: a small slip **needn't** ruin a good evening.
词表:slip of the tongue, host, stage, calm, introduce, nervous, mix up, whisper, breathe, ruin

## L91《Three People in a Hot-Air Balloon》· am2_l91 · U12 · can/be able to/manage to(KS19/43/67)
考点:G1 can/could(能力)· G2 be able to(各时态能力)· G3 manage to(设法做成)· G4 三者区别。(原课干净:热气球趣事,照搬美语化。)
1. Last summer, three friends **were able to** join a hot-air balloon ride.
2. "Can you all fit in the basket?" the pilot asked. "We **can** try!" they said.
3. Soon they **could** see the whole valley below them.
4. But the wind **was** strong, and the pilot **couldn't** steer easily.
5. "Don't worry — I **can** handle it," he said calmly.
6. After a tense minute, he **managed to** turn the balloon around.
7. None of them **had ever been able to** see their town from the sky before.
8. "I **can't** believe how beautiful it is!" one of them said.
9. Finally, the pilot **was able to** land the balloon safely in a field.
10. They **managed to** have the adventure of a lifetime — and no one got hurt.
词表:hot-air balloon, basket, pilot, fit, valley, steer, handle, tense, land, adventure

## L92《The Windows Need Cleaning》· am2_l92 · U12 · need doing / want doing(KS20/44/68)
考点:G1 need + doing(= need to be done)· G2 want + doing(同义)· G3 主动形式表被动意义。(原课干净:大扫除趣事,照搬美语化。)
1. "I don't think the windows **need cleaning**," Dad said, looking at them.
2. "Actually, they really **need washing**," Mom laughed. "They're covered in dust."
3. "The whole house **wants tidying** before the guests come," she added.
4. "The carpet **needs vacuuming**, and the plants **need watering**."
5. "Does the garden **need doing** too?" I asked with a groan.
6. "The grass **wants cutting**," Dad admitted, "but that can wait."
7. We all agreed the kitchen **needed cleaning** the most.
8. "My room doesn't **need tidying**," my brother claimed. Nobody believed him.
9. After three hours, nothing **needed doing** anymore.
10. "The house **needs** more than cleaning," Dad joked. "It **needs** a rest!"
词表:need doing, tidy, dust, carpet, vacuum, water(浇水), groan, admit, claim, guest
