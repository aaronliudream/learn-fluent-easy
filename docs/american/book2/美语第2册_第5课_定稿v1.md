## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,现在完成时 + 一般过去时对比)

> My uncle Jack has owned a small bookstore downtown for years. Last month he opened a second store across the river. The new store is only two miles away. But the phone company hasn't installed a phone line yet. So my uncle has come up with a clever idea. He has just bought ten pigeons! Yesterday, one pigeon carried his first note from the new store to the old one. The little bird covered the distance in just four minutes. Up to now, Jack has sent a great many requests and messages from one store to the other. He has never lost a single message. In this way, he has started his own private "pigeon mail" service.
