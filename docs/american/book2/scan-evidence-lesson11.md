## 一、教材(新概念英语第二册 · WeLib 443pg)—— 定主考

- **PDF p55(印52)**:`Lesson 11 One good turn deserves another 礼尚往来` — First listen…(Who paid for Tony's dinner?)+ 课文。核心(**满篇混用各时态语态**):
  - I **was having** dinner at a restaurant **when** Tony Steele **came** in.(过去进行 + when + 一般过去)
  - Tony **worked** in a lawyer's office years ago, **but** he **is now working** at a bank.(一般过去 vs 现在进行,对比)
  - He **gets** a good salary, **but** he always **borrows** money from his friends **and** never **pays** it back.(一般现在,习惯)
  - He **has never borrowed** money from me.(现在完成,经历)
  - **While** he **was eating**, I **asked** him **to lend** me twenty pounds.(过去进行 while + 一般过去 + **ask sb to do 宾补**)
  - To my surprise, he **gave** me the money immediately.(一般过去)
  - 'I **have never borrowed** any money from you,' Tony said, '**so** now you can pay for my dinner!'
  - New words(官方6):turn(title)/tɜːrn/、deserve(title)/dɪˈzɜːrv/、lawyer(l.2)/ˈlɔːjər/、bank(l.3)/bæŋk/、salary(l.3)/ˈsæləri/、immediately(l.7)/ɪˈmiːdiətli/。
  - **Notes(考纲边界权威)**:① One good turn deserves another=谚语"礼尚往来";② gets a good salary=有一份很高的薪水;③ never pays it back=从不归还。
- **PDF p56(印53)**:`Key structures 关键句型 · **Review KS2-10 复习第 2–10 课的关键句型**`(**主考权威页**):
  - **Now, Often and Always (KS2)** 第2课 = 现在进行时/一般现在时
  - **What happened? (KS3)** 第3课 = 一般过去时
  - **What has happened? (KS4)** 第4课 = 现在完成时
  - **What were you doing when I telephoned? (KS7)** 第7课 = 过去进行时
  - **It was made in Germany. (KS10)** 第10课 = 被动语态
  - Exercise A:从课文选出 a 现在正在进行 / b 通常发生 / c 过去发生 / d 已经发生 / e 过去某刻正在进行 的动词(**= 时态辨析核心技能**)。

## 四、三源边界裁决(A+覆盖补丁 · 第二次执行)

- **主考(教材 Key structures)= 时态语态综合复习/辨析**:一般现在(习惯 gets/borrows/pays)· 现在进行(此刻 is working)· 一般过去(worked/saw/came/asked/gave)· 现在完成(经历 has never borrowed)· 过去进行(was having…when / while…was eating)· 被动(复习 was made/was built)· **综合辨析(依语境/时间状语选时态)= 复习核心技能**。题量大头(11题)。
- **辅考1(同步①类,课文出现)= 并列连词 and/but/for/so**:4题 + 关6语块。
- **辅考2(同步①类,课文出现)= v.+sb+to do 宾补(ask/want/tell/allow sb to do)**:3题 + 关6语块。
- **挂账**:同步宾补深度(let/make/have 省to、被动加to、had better)+ 连词深度(连接性副词 however/therefore、and·or 表条件)→ 挂 PROGRESS,并入后续合适课次,单元2末批次核对清零。
