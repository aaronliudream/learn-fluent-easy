### s5 seq1 | ans: match
match sth for …,“就…而论比得上”;nothing can match … 表“没有能比得上的”。✓ Nothing can match a plane for speed. ✗ join 加入;✗ follow 跟随;✗ copy 复制——都不表“比得上”。

### s5 seq2 | ans: for
match … for …,for 作“就…而论”。✓ match a plane for speed. ✗ to / with / at:此处固定用 for。

### s5 seq3 | ans: nothing
表“没有任何东西能比得上”,用否定词 nothing 作主语。✓ Nothing can match a plane. ✗ anything / something / everything:此处需否定词 nothing。

### s5 seq4 | ans: Hardly
“几乎没有人”用 hardly anyone;hardly 本身表否定。✓ Hardly anyone can enjoy … ✗ Nearly / Almost / Quite:表“几乎没有”用 hardly。

### s5 seq5 | ans: hardly
“几乎没有(空间)”用 hardly any;hardly 表否定程度。✓ hardly any space. ✗ nearly / almost / mostly:“几乎没有”用 hardly。

### s5 seq6 | ans: truly
hardly anyone 已含否定,后面动词用肯定形式,不能再加 not/never;填肯定副词 truly。✓ Hardly anyone truly enjoys it. ✗ not / never(双重否定)/ hardly(重复)。

### s5 seq7 | ans: to take
it 作形式主语,真正主语是不定式;It is impossible to take …。✓ It is almost impossible to take your mind off. ✗ taking / take / took:形式主语后接不定式 to take。

### s5 seq8 | ans: to read

### s5 seq9 | ans: It
真正主语是后面的不定式 to fly,句首用形式主语 It。✓ It is exhilarating to fly above the clouds. ✗ That / This / There:形式主语用 It。
