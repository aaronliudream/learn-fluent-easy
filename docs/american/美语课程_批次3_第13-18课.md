# Lesson 13  Is there a pan here?
## 这里有平底锅吗？

> 语法目标：There is... / Is there...? 问答 · 方位介词 in / on / under · There it is.
> 场景：新公寓厨房，朋友帮忙做塔可

## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: Where is the salt?
（a) On the counter　b) In the cabinet　c) Under the coffee maker ✓）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | OWEN: Nice kitchen, Nina! | 欧文：厨房不错啊，妮娜！ |
| 2 | NINA: Thanks! It's small but cozy. | 妮娜：谢谢！小是小，但很温馨。 |
| 3 | OWEN: Okay, taco night! Is there a pan here? | 欧文：好嘞，塔可之夜！这儿有平底锅吗？ |
| 4 | NINA: Yes, there is. It's in that cabinet. | 妮娜：有的，在那个柜子里。 |
| 5 | OWEN: Got it. And the salt? | 欧文：找到了。盐呢？ |
| 6 | NINA: It's on the counter, next to the stove. | 妮娜：在台面上，炉灶旁边。 |
| 7 | OWEN: Hmm, it isn't here. | 欧文：呃，这儿没有啊。 |
| 8 | NINA: Oops! Then it's under the coffee maker. Sorry! | 妮娜：哎呀！那就是在咖啡机下面。抱歉！ |
| 9 | OWEN: Ha! There it is. | 欧文：哈！找到了。 |
| 10 | NINA: Great. Taco time! | 妮娜：太好了。开做塔可！ |

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① There is... 句型（"某处有某物"，单数）**

| 句式 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| 肯定 | **There is** a pan in the cabinet.（= There's） |
| 否定 | **There isn't** a pan here. |
| 疑问 | **Is there** a pan here? — Yes, **there is**. / No, **there isn't**. |

**② 方位介词三件套**

| 介词 | 含义 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|
| **in** | 在……里面 | **in** the cabinet |
| **on** | 在……上面 | **on** the counter |
| **under** | 在……下面 | **under** the coffee maker |

**③ next to 复习**：next to the stove（炉灶旁边）。

**④ There it is.**：找到东西时的感叹（"在这儿呢"）。

## 关8 · 听对话理解题

1. What night is it? — a) Taco night ✓　b) Pizza night　c) Movie night　d) Game night
2. Where is the pan? — a) On the counter　b) In the cabinet ✓　c) Under the table　d) Next to the stove

## 关9 · 情景应答

1. 问朋友家里有没有杯子 → Is there a cup here?
2. 别人在找钥匙，钥匙就在椅子下面 → It's under the chair.
3. 终于找到了手机 → There it is!

## 关10 · 本课通关

A. 选择填空
1. ___ there a lamp in your room? — Yes, there is.　a) Is　b) Are　c) Am　d) Be → **a**
2. There ___ a truck on the street.　a) are　b) is　c) am　d) be → **b**
3. The keys are ___ the box.（盒子里）　a) on　b) under　c) in　d) at → **c**
4. Is there a pen here? — No, there ___.　a) aren't　b) is　c) not　d) isn't → **d**

B. 句型转换
1. There is a lamp on the desk.（变一般疑问句）→ Is there a lamp on the desk?
2. The cat is on the chair.（改到椅子下面）→ The cat is under the chair.
3. There is a pan in the cabinet.（变否定句）→ There isn't a pan in the cabinet.

> 语法目标：There are... / Are there...? 问答 · some / any · 介词 behind
> 场景：收拾车库，准备车库旧物出售

## 关1 · 课文学习

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① There are... 句型（复数）**

| 句式 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| 肯定 | **There are** two bikes behind the boxes. |
| 否定 | **There aren't** any tools here. |
| 疑问 | **Are there** any bikes? — Yes, **there are**. / No, **there aren't**. |

**② some / any**

| 用法 | 例子 |
|---|---|
| 肯定句用 **some** | There are **some** old games. |
| 疑问句/否定句用 **any** | **Are there any** tools? / There aren't **any** cups. |

**③ 介词 behind（在……后面）**：behind the boxes（介词地图：in / on / under / next to / **behind**）。

**④ there is / there are 对照**：单数用 is，复数用 are。

# Lesson 15  Come in!
## 快请进！

> 语法目标：祈使句（Come in / Sit down / Have some... / Try...） · must + 动词原形 · please
> 场景：招待来家做客的朋友

## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: What is fresh?
（a) The cookies　b) The lemonade ✓　c) The brownies）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | PRIYA: Leo! Come in, come in! | 普丽娅：利奥！快请进！ |
| 2 | LEO: Thanks, Priya. Wow, your place is nice! | 利奥：谢谢，普丽娅。哇，你家真不错！ |
| 3 | PRIYA: Sit down, please. Make yourself at home. | 普丽娅：请坐，别拘束，就当自己家。 |
| 4 | LEO: Thank you. | 利奥：谢谢。 |
| 5 | PRIYA: Have some lemonade. It's fresh. | 普丽娅：喝点柠檬水，刚做的。 |
| 6 | LEO: Mmm, it's so good! | 利奥：嗯，太好喝了！ |
| 7 | PRIYA: And try these cookies. But careful—they're hot! | 普丽娅：再尝尝这些曲奇。不过小心——烫！ |
| 8 | LEO: Ha! Okay. Oh wow, they're amazing. | 利奥：哈哈！好。哇，太好吃了。 |
| 9 | PRIYA: You must try my brownies next time. | 普丽娅：下次你一定要尝尝我的布朗尼。 |
| 10 | LEO: It's a deal! | 利奥：一言为定！ |

## 关2–4 · 核心词汇

| 词汇 | 美式音标 | 词性 | 释义 | 例句 / 搭配 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| come in | /kʌm ɪn/ | 短语 | 进来 | Come in! |
| place | /pleɪs/ | n. | 家；住处（口语） | your place |
| sit down | /sɪt daʊn/ | 短语 | 坐下 | Sit down, please. |
| make yourself at home | — | 短语 | 别拘束，随意 | Make yourself at home. |
| have | /hæv/ | v. | 吃；喝（招待用） | Have some lemonade. |
| lemonade | /ˌlɛməˈneɪd/ | n. | 柠檬水 | fresh lemonade |
| fresh | /frɛʃ/ | adj. | 新鲜的；现做的 | It's fresh. |
| cookie | /ˈkʊki/ | n. | 曲奇饼干 | try these cookies |
| try | /traɪ/ | v. | 尝试；品尝 | Try this! |
| brownie | /ˈbraʊni/ | n. | 布朗尼蛋糕 | my brownies |
| next time | /nɛkst taɪm/ | 短语 | 下次 | next time |
| It's a deal. | — | 短语 | 一言为定 | It's a deal! |

## 关5 · 语法专项

**① 祈使句（动词原形开头，向对方发出请求/指令）**

| 例子 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| **Come** in! | 请进！ |
| **Sit** down, please. | 请坐。 |
| **Have** some lemonade. | 喝点柠檬水。 |
| **Try** these cookies. | 尝尝这些曲奇。 |

**② please 的位置**：句首或句尾均可——Please sit down. / Sit down, please.

**③ must + 动词原形（强烈推荐）**：You **must try** my brownies.（must 后面必须接动词原形）

**④ 否定祈使预告**：Don't + 动词原形（如 Don't worry. 别担心），第 20 课详学。

## 关6 · 美语点睛

| 美语 🇺🇸 | 英语 🇬🇧 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| Make yourself at home. | 同（美语高频） | 别拘束，当自己家 |
| lemonade | —（英式 lemonade 指汽水） | 美式指鲜榨柠檬水 |
| cookie | biscuit | 曲奇饼干 |
| brownie | —（美式甜点） | 布朗尼蛋糕 |
| It's a deal! | Done! | 一言为定 |

## 关8 · 听对话理解题

2. What is fresh? — a) The cookies　b) The lemonade ✓　c) The brownies　d) The tacos

## 关9 · 情景应答

1. 朋友敲门，请他进来 → Come in!
2. 请客人随意别拘束 → Make yourself at home.
3. 强烈推荐朋友尝你做的披萨 → You must try my pizza!

## 关10 · 本课通关

A. 选择填空
1. ___ down, please.　a) Sit　b) Sits　c) Sitting　d) To sit → **a**
2. ___ some water.　a) Has　b) Have　c) Having　d) To have → **b**
3. You ___ try this cake. It's amazing!　a) is　b) are　c) must　d) am → **c**
4. 请朋友进门时说：　a) Go out!　b) Sit down!　c) Careful!　d) Come in! → **d**

B. 句型转换
1. You sit down.（变祈使句）→ Sit down.
2. Try the cookies.（加 please 表礼貌）→ Try the cookies, please. / Please try the cookies.
3. You try my tacos.（用 must 表强烈推荐）→ You must try my tacos.
