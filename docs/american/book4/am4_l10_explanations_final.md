# AM4 L10 逐题解释(定稿·原创承载·情态推测/there be+定从/without+动名词/As随着/现在分词作结果状语·硅谷) · 供 gen-book4-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: may
表“可能、也许”用情态词 may。✓ The next wave may carry the Valley back. ✗ must(必然)/ need / dare:表“可能”用 may。

### s5 seq2 | ans: can
表“能够”用情态词 can。✓ Many engineers can draw on their home languages. ✗ must / should / ought:表“能够”用 can。

### s5 seq3 | ans: may
表“可能仍会”用 may。✓ Silicon Valley may yet prove a great classroom. ✗ need / dare / used:表“可能”用 may。

### s5 seq4 | ans: that
定语从句先行词 workstations(物),关系词在从句中作主语,用 that。✓ workstations that let engineers make chips. ✗ who(指人)/ what / whose:指物作主语用 that。

### s5 seq5 | ans: enable
“使某人能够做…”=enable sb to do。✓ The new tools enable engineers to test a chip. ✗ prevent 阻止;✗ forbid 禁止;✗ refuse 拒绝——都相反或不对。

### s5 seq6 | ans: design
let sb do:let 后接动词原形(不带 to)。✓ A workstation lets one engineer design a chip. ✗ designs / designing / to design:let 后用原形 design。

### s5 seq7 | ans: being
without 是介词,后接动名词;表被动用 being + 过去分词;中间那个词是 being。✓ without being penalized. ✗ be / been / to be:被动动名词用 being + 过去分词。

### s5 seq8 | ans: saying
without + 动名词;say 的 -ing 是 saying。✓ He left without saying a word. ✗ said / say / to say:介词后用动名词 saying。

### s5 seq9 | ans: costing
without + 动名词;cost 的 -ing 是 costing。✓ improved without costing much money. ✗ cost / costs / to cost:介词后用动名词 costing。

### s5 seq10 | ans: As
表“随着…”用连词 As。✓ As the cost falls, engineers grow bolder. ✗ If(如果)/ Unless(除非)/ Though(尽管):表“随着”用 As。

### s5 seq11 | ans: As
表“随着时间推移”用 As。✓ As time goes on, the Valley reinvents itself. ✗ Because / Before / Whether:表“随着”用 As。

### s5 seq12 | ans: as
表“随着…”用 as。✓ Costs drop as the technology improves. ✗ until / unless / although:表“随着”用 as。

### s5 seq13 | ans: spawning
现在分词作结果状语,主动“催生”。✓ Inventors perfect chips fast, spawning start-ups. ✗ spawned(被动/过去)/ spawn / to spawn:主动结果用 -ing。

### s5 seq14 | ans: giving
现在分词作结果状语,主动“给予”。✓ The new speed helps them, giving the country a lead. ✗ given / give / to give:主动结果用 -ing。

### s5 seq15 | ans: forging
现在分词作结果状语,主动“建立”。✓ They share their skills, forging links. ✗ forged / forge / to forge:主动结果用 -ing。

### s5 seq16 | ans: chip
承载电路的一小片硅是 chip(芯片)。✓ a small piece of silicon holding a circuit is a chip. ✗ brick 砖;✗ coin 硬币;✗ leaf 叶——都不对。

### s5 seq17 | ans: thrives
生意红火、蓬勃发展用 thrive。✓ When a business succeeds, it thrives. ✗ fails 失败;✗ closes 关闭;✗ shrinks 萎缩——都相反。
