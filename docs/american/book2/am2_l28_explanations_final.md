### s5 seq1 | ans: has
现在完成时 = have/has + 过去分词;主语 He 单数用 has。✓ He has just bought a new house.

### s5 seq2 | ans: have
现在完成时主语复数用 have + 过去分词。✓ The signs have not had any effect.

### s5 seq3 | ans: yet
yet 用于否定/疑问句尾,表"还(没)",常配现在完成时。✓ He hasn't found a parking spot yet.

### s5 seq4 | ans: since
since 接过去的时间点,表"自从…以来",配现在完成时。✓ I have not seen him since 1994.

### s5 seq5 | ans: since

### s5 seq6 | ans: for
for 接一段时间,表"持续多久"。✓ Tim has been abroad for three years.

### s5 seq7 | ans: have lived

### s5 seq8 | ans: Up till now
up till now = 至今为止,常配现在完成时。✓ Up till now, he has won five prizes.

### s5 seq9 | ans: so far

### s5 seq10 | ans: ever

### s5 seq11 | ans: just

### s5 seq12 | ans: already

### s5 seq13 | ans: been

### s5 seq14 | ans: gone

### s5 seq15 | ans: who

### s5 seq16 | ans: which

### s5 seq17 | ans: whose

### s5 seq18 | ans: whom

### s6 seq3 | ans: 罕见的,稀有的

### s6 seq4 | ans: 古代的,古老的

### s6 seq5 | ans: 麻烦,难题

### s6 seq6 | ans: 结果,效果
