### u1.01 各时态的被动语态(is/was/will be/has been/had been/is being done)
- Robots first ____ in science fiction novels many years ago.
  **appeared✓** / were appeared / are appeared / had appeared
  考点:各时态的被动语态(is/was/will be/has been/had been/is being done) ｜ 解析:appear 是不及物动词,无被动;主语是动作发出者,用主动一般过去 appeared。
- Right now a new humanoid robot ____ in the laboratory next door.
  is built / **is being built✓** / builds / was built
  考点:各时态的被动语态(is/was/will be/has been/had been/is being done) ｜ 解析:现在此刻正在进行、主语是被造的对象 → 现在进行被动 is being built。
- The whole surface of the earth ____ before the Time Traveller's eyes.
  was changing / changed / **was being changed✓** / is changed
  考点:各时态的被动语态(is/was/will be/has been/had been/is being done) ｜ 解析:过去某时正在发生、地表是被改变的对象 → 过去进行被动 was being changed。
- By the time he stopped the machine, the laboratory ____ completely.
  had rebuilt / was rebuilding / rebuilds / **had been rebuilt✓**
  考点:各时态的被动语态(is/was/will be/has been/had been/is being done) ｜ 解析:在过去某动作之前已完成、实验室是被重建的对象 → 过去完成被动 had been rebuilt。
- Sophia, the AI robot, ____ many times by reporters around the world.
  **has been interviewed✓** / has interviewed / interviews / is interviewing
  考点:各时态的被动语态(is/was/will be/has been/had been/is being done) ｜ 解析:对现在有影响且主语是被采访者 → 现在完成被动 has been interviewed。
- The first Time Machine ____ at ten o'clock that very morning.
  started / **was started✓** / has started / is started
  考点:各时态的被动语态(is/was/will be/has been/had been/is being done) ｜ 解析:过去明确时间、机器是被启动的对象 → 一般过去被动 was started。

  考点:各时态的被动语态(is/was/will be/has been/had been/is being done) ｜ 解析:将来、场馆是被建造的对象 → 一般将来被动 will be built。
- When I entered the room, I found the salesman's salary ____ at the meeting.
  was discussing / **was being discussed✓** / discussed / has been discussed
  考点:各时态的被动语态(is/was/will be/has been/had been/is being done) ｜ 解析:进入房间那一刻正在进行、薪水是被讨论的对象 → 过去进行被动 was being discussed。

  考点:各时态的被动语态(is/was/will be/has been/had been/is being done) ｜ 解析:一般现在的普遍做法、任务是被交付的对象 → 一般现在被动 are given。
