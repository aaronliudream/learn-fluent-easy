Shikumen and Lilong Life
Homes have changed over time. Today, most of us live in high-rise, self-contained flats, 
while decades ago, connected residential blocks with an intimate neighbourhood were 
more common. Lilong, a neighbourhood of lanes with shikumen houses, is a historical type 
of housing that was commonly found in Shanghai. At the height of their popularity in the 
1930s, shikumen-style buildings housed as much as 80% of Shanghai’s total population. 
Today, this proportion is much lower, as many Shanghainese families have been eager to 
move to newer and more spacious homes, especially in the 1990s. The elderly residents, 
who are used to the intimacy and conveniences of their neighbourhoods, have a more 
difficult time leaving lilong life behind.
