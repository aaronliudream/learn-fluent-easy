### s5 seq1 | ans: had
过去完成时 had + 过去分词,表比“他来”更早的过去;助动词用 had(不分单复数)。✓ The raiders had overrun his coast. ✗ have / has(过去完成用 had)/ was:助动词用 had。

### s5 seq2 | ans: had
过去完成时 had + 过去分词。✓ the invaders had already surrendered. ✗ have / did / were:过去完成助动词用 had。

### s5 seq3 | ans: grown
过去完成 had + 过去分词;grow 的过去分词是 grown。✓ discipline had grown slack. ✗ grew(过去式)/ grow(原形)/ growing:had 后用过去分词 grown。

### s5 seq4 | ans: Dressed
过去分词短语作状语,表被动“被装扮成”;dress 的过去分词 Dressed。✓ Dressed as a minstrel, the king entered. ✗ Dressing(主动)/ Dress / To dress:被动伴随用过去分词。

### s5 seq5 | ans: Faced
过去分词短语作状语,‘面对…’(be faced with);face 的过去分词 Faced。✓ Faced with the advance, he chose not to fight. ✗ Facing / Face / To face:此处用过去分词 Faced。

### s5 seq6 | ans: compared
过去分词短语 compared with,‘与…相比’。✓ tiny compared with the enemy host. ✗ comparing / compare / compares:固定用过去分词 compared with。

### s5 seq7 | ans: Living
现在分词短语作状语,表主动“正靠…生活”;live 的 -ing 是 Living。✓ Living well on stolen food, the raiders grew careless. ✗ Lived / Live / To live:主动伴随用现在分词。

### s5 seq8 | ans: harrying
现在分词短语作状语,表主动“骚扰”。✓ He kept his men moving, harrying the enemy. ✗ harried(被动/过去)/ harry / to harry:主动伴随用 -ing。

### s5 seq9 | ans: noting
现在分词短语作状语,表主动“注意到”。✓ He slipped in, noting every weakness. ✗ noted / note / to note:主动伴随用 -ing。

### s5 seq10 | ans: before
时间连词 before,‘在…之前’(引申“…才…”)。✓ He stayed a week before he returned. ✗ since / unless / though:表“之前”用 before。

### s5 seq11 | ans: While
时间连词 While,‘当…的时候’,引导正在进行的背景。✓ While his army was gathering, the king went spying. ✗ Before / Unless / Although:表“当…时”用 While。

### s5 seq12 | ans: until
时间连词 until,‘直到…(才)’。✓ He did not attack until his men were ready. ✗ since / although / whether:表“直到”用 until。

### s5 seq13 | ans: soft
make + 名词 + 形容词,形容词作补足语;此处填形容词 soft。✓ had made them soft. ✗ softly(副词)/ softness(名词)/ soften(动词):补足语用形容词 soft。

### s5 seq14 | ans: guarded
find + 名词 + 过去分词,表“发现…被(守卫)”;guard 的过去分词 guarded。✓ found the camp poorly guarded. ✗ guarding / guard / guards:被动补足语用过去分词。

### s5 seq15 | ans: hungry
keep + 名词 + 形容词;填形容词 hungry。✓ kept the enemy hungry and tired. ✗ hunger(名词)/ hungrily(副词)/ hungered(动词):补足语用形容词。

### s5 seq16 | ans: minstrel

### s5 seq17 | ans: espionage

### s5 seq18 | ans: prolonged
持续很久的是 prolonged(持久的)。✓ a prolonged campaign. ✗ brief 简短;✗ sudden 突然;✗ tiny 微小——都不对。
