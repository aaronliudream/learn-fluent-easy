### u3.01 陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换)
- He said, "I recycle paper every week." → He said that he ____ paper every week.
  **recycled✓** / recycle / recycles / will recycle
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:主句 said 过去时,从句一般现在 recycle 后移为一般过去 recycled;人称 I→he。
- She said, "The air is getting cleaner." → She said that the air ____ getting cleaner.
  is / **was✓** / were / be
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:现在进行 is getting 后移为过去进行 was getting。
- The expert said, "Polar bears will die out." → The expert said that polar bears ____ die out.
  will / shall / **would✓** / are going to
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:will 后移为 would。
- Tom said, "I have planted ten trees." → Tom said that he ____ ten trees.
  has planted / planted / plants / **had planted✓**
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:现在完成 have planted 后移为过去完成 had planted。
- She said, "We can save a lot of water." → She said that they ____ save a lot of water.
  **could✓** / can / may / must
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:can 后移为 could;人称 we→they。
- He told me, "The factory released waste gas." → He told me that the factory ____ released waste gas.
  has / **had✓** / have / will have
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:一般过去 released 后移为过去完成 had released。
- Lily said, "I am cutting my carbon footprint." → Lily said that she ____ cutting her carbon footprint.
  is / are / **was✓** / be
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:现在进行 am cutting 后移为过去进行 was cutting;人称 I→she。
- They said, "The river is badly polluted." → They said that the river ____ badly polluted.
  is / be / were / **was✓**
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:is 后移为 was;主语 the river 单数。
- He said, "I don't waste electricity at home." → He said that he ____ waste electricity at home.
  **didn't✓** / doesn't / don't / won't
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:一般现在否定 don't 后移为一般过去 didn't。
- She said, "Smog has become a serious problem." → She said that smog ____ become a serious problem.
  has / **had✓** / have / will have
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:现在完成 has become 后移为过去完成 had become。
- Ben said, "We are going to plant more trees." → Ben said that they ____ going to plant more trees.
  are / is / **were✓** / be
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:be going to 中的 are 后移为 were;人称 we→they。
- The girl said, "I will turn off the lights." → The girl said that she ____ turn off the lights.
  will / shall / is going to / **would✓**
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:will 后移为 would;人称 I→she。
- He said, "People throw too much garbage away." → He said that people ____ too much garbage away.
  **threw✓** / throw / throws / have thrown
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:一般现在 throw 后移为一般过去 threw。
- Mum said, "The ice is melting fast." → Mum said that the ice ____ melting fast.
  is / **was✓** / were / be
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:is melting 后移为 was melting。
- She said, "I have never used a plastic bag." → She said that she ____ never used a plastic bag.
  has / have / **had✓** / did
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:现在完成 have used 后移为过去完成 had used。
- He said, "Clean energy can sustain our future." → He said that clean energy ____ sustain their future.
  can / may / should / **could✓**
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:can 后移为 could。
- The reporter said, "The smog killed many people." → The reporter said that the smog ____ killed many people.
  **had✓** / has / have / will have
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:一般过去 killed 后移为过去完成 had killed。
- Sara said, "I am saving water at home." → Sara said that she ____ saving water at home.
  is / **was✓** / were / are
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:am saving 后移为 was saving;人称 I→she。

  don't / doesn't / **didn't✓** / won't
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:一般现在否定 don't 后移为一般过去 didn't。
- He said, "The trees will protect the soil." → He said that the trees ____ protect the soil.
  will / shall / are going to / **would✓**
  考点:陈述句的间接引语(say/tell + that;时态后移、人称变换) ｜ 解析:will 后移为 would。

### u3.02 疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序)
- She asked, "Do you save water at home?" → She asked ____ I saved water at home.
  **if✓** / that / what / do
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:一般疑问句转述用 if/whether 引导,并变陈述语序。
- He asked, "What can we do to help?" → He asked what we ____ do to help.
  can / **could✓** / may / should
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:特殊疑问句保留 what,用陈述语序,can 后移为 could。
- The reporter asked, "Will polar bears die out?" → The reporter asked ____ polar bears would die out.
  that / what / **whether✓** / why
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:一般疑问句用 whether 引导;will 后移为 would。
- She asked, "Why is smog so harmful?" → She asked why smog ____ so harmful.
  is / be / were / **was✓**
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:保留 why,陈述语序,is 后移为 was。
- He asked, "How do you cut carbon emissions?" → He asked how I ____ carbon emissions.
  **cut✓** / cuts / did cut / cutting
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:陈述语序去掉助动词 do,cut 后移仍为 cut(原形=过去式)。
- They asked, "Are the rivers getting cleaner?" → They asked ____ the rivers were getting cleaner.
  that / **whether✓** / what / how
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:一般疑问句用 whether 引导,变陈述语序。
- She asked, "Where does the smog come from?" → She asked where the smog ____ from.
  come / comes / **came✓** / coming
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:陈述语序、去 does,comes 后移为 came。
- He asked, "Can we still save the forest?" → He asked ____ we could still save the forest.
  that / what / why / **if✓**
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:一般疑问句用 if 引导;can 后移为 could。
- The teacher asked, "What causes climate change?" → The teacher asked what ____ climate change.
  **caused✓** / cause / causes / did cause
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:what 作主语,causes 后移为 caused。
- She asked, "Have you joined the clean-up?" → She asked ____ I had joined the clean-up.
  that / **whether✓** / what / how
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:一般疑问句用 whether 引导;have joined 后移为 had joined。
- He asked, "When will the new policy start?" → He asked when the new policy ____ start.
  will / shall / **would✓** / is going to
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:保留 when,陈述语序,will 后移为 would。
- They asked, "Why did the factory close?" → They asked why the factory ____ closed.
  has / have / did / **had✓**
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:保留 why、陈述语序;一般过去 did close 后移为 had closed。
- She asked, "Do the villagers recycle waste?" → She asked if the villagers ____ waste.
  **recycled✓** / recycle / recycles / recycling
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:if 引导、陈述语序、去 do;recycle 后移为 recycled。
- He asked, "How much water do we waste?" → He asked how much water we ____.
  waste / **wasted✓** / wastes / wasting
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:陈述语序去 do;waste 后移为 wasted。
- The expert asked, "Is nuclear power safe?" → The expert asked ____ nuclear power was safe.
  that / what / **whether✓** / why
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:一般疑问句用 whether 引导;is 后移为 was。
- She asked, "What are you doing for the planet?" → She asked what I ____ doing for the planet.
  am / is / were / **was✓**
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:陈述语序;am doing 后移为 was doing(I→第三人称由说话人决定,这里转述者用 was)。
- He asked, "Can the river be cleaned up?" → He asked ____ the river could be cleaned up.
  **whether✓** / that / what / how
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:一般疑问句用 whether 引导;can 后移为 could。
- They asked, "How has the air improved?" → They asked how the air ____ improved.
  has / **had✓** / have / did
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:保留 how;现在完成 has improved 后移为过去完成 had improved。
- She asked, "Do we need stricter rules?" → She asked if we ____ stricter rules.
  need / needs / **needed✓** / needing
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:if 引导、陈述语序、去 do;need 后移为 needed。
- He asked, "Which energy is cleaner?" → He asked which energy ____ cleaner.
  is / be / were / **was✓**
  考点:疑问句的间接引语(一般疑问→if/whether;特殊疑问→wh-词;一律陈述语序) ｜ 解析:保留 which,陈述语序,is 后移为 was。

### u3.03 祈使句的间接引语(tell/ask/order/advise sb (not) to do)+ 时间/地点状语呼应
- "Turn off the lights, please," she said. → She told us ____ off the lights.
  **to turn✓** / turn / turning / turned
  考点:祈使句的间接引语(tell/ask/order/advise sb (not) to do)+ 时间/地点状语呼应 ｜ 解析:祈使句转述用 tell sb to do。
- "Don't throw garbage into the river," he said. → He told the tourists ____ throw garbage into the river.
  to not / **not to✓** / don't / not
  考点:祈使句的间接引语(tell/ask/order/advise sb (not) to do)+ 时间/地点状语呼应 ｜ 解析:否定祈使句用 tell sb not to do。
- "Please save water," the teacher said. → The teacher asked us ____ water.
  save / saving / **to save✓** / saved
  考点:祈使句的间接引语(tell/ask/order/advise sb (not) to do)+ 时间/地点状语呼应 ｜ 解析:ask sb to do 转述请求型祈使句。
- "Let's work together to stop pollution," the expert said. → The expert advised us ____ together to stop pollution.
  work / working / worked / **to work✓**
  考点:祈使句的间接引语(tell/ask/order/advise sb (not) to do)+ 时间/地点状语呼应 ｜ 解析:advise sb to do 转述建议型祈使句。
- "Stop burning coal now," the officer said. → The officer ordered them to stop burning coal ____.
  **then✓** / now / today / here
  考点:祈使句的间接引语(tell/ask/order/advise sb (not) to do)+ 时间/地点状语呼应 ｜ 解析:时间状语呼应:now→then。
- "Plant more trees here," she said. → She told us to plant more trees ____.
  here / **there✓** / this / now
