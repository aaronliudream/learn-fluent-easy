# 外研社七上 U5 语法(一般将来时)— 待审 v1

> code=`wy7-u5-simple-future` · junior_fltrp/wy7A/U5/grade7 · 20题 · 答案分布[5, 5, 5, 5] · 难度{1: 9, 2: 8, 3: 3}

**summary**:一般将来时表示将要发生的动作,常与 tomorrow、next week、soon 连用。两种结构:will + 动词原形;be(am/is/are) going to + 动词原形。否定:will not = won't;be not going to。一般疑问把 will 或 be 提到句首。There will be… 表示“将会有”。

**examples**:I will visit my grandma tomorrow. / She is going to buy a bag. / Will you help me?

## 题目

**1. [d1]** I ____ visit my grandma tomorrow.
  - A)am  B)was  C)do  D)will  →**D**
  - 解析:will + 动词原形表将来。
**2. [d1]** She will ____ us next week.
  - A)helps  B)helping  C)help  D)helped  →**C**
  - 解析:will 后接动词原形。
**3. [d1]** They ____ going to have a party.
  - A)are  B)do  C)will  D)is  →**A**
  - 解析:be going to 结构中主语为复数用 are。
**4. [d1]** It ____ rain this afternoon.
  - A)was  B)will  C)does  D)is  →**B**
  - 解析:will 表将来,后接原形 rain。
**5. [d1]** We are going to ____ a film tonight.
  - A)watches  B)watching  C)watched  D)watch  →**D**
  - 解析:be going to 后接动词原形。
**6. [d1]** He ____ be twelve next year.
  - A)is  B)does  C)will  D)was  →**C**
  - 解析:next year 表将来,用 will be。
**7. [d1]** There ____ be a sports meeting tomorrow.
  - A)will  B)is  C)does  D)are  →**A**
  - 解析:There will be 表“将会有”。
**8. [d1]** My friends ____ going to visit Beijing.
  - A)be  B)are  C)is  D)will  →**B**
  - 解析:be going to 中复数主语用 are。
**9. [d1]** You ____ see him soon.
  - A)were  B)do  C)are  D)will  →**D**
  - 解析:soon 表将来,用 will。
**10. [d2]** ____ you help me with my English?
  - A)Will  B)Are  C)Do  D)Is  →**A**
  - 解析:will 的一般疑问把 will 提前。
**11. [d2]** He ____ come to the party because he is ill.
  - A)doesn't  B)won't  C)wasn't  D)isn't  →**B**
  - 解析:won't = will not,表将来否定。
**12. [d2]** — Will it be sunny tomorrow? — Yes, it ____.
  - A)is  B)does  C)will  D)be  →**C**
  - 解析:will 疑问的肯定简答用 will。
**13. [d2]** ____ she going to buy a new bag?
  - A)Will  B)Does  C)Are  D)Is  →**D**
  - 解析:be going to 的疑问用 be,单数用 Is。
**14. [d2]** We ____ not going to stay at home this weekend.
  - A)are  B)is  C)will  D)do  →**A**
  - 解析:be going to 否定在 be 后加 not。
**15. [d2]** — Will they arrive soon? — No, they ____.
  - A)willn't  B)won't  C)aren't  D)don't  →**B**
  - 解析:will 疑问的否定简答用 won't;无 willn't。
**16. [d2]** There ____ be any classes next Sunday.
  - A)aren't  B)isn't  C)won't  D)doesn't  →**C**
  - 解析:There will be 的否定用 won't be。
**17. [d2]** What ____ you do this weekend?
  - A)is  B)are  C)do  D)will  →**D**
  - 解析:询问将来计划用 will。
**18. [d3]** My sister ____ go to school tomorrow because she ____ visit the doctor.
  - A)won't; will  B)isn't; will  C)willn't; will  D)won't; wills  →**A**
  - 解析:将来否定 won't + 原形;肯定 will + 原形。
**19. [d3]** — What are you going to do next Sunday? — I ____ going to ____ my uncle.
  - A)am; visiting  B)am; visit  C)am; visits  D)will; visit  →**B**
  - 解析:be going to 中主语 I 用 am,后接原形 visit。
**20. [d3]** Look at the clouds. It ____ rain soon, so I ____ take an umbrella.
  - A)will; am  B)is; will  C)will; will  D)won't; will  →**C**
  - 解析:两处都表将来,用 will + 原形。
