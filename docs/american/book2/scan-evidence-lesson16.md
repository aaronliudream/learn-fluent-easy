## 一、教材(新概念英语第二册 · WeLib 443pg)—— 定主考

- **PDF p75(印72)**:`Lesson 16 A polite request 彬彬有礼的要求` — First listen…(What was the polite request?)+ 课文。核心(**满篇 if 条件句 + 一处主谓一致**):
  - **If you park** your car in the wrong place, a traffic policeman **will** soon find it.(if+现在时,主句 will)
  - You **will be** very lucky **if he lets** you go without a ticket.(条件句后置)
  - However, this **does not** always happen. **Traffic police are** sometimes very polite.(**主谓一致**:police 集合名词+复数动词 are)
  - During a holiday in Sweden, I **found** this note: 'Sir, we **welcome** you to our city. This **is** a "No Parking" area. You **will enjoy** your stay here **if you pay** attention to our street signs. This note **is** only a reminder.'
  - **If you receive** a request like this, you **cannot fail to obey** it!(if+现在时,主句 cannot)
  - New words(官方9):park(l.1)/pɑːk/、traffic(l.1)/ˈtræfɪk/、ticket(l.3)/ˈtɪkɪt/、note(l.5)/nəʊt/、area(l.6)/ˈeəriə/、sign(l.7)/saɪn/、reminder(l.7)/rɪˈmaɪndə/、fail(l.8)/feɪl/、obey(l.8)/əˈbeɪ/。
  - **Notes**:① if he lets you go without a ticket(let sb go=放开);② **traffic police,police 是集合名词,后面用复数动词**(=主谓一致);③ "No Parking" area;④ street signs=交通标志。
- **PDF p76(印73)**:`Key structures 关键句型 · **If you open the door you will get a surprise. 条件句**`(**主考权威页**):
  - **第一类真实条件句:if 从句用一般现在时,主句用将来时(will)**,用来询问或谈论十分可能的事情:
    - If he is out, I'll call tomorrow. · If it rains tomorrow, we'll stay at home. · You'll miss the train if you don't hurry. · If you see him, will you tell him?(条件+疑问)· If he is working, I won't disturb him.(主句否定 won't)· If I have time, I'll be writing to him.· He will come if he can.· If they can help you they will.
    - **(b)条件句 + 祈使句**:If you make a mistake, correct it. · If you don't like the food, don't eat it. · Please don't disturb him if he is busy.
  - Exercise A:How many times has 'if' been used in the passage?(数条件句)。
  - **裁决**:教材 Key structures 明标条件句 → **本课主考=if 条件句**(第一类真实条件:if + 一般现在时,主句 will/won't/祈使句/can;条件句可前可后;**从句用现在时不用 will**)。

## 二、语法同步(新概念第二册语法同步 · 356pg)—— 定辅考(岔开主题=主谓一致)

- **PDF p103(印102)**:`Lesson 16 A polite request` 章,专题=**主谓一致**(**与教材主题分歧**):

  - (二)复数形式的名词作主语与谓语的关系:成对概念(pants/glasses/scissors 用复数 are)/形式复数意义单数(politics/statistics is)/时间重量数额看成整体(Ten years is / Two tons is)/数词单用表人物用复数(Ten were killed / Few know)。
