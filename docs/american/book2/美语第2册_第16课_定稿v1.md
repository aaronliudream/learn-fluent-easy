## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,if 条件句密集 + 主谓一致)

> If you park in the wrong parking lot, a police officer will find your car fast. You will be lucky if the officer lets you off without a ticket. However, that does not always happen. Traffic police are sometimes surprisingly polite. On a trip downtown in Sweden, I found this friendly note on my car: "Welcome to our city! This is a No Parking area. You will enjoy your stay here if you pay attention to our street signs. We have your license plate, but this note is only a reminder." If you get a polite note like this, you cannot fail to obey it. A kind reminder often works better than an angry ticket. If you ever visit this city, you will surely feel welcome.
