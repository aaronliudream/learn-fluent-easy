### s5 seq1 | ans: that
定语从句,先行词 things 指物,that 在从句中作主语。✓ things that happened thousands of years ago. ✗ who(指人)/ what(不引导定从)/ whose(所属):指物用 that。

### s5 seq2 | ans: who
定语从句,先行词 Anthropologists 指人,who 作从句主语。✓ Anthropologists who wondered… ✗ which(指物)/ whom(作宾语)/ what:指人作主语用 who。

### s5 seq3 | ans: that
定语从句修饰 the only way,关系词用 that(表方式的先行词常用 that)。✓ the only way that they could keep history. ✗ what / who / whose:此处用 that。

### s5 seq4 | ans: handed
过去分词作定语,表被动“被传下来”,修饰 legends。✓ legends handed down from one generation to the next. ✗ handing(主动)/ hand / to hand:表被动用过去分词 handed。

### s5 seq5 | ans: buried
过去分词作定语,表被动“被埋”,修饰 bone。✓ a bone buried in rock. ✗ burying(主动)/ bury / to bury:表被动用过去分词 buried。

### s5 seq6 | ans: worked
过去分词作定语,“被加工过的”,修饰 flints。✓ a few worked flints. ✗ working(主动)/ work / to work:表被动/完成用过去分词 worked。

### s5 seq7 | ans: where
疑问副词 where 引导从句,“从哪里来”;用陈述语序。✓ wondered where the ancestors came from. ✗ which / that / what:问“哪里”用 where。

### s5 seq8 | ans: where
疑问副词 where 引导从句,“住在哪里”。✓ find out where the earliest humans lived. ✗ whose / that / so:问地点用 where。

### s5 seq9 | ans: what
疑问代词 what 引导从句,作 did 的宾语,“做了什么”。✓ write down what those people did. ✗ which / whom / whose:问“什么”用 what。

### s5 seq10 | ans: may
may + have + 过去分词,对过去作推测“可能…过”。✓ Early people may have used wood and skins. ✗ will / shall / need:表推测用 may。

### s5 seq11 | ans: have
现在完成时 have + 过去分词,主语 tools(复数)用 have。✓ The wooden tools have rotted away. ✗ has(单数)/ had(过去完成)/ having:复数用 have。

### s5 seq12 | ans: have
现在完成时 have + 过去分词,主语 tools 复数用 have。✓ The stone tools have remained. ✗ has / was / were:现在完成用 have + 过去分词。

### s5 seq13 | ans: easier
比较级“更容易”:easy → easier(与 than 呼应)。✓ easier to shape than most other kinds. ✗ easy(原级)/ easiest(最高级)/ more easy(不合规则):双音节以 y 结尾变 easier。

### s5 seq14 | ans: neither
neither … nor …,“既不…也不…”;与 nor 呼应的前一个词是 neither。✓ neither history nor legend. ✗ either / both / not:与 nor 搭配用 neither。

### s5 seq15 | ans: longer
比较级“更久”:long → longer(与 than 呼应)。✓ Stone lasts longer than wood or skin. ✗ long(原级)/ longest(最高级)/ more long(不合规则):单音节加 -er。
