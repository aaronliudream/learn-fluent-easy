## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,全一般过去时)

> Postcards always spoil my vacations. Last summer I went to Italy. I visited a lot of museums and sat in public parks. The museums were free, and the weather was warm. A friendly waiter taught me a few words of Italian, and then he lent me a book. I read a few lines, but I didn't understand a word. Every day I thought about the postcards, but I didn't send any. My vacation passed quickly. On the last day I made a big decision. I got up early and bought thirty-seven postcards. I spent the whole day in my room, but I didn't write a single card!
