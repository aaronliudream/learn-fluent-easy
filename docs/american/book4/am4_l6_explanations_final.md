# AM4 L6 逐题解释(定稿·原创承载·宾语从句/虚拟条件/状语连词+被动/定语从句/not…but·体育的精神) · 供 gen-book4-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: that
宾语从句由 that 引导,接在 hear it said 之后。✓ hear it said that sport builds goodwill. ✗ which / what / whom:引导宾语从句用 that。

### s5 seq2 | ans: that
宾语从句由 that 引导,接在 believe 之后。✓ believe that sport brings nations together. ✗ what / which / whose:此处用 that。

### s5 seq3 | ans: when
when 引导时间从句,“每当…时”。✓ amazed when I hear such claims. ✗ which / that / what:表“当…时”用 when。

### s5 seq4 | ans: could
与现在相反的虚拟,if only + 情态词过去式 could。✓ If only people could meet… ✗ can(非虚拟)/ will / may:虚拟里用 could。

### s5 seq5 | ans: would
与现在相反的虚拟,主句用 would + 动词原形。✓ serious sport would be a poor choice. ✗ will(非虚拟)/ can / must:主句用 would。

### s5 seq6 | ans: had
与现在相反的虚拟,even if 从句用过去式;have → had。✓ Even if we had no examples… ✗ have / has / will have:虚拟用过去式 had。

### s5 seq7 | ans: unless
unless=“除非”,引导否定条件从句。✓ a game means little unless you do your utmost. ✗ because(因为)/ although(虽然)/ since(既然)——都不表“除非”。

### s5 seq8 | ans: are
被动结构 be + 过去分词;主语 instincts 复数,用 are。✓ savage instincts are aroused. ✗ is(单数)/ have / has:复数被动用 are。

### s5 seq9 | ans: picked
一般现在时的被动 are + 过去分词。✓ Sides are picked at random. ✗ pick / picking / picks:are 后用过去分词 picked。

### s5 seq10 | ans: who
定语从句,先行词 Anyone 指人,who 作从句主语。✓ Anyone who has played… ✗ which(指物)/ what / whose:指人作主语用 who。

### s5 seq11 | ans: that
定语从句,先行词 nations 指人(集体),用 that。✓ the nations that work themselves into a fury. ✗ what / whose / whom:此处用 that。

### s5 seq12 | ans: where
定语从句,先行词 green 指地点,用关系副词 where。✓ a green where no patriotism is involved. ✗ which / that(此处需关系副词)/ what:指地点用 where。

### s5 seq13 | ans: not
not … but …,“不是…而是…”;前半用 not。✓ not the players' behavior but the spectators' attitude. ✗ no / none / never:此结构前半用 not。

### s5 seq14 | ans: utmost
do one's utmost,“竭尽全力”;固定用名词 utmost。✓ do your utmost to win. ✗ best(应为 do one's best)/ most / hardest:此固定搭配用 utmost。

### s5 seq15 | ans: themselves
work oneself into,“使自己陷入…”;主语 nations 复数,反身代词用 themselves。✓ work themselves into a fury. ✗ them / their / they:反身代词用 themselves。

### s5 seq16 | ans: goodwill
友好、乐于助人的心意是 goodwill。✓ sport builds goodwill. ✗ anger 愤怒;✗ fear 恐惧;✗ greed 贪婪——都不对。

### s5 seq17 | ans: competitive
渴望竞争取胜的是 competitive。✓ fiercely competitive. ✗ lazy 懒;✗ gentle 温和;✗ calm 平静——都不对。

### s5 seq18 | ans: absurd

### s9 seq1 | ans: If only people could meet on the field, they might not fight.
