### s5 seq1 | ans: of
consist of + 动名词,‘由…构成/在于…’;固定介词 of。✓ consists of setting the animal to work. ✗ in(consist in 义不同)/ on / at:‘由…组成’用 consist of。

### s5 seq2 | ans: building
consist of + 动名词;填 build 的动名词 building。✓ consists of building a good relationship. ✗ build(原形)/ built / to build:of 后用动名词。

### s5 seq3 | ans: showing

### s5 seq4 | ans: of

### s5 seq5 | ans: of

### s5 seq6 | ans: recognizing

### s5 seq7 | ans: of

### s5 seq8 | ans: of

### s5 seq10 | ans: every time
‘每当…就…’引导时间从句用 every time。✓ plunges every time a man approaches. ✗ in case 以防 / as if 好像 / even though 即使:‘每当’用 every time。

### s5 seq13 | ans: for

### s5 seq14 | ans: for

### s5 seq15 | ans: to
it is + 形容词 + for sb + 不定式;填不定式记号 to。✓ It is important for the trainer to win its trust. ✗ for / that / of:逻辑主语后接 to do。

### s5 seq16 | ans: assign
把任务或职责交给某人是 assign(分配、指派)。✓ to give a duty to someone is to assign it. ✗ resign 辞职;✗ design 设计;✗ consign 托运——都不对。

### s5 seq17 | ans: resentful
因不公待遇而痛苦愤怒是 resentful(愤懑的、充满怨恨的)。✓ feeling bitter and angry makes one resentful. ✗ respectful 恭敬的;✗ resourceful 足智多谋的;✗ restful 宁静的——都不对。

### s5 seq18 | ans: reinforce
使某种感受或信念更强烈是 reinforce(强化、加强)。✓ to make a belief stronger is to reinforce it. ✗ reproduce 复制;✗ replace 取代;✗ remove 移除——都不对。

### s9 seq3 | ans: Every time a person approaches, the captive plunges and screams.
