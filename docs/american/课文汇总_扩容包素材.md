### am1_l53  (Lesson 53  You write well!)

【关1·课文学习】
## 关1 · 课文学习

**前置听力问题**：Listen and answer: What should "He runs quick" be?
（a) He runs quicker　b) He runs quickly ✓　c) He quickly runs good）

| # | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ERIN: Okay, Jamal. I read your essay. It's really good! | 艾琳：好了，贾马尔。我读完你的作文了。写得真不错！ |
| 2 | JAMAL: Really? Be honest. Grade it hard. | 贾马尔：真的？说实话，往狠了批。 |
| 3 | ERIN: Okay... here: "He runs quick." That needs an L-Y: "quickly." | 艾琳：好……这里："He runs quick."，得加 -ly："quickly"。 |
| 4 | JAMAL: Ah, right. Adverb. | 贾马尔：啊对，副词。 |
| 5 | ERIN: Same here: "She sings beautiful." → "She sings beautifully." | 艾琳：这儿也一样："She sings beautiful" 要改成 "beautifully"。 |
| 6 | JAMAL: Got it. Adjectives describe things, adverbs describe actions. | 贾马尔：明白了。形容词描述事物，副词描述动作。 |
| 7 | ERIN: Exactly! But careful—some words don't change. "He works hard" is correct. | 艾琳：完全正确！不过小心——有些词不变形。"He works hard" 就是对的。 |
| 8 | JAMAL: Not "hardly"? | 贾马尔：不是 "hardly"？ |
| 9 | ERIN: No! "Hardly" means "almost not." He hardly works = he's lazy! | 艾琳：不是！"hardly" 意思是"几乎不"。He hardly works = 他很懒！ |
| 10 | JAMAL: Whoa. English is tricky. | 贾马尔：哇哦。英语真难缠。 |
| 11 | ERIN: A little. But your story is great. You write well! | 艾琳：有一点。不过你的故事很棒，你写得真好！ |
| 12 | JAMAL: Thanks! You explain things clearly. | 贾马尔：谢谢！你讲得也特别清楚。 |

【关7·对话填空(既有)】

| 2 | a) beautiful　b) beauty　c) beautifully　d) beautify | c |
