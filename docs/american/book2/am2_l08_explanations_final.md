# AM2 L8 逐题解释(定稿) · 供 gen-book2-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: larger
large 是短词,比较级直接在词尾加 -r(已有 e)→ larger,后面接 than。✓ Bill's garden is larger than Joe's.

### s5 seq2 | ans: the tallest
tall 是短词,最高级加 -est,前面加 the → the tallest。✓ She is the tallest girl in the class.

### s5 seq3 | ans: bigger
big 是"一个元音+一个辅音"结尾,比较级要双写 g 再加 -er → bigger。✓ 别写成 biger。

### s5 seq4 | ans: nicest
nice 以 e 结尾,最高级直接加 -st → nicest。✓ 别写成 niceest。

### s5 seq5 | ans: happier
happy 以"辅音+y"结尾,比较级把 y 变 i 再加 -er → happier。✓ 别写成 happyer。

### s5 seq6 | ans: more interesting
interesting 有两个以上音节(长词),比较级在前面加 more → more interesting(不加 -er)。✓

### s5 seq7 | ans: 前面加 more / most(不加 -er/-est)
两个以上音节的长词(interesting / expensive / beautiful),比较级前加 more,最高级前加 most,不再加 -er/-est。

### s5 seq8 | ans: better / best
good 是不规则的,比较级 better,最高级 best,要单独记(不是 gooder/goodest)。

### s5 seq9 | ans: worst
bad 是不规则的,比较级 worse,最高级 worst。✓ the worst garden。

### s5 seq10 | ans: 是不规则的,要单独记
good→better→best、bad→worse→worst、many/much→more→most 都是不规则变化,要单独记住,不能套 -er/-est 或 more/most 的规则。

### s5 seq11 | ans: harder than
hard 的比较级 harder(副词也这样比),"比乔"用 than。✓ Bill works harder than Joe.

### s5 seq12 | ans: My garden is bigger than yours.
big→bigger(双写),"比你的"用 than。✓ My garden is bigger than yours.

### s5 seq13 | ans: in
"在……(范围)中最……"用 the + 最高级 + in。✓ the most beautiful garden in our town.

### s5 seq14 | ans: Mary's dress is the most expensive.
expensive 是长词,最高级 = the most expensive。✓ Mary's dress is the most expensive.

### s5 seq15 | ans: more
"更多的花"用 more + 名词(more flowers),后面接 than。✓ Bill grows more flowers than Joe.

### s5 seq16 | ans: 更多的(修饰名词)
more flowers 里的 more = 更多的,直接修饰名词 flowers,表示数量更多。

### s5 seq17 | ans: 报名参加比赛
enter for the competition = 报名参加比赛。✓ Everyone enters for the competition.

### s5 seq18 | ans: 辛苦的活儿,重活
hard work = 辛苦的活儿、重体力活。✓ I do not like hard work.

### s6 seq1 | ans: favorite
美语"最喜欢的"拼 favorite(-or 结尾);英式拼 favourite。✓ my favorite garden.

### s6 seq2 | ans: color
美语"颜色"拼 color;英式拼 colour。✓ colorful vegetables.

### s6 seq3 | ans: meter
美语"米(长度)"拼 meter;英式拼 metre。✓ ten meters wide.

### s6 seq4 | ans: 报名参加
enter for = 报名参加。✓ enter for the competition.
顺带记:退出 = drop out / withdraw;赢得 = win;观看 = watch.

### s6 seq5 | ans: 赢得奖品
win a prize = 赢得奖品。✓ Joe wins a prize every year.
顺带记:交报名费 = pay the entry fee;输掉比赛 = lose the game;颁发奖品 = give out a prize.

### s6 seq6 | ans: 辛苦的活儿
hard work = 辛苦的活儿、重活。✓ I do not like hard work.
顺带记:简单的事 = an easy job;开心的事 = a happy thing;一点点活 = a little work.

### s7 seq1 | ans: most beautiful
beautiful 是长词,最高级 = the most beautiful。✓ Joe has the most beautiful garden.
✗ beautifulest:beautiful 是长词,最高级用 most beautiful,不加 -est;✗ more beautiful:这里是最高级(in town),不是比较级;✗ most beautifulest:most 和 -est 不能重复叠加.

### s7 seq2 | ans: bigger
big→bigger(双写 g),后面接 than。✓ Bill's garden is bigger than Joe's.
✗ biggest:后面有 than,该用比较级 bigger,不用最高级;✗ more big:big 是短词,比较级双写加 -er,不用 more;✗ biger:拼写错,big 要双写成 bigger.

### s7 seq3 | ans: harder
hard 的比较级 harder。✓ Bill works harder.
✗ hardest:这里是"比…更卖力",用比较级 harder;✗ more hard:hard 是短词,比较级加 -er,不用 more;✗ harderer:拼写错,没有这种叠加.

### s7 seq4 | ans: worst
bad 不规则,最高级 worst。✓ the worst garden.
✗ baddest:bad 的最高级是不规则的 worst,不是 baddest;✗ most bad:bad 最高级用 worst,不用 most bad;✗ worse:有 the + in town,该用最高级 worst,不用比较级 worse.

### s9 seq1 | ans: This book is more interesting than that one.

### s9 seq3 | ans: My grades are better than last time.
good 不规则,比较级 better,"比上次"用 than。✓ My grades are better than last time. 场合:告诉别人自己进步了。
