### s5 seq1 | ans: explored
“几年前勘探了遗址”是过去发生、已完成的事,用一般过去时;explore 是规则动词,加 -ed。✓ the team explored an ancient site. ✗ explores / is exploring:不是现在;✗ has explored:这里有明确的过去时间(a few years ago),不用现在完成。

### s5 seq2 | ans: enjoyed
“曾拥有很高的文明水平”讲过去,enjoy 加 -ed。✓ the city enjoyed a high level of civilization. ✗ enjoys / is enjoying / has enjoyed:时态不对。

### s5 seq3 | ans: showed
“每尊都是一位女神”讲过去,show 的过去式 showed。✓ each statue showed a goddess. ✗ shows / is showing / has shown:时态不对。

### s5 seq4 | ans: dug
dig(挖)是不规则动词,过去式是 dug(不是 digged)。✓ the workers dug up old pipes. ✗ digged:不存在;✗ digs / dig:不是过去式。

### s5 seq5 | ans: found
find(找到)的过去式是不规则的 found(不是 finded)。✓ they found many pieces. ✗ finded:不存在;✗ finds / find:不是过去式。

### s5 seq6 | ans: stood
stand(立着)的过去式是不规则的 stood(不是 standed)。✓ the houses stood along the road. ✗ standed:不存在;✗ stands / stand:不是过去式。

### s5 seq7 | ans: began
begin 的过去式是 began;begun 是过去分词,不能单独作过去式。✓ the team began the work. ✗ begun:那是过去分词;✗ begins / beginned:都不对。

### s5 seq8 | ans: came

### s5 seq9 | ans: felt

### s5 seq10 | ans: wore

### s5 seq11 | ans: happened

### s5 seq12 | ans: happened

### s5 seq13 | ans: story
