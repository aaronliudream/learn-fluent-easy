## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,过去完成时 + 一般过去混用叙事)

> I had an amusing experience last summer while I was on vacation in France. After I had left a small village in the south of France, I drove on toward the next town. On the way, a young man waved at me and asked me for a lift. As soon as he had gotten into the car, I said good morning to him in French. He replied in the same language, so I figured he was a local Frenchman. Apart from a few words, I did not know any French at all. Neither of us spoke during the whole journey. I had nearly reached the town when the young man suddenly turned to me. Very slowly, he asked, "Do you speak English?" I was surprised, because before he spoke, I had thought he was French. As I soon learned, the young man was English himself!

**前置听力题**:Did the young man speak English? → **Yes — he was English himself** ✓ / No, only French / No, he was silent / Yes, but badly
