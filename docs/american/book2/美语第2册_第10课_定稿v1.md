## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,被动语态密集 + 双重所有格)

> My family owns an old musical instrument called a clavichord. It was made in Germany over three hundred years ago. The clavichord is kept in our living room. It has belonged to my family for generations. It was bought by my grandpa a long time ago. Recently, it was damaged by a visitor. She wanted to practice jazz on it and struck the keys too hard. Two of the strings were broken, and my dad was shocked. Now none of us are allowed to touch it. It is being repaired by a friend of my dad's. Soon we will be able to play it again.
