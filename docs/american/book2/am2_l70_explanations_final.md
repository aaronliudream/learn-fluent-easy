### s5 seq1 | ans: for
“因…而出名”固定用 famous for。✓ Our school is famous for its many clubs. ✗ of / at / to:都不与 famous 搭配表“因…出名”。

### s5 seq2 | ans: for

### s5 seq5 | ans: of
