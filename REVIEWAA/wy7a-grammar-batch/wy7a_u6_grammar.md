# 外研社七上 U6 语法(现在进行时)— 待审 v1

> code=`wy7-u6-present-continuous` · junior_fltrp/wy7A/U6/grade7 · 20题 · 答案分布[5, 5, 5, 5] · 难度{1: 9, 2: 8, 3: 3}

**summary**:现在进行时表示此刻正在进行的动作,结构为 be(am/is/are)+ 动词的 -ing 形式,常与 now、Look!、Listen! 连用。现在分词拼写:一般直接加 -ing(read→reading);以不发音 e 结尾去 e 加 -ing(write→writing);重读闭音节双写末尾辅音字母(swim→swimming, sit→sitting)。否定在 be 后加 not;疑问把 be 提到句首。

**examples**:Look! The children are playing. / She is writing a letter. / What are you doing now?

## 题目

**1. [d1]** Look! The children ____ playing football.
  - A)do  B)am  C)are  D)is  →**C**
  - 解析:复数主语 children 用 are。
**2. [d1]** She ____ reading a book now.
  - A)am  B)do  C)are  D)is  →**D**
  - 解析:单数主语用 is。
**3. [d1]** I ____ doing my homework.
  - A)am  B)do  C)are  D)is  →**A**
  - 解析:主语 I 用 am。
**4. [d1]** Listen! Someone ____ singing.
  - A)does  B)is  C)am  D)are  →**B**
  - 解析:someone 视为单数,用 is。
**5. [d1]** They ____ swimming in the pool.
  - A)are  B)be  C)am  D)is  →**A**
  - 解析:复数主语用 are。
**6. [d1]** He is ____ a picture now.
  - A)draw  B)drawing  C)drew  D)draws  →**B**
  - 解析:现在进行时用动词 -ing 形式。
**7. [d1]** Look, the dog ____ running fast.
  - A)are  B)be  C)is  D)am  →**C**
  - 解析:单数主语 the dog 用 is。
**8. [d1]** We ____ cleaning the classroom.
  - A)is  B)am  C)do  D)are  →**D**
  - 解析:复数主语 we 用 are。
**9. [d1]** My mother is ____ dinner in the kitchen.
  - A)cooked  B)cooking  C)cook  D)cooks  →**B**
  - 解析:现在进行时用 cooking。
**10. [d2]** The boys are ____ in the river.
  - A)swiming  B)swimmming  C)swims  D)swimming  →**D**
  - 解析:swim 重读闭音节,双写 m 加 -ing。
**11. [d2]** Tom is ____ a letter to his friend.
  - A)writing  B)writting  C)writeing  D)writes  →**A**
  - 解析:write 去掉不发音的 e 加 -ing。
**12. [d2]** Look! She is ____ a kite in the park.
  - A)fling  B)flys  C)flying  D)flieing  →**C**
  - 解析:fly 直接加 -ing。
**13. [d2]** The cat is ____ on the chair.
  - A)siting  B)sitting  C)sits  D)sittting  →**B**
  - 解析:sit 重读闭音节,双写 t 加 -ing。
**14. [d2]** He ____ watching TV; he is reading a book.
  - A)aren't  B)doesn't  C)isn't  D)not  →**C**
  - 解析:现在进行时否定在 be 后加 not。
**15. [d2]** ____ you listening to me now?
  - A)Is  B)Do  C)Be  D)Are  →**D**
  - 解析:现在进行时疑问把 be 提前,主语 you 用 Are。
**16. [d2]** What ____ they doing now?
  - A)are  B)do  C)be  D)is  →**A**
  - 解析:复数主语 they 的进行时疑问用 are。
**17. [d2]** The students are ____ an English song.
  - A)sings  B)singeing  C)singging  D)singing  →**D**
  - 解析:sing 直接加 -ing。
**18. [d3]** — What ____ your father ____ now? — He ____ washing the car.
  - A)is; doing; is  B)does; doing; is  C)is; doing; does  D)is; do; is  →**A**
  - 解析:进行时疑问 is + doing;简答 is washing。
**19. [d3]** Usually I ____ to school by bike, but look, today I ____ the bus.
  - A)go; take  B)go; am taking  C)am going; am taking  D)am going; take  →**B**
  - 解析:usually 用一般现在时 go;look/today 表此刻,用进行时 am taking。
**20. [d3]** Listen! The girls ____ dancing and the boy ____ singing.
  - A)are; are  B)is; is  C)are; is  D)is; are  →**C**
  - 解析:复数 girls 用 are,单数 boy 用 is。
