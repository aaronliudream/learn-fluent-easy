### s5 seq4 | ans: than

### s5 seq5 | ans: any
‘比其他任何一艘都’= than any other + 单数名词;填 any。✓ more magnificent than any other ship. ✗ some / all / none:‘任何’用 any。

### s5 seq6 | ans: more
‘没有比…更…’,比较级表最高级;splendid 是双音节形容词,前面加 more。✓ No ship was more splendid than … ✗ most(最高级)/ very / so:多音节比较级用 more。

### s5 seq7 | ans: with
‘随着/伴随…’的独立结构(with + 名词 + 现在分词),句中那个介词是 with。✓ sank with all her flags flying. ✗ in / on / at:独立结构用 with。

### s5 seq8 | ans: churned

### s5 seq9 | ans: sliding

### s5 seq10 | ans: came
