> v3改:Q14换无性别主语(library·His不再成立)·Q13改authored(改编非原句)·Q19去同义反复·答案位置过站内算法

**summary**:人称代词:作主语用主格(I/we/he…),作宾语或表语用宾格(me/us/him…);单独作答或带 not 的简短回答常用宾格(—Who is it? —Me.);it 可表时间/天气/距离。物主代词:形容词性(my/our/your/his/her/its/their)用在名词前;名词性(mine/ours/yours/his/hers/its/theirs)独立使用,代替“形容词性+名词”。

## 题目(d1×9/d2×8/d3×3)

**1. [d1·authored]** ____ am a middle school student now.
  - A)Me  B)Mine  C)My  D)I  →**D**
  - 作主语用主格 I。
**2. [d1·authored]** My grandpa often tells ____ good stories.
  - A)me  B)I  C)mine  D)my  →**A**
  - 作宾语用宾格 me。
**3. [d1·authored]** ____ school uniform is blue and white.
  - A)Me  B)I  C)My  D)Mine  →**C**
  - 名词 uniform 前用形容词性物主代词 my。
**4. [d1·authored]** Tom is my classmate. ____ is good at maths.
  - A)Him  B)He  C)His  D)He's  →**B**
  - 作主语用主格 He；He's=He is。
**5. [d1·authored]** Our teacher helps ____ with English every day.
  - A)us  B)our  C)we  D)ours  →**A**
  - 作宾语用宾格 us。
**6. [d1·authored]** The students are cleaning ____ classroom now.
  - A)theirs  B)their  C)they  D)them  →**B**
  - 名词 classroom 前用形容词性物主代词 their。
**7. [d1·authored]** Lily loves ____ new bike very much.
  - A)she's  B)hers  C)her  D)she  →**C**
  - 名词 bike 前用形容词性物主代词 her。
**8. [d1·authored]** We all love ____ school.
  - A)us  B)ours  C)we  D)our  →**D**
  - 名词 school 前用形容词性物主代词 our。
**9. [d1·authored]** Welcome to our school! Nice to meet ____!
  - A)you  B)you're  C)yours  D)your  →**A**
  - meet 后作宾语用宾格 you。
**10. [d2·authored]** This blue pen isn't yours. It's ____.
  - A)my  B)mine  C)me  D)I  →**B**
  - 名词性物主代词 mine 代替 my pen。
**11. [d2·authored]** ____ is sunny and warm today.
  - A)He  B)That  C)This  D)It  →**D**
  - it 可表天气。
**12. [d2·authored]** — Is this schoolbag ____? — Yes, it's mine.
  - A)your  B)you're  C)yours  D)you  →**C**
  - 名词性物主代词 yours 代替 your schoolbag。
**13. [d2·authored]** — Who is at the door? — ____.
  - A)Mine  B)My  C)I  D)Me  →**D**
  - 单独作答常用宾格 Me；主格一般不单独使用。规则依据教材 印136 例句 —Who is it? —Me.（本题为改编，非原句）。
**14. [d2·authored]** This is our school library. ____ reading room is very big.
  - A)It's  B)Its  C)It  D)Their  →**B**
  - its 修饰名词 reading room 表“它的”；It's=It is；library 单数不用 Their。（避开宠物 he/she 成立的歧义）
**15. [d2·authored]** These red shoes are ____, not mine.
  - A)her's  B)she  C)hers  D)her  →**C**
  - 名词性物主代词 hers 代替 her shoes；无 her's 写法。
**16. [d2·authored]** This is Peter's eraser. The eraser is ____.
  - A)his  B)her  C)he  D)him  →**A**
  - his 兼形容词性/名词性，此处名词性代替 his eraser。
**17. [d2·authored]** My sister and I share a room. The bed near the window is ____.
  - A)my  B)I  C)mine  D)me  →**C**
  - 名词性物主代词 mine 代替 my bed。
**18. [d3·authored]** Lily can't find her ruler. Can you give ____ mine? ____ needs it now.
  - A)she; Her  B)her; She  C)her; Her  D)hers; She  →**B**
  - give 后接宾格 her（间接宾语）；第二空作主语用主格 She。
**19. [d3·authored]** Anna has a red schoolbag and I have a blue one. The red one is ____, and the blue one is ____.
  - A)her; mine  B)her; my  C)hers; my  D)hers; mine  →**D**
  - 独立使用代替 Anna's bag / my bag，用名词性物主代词 hers 和 mine。
**20. [d3·authored]** My parents work hard. I love ____, and ____ love me too.
  - A)them; they  B)they; them  C)their; they  D)them; their  →**A**
  - love 后接宾格 them；第二空作主语用主格 they。
