# AM2 L4 逐题解释(定稿) · 供 gen-book2-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: have
现在完成时 = have/has + 过去分词。主语 I(还有 you/we/they)用 have。✓ I have just received a letter.

### s5 seq2 | ans: has
主语 He(还有 she/it)用 has。✓ He has already visited many cities.

### s5 seq3 | ans: received
receive 是规则动词,过去分词直接加 -d → received(与它的过去式同形)。✓ I have just received a letter.

### s5 seq4 | ans: gone
go 是不规则动词,过去分词是 gone(不是 went,也不是 goed)。✓ He has gone to Alice Springs.

### s5 seq5 | ans: buy → bought
规则动词的过去分词直接加 -ed(visit→visited / receive→received / arrive→arrived);只有 buy→bought 要单独记,是不规则的。

### s5 seq6 | ans: I have just had breakfast.
just 要放在 have 和过去分词 had 中间。✓ I have just had breakfast. 既不放句首,也不放句尾。

### s5 seq7 | ans: 在 have/has 和过去分词中间
just / already / never 这类词,位置在 have/has 和过去分词之间。✓ He has already gotten a job. / I have never been there.

### s5 seq8 | ans: Have
现在完成时的疑问句把 have/has 提到主语前面;you 用 Have。✓ Have you ever been to Canada?

### s5 seq9 | ans: never
"从来没有"用 never,放在 has 和过去分词中间。✓ My brother has never been abroad before.

### s5 seq10 | ans: yet
yet 表示"还(没)",用在否定句末。✓ The train has not arrived yet.

### s5 seq11 | ans: yet
yet 也用在疑问句末,表示"……了吗"。✓ Have you finished your homework yet?

### s5 seq12 | ans: for
"达六个月"是一段时间,用 for + 时间段。✓ He has been there for six months. since 后面接的是过去的时间点(如 since 1980)。

### s5 seq13 | ans: He has lived here for three years.
现在完成时 + for + 时间段表示"到现在为止一直……"。✓ He has lived here for three years. 注意不能说 since three years(要说 since three years ago)。

### s5 seq14 | ans: gone
has gone 表示"去了那儿、现在还没回来"。✓ Tom has gone to Alice Springs.(人还在那)

### s5 seq15 | ans: gone=去了还没回;been=去过又回来了
has gone to = 去了那里、人还没回来;has been to = 去过那里、现在已经回来了。这是两者最大的区别。

### s5 seq16 | ans: He has broken his leg, so he can't walk now.
现在完成时强调"过去的动作对现在造成的结果":他把腿摔断了,所以现在走不了路。✓ 其它几句要么是一般过去时(last year),要么是习惯或正在进行。

### s5 seq17 | ans: I went to Canada last year.
有明确的过去时间 last year,就用一般过去时,不能用现在完成时。✓ I went to Canada last year.(现在完成时不和 yesterday/last year 这类明确过去时间连用)

### s5 seq18 | ans: has just rung
铃"刚刚响过、现在停了",看的是对现在的结果,用现在完成时 has just rung。✓ The bell has just rung. is ringing 是"正在响",意思不同。

### s6 seq1 | ans: center
美语"中心"拼作 center(-er 结尾);英式拼 centre(-re 结尾)。✓ in the center of the city.

### s6 seq2 | ans: gotten
get 的过去分词,美语常用 gotten。✓ He has gotten a new car. 英式则说 has got。

### s6 seq3 | ans: traveled
美语里 travel 加词尾只单写一个 l → traveled;英式双写 → travelled。✓ He has traveled a lot.

### s6 seq4 | ans: 许多(修饰可数名词复数)
a great number of = 许多,后面接可数名词复数。✓ a great number of cities.
顺带记:一点点 = a little;一半 = half;只有几个 = only a few.

### s6 seq5 | ans: 达六个月(一段时间)
for six months = 达六个月,表示一段持续的时间。✓ He has been there for six months.
顺带记:在六月份 = in June;六个月前 = six months ago;第六个月 = the sixth month.

### s6 seq6 | ans: 在这个国家的中心
in the center of the country = 在这个国家的中心位置。✓ a town in the center of the country.
顺带记:在国家外面 = outside the country;在国家旁边 = next to the country;去这个国家 = go to the country.

### s7 seq1 | ans: received
"我刚收到一封信"用现在完成时 have received;receive 的过去分词是 received。✓ I have just received a letter.
✗ receive:完成时 have 后要用过去分词 received;✗ receiving:现在分词不作完成时;✗ receives:完成时用过去分词,不用单三 receives.

### s7 seq2 | ans: been
"在那儿待了六个月"用 has been there;be 的过去分词是 been。✓ He has been there for six months.
✗ be:has 后接过去分词 been,不用原形;✗ was:完成时不用 was;✗ being:being 不是过去分词.

### s7 seq3 | ans: already
"已经找到了好工作"用 already,放在 has 和过去分词中间。✓ He has already gotten a good job.
✗ yet:yet 多用于否定/疑问且放句末,这是肯定句用 already;✗ ever:ever 多用于疑问"曾经";✗ since:since 引导时间起点,不作副词插这儿.

### s7 seq4 | ans: driven
"开车去了小镇"用 has driven;drive 的过去分词是 driven(不是 drove)。✓ He has driven to a small town.
✗ drove:完成时用过去分词 driven,不用过去式 drove;✗ drive:原形不作完成时;✗ driving:现在分词不作完成时.
