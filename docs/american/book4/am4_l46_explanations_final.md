# AM4 L46 逐题解释(定稿·原创承载·the+比较级/it is no use doing/those who定从/情态被动must be done/不定式作目的状语·业余爱好) · 供 gen-book4-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: stronger
‘越…越…’= the + 比较级…, the + 比较级…;第一分句填 strong 的比较级 stronger。✓ the stronger the will, the more futile the effort. ✗ strong(原级)/ strongest(最高级)/ strongly(副词):此结构用比较级。

### s5 seq2 | ans: more
the + 比较级,the + 比较级;absorbing 的比较级用 more absorbing,填 more。✓ the more absorbing the hobby. ✗ most(最高级)/ very / much:比较级用 more。

### s5 seq3 | ans: greater
the + 比较级,the + 比较级;第二分句用 great 的比较级 greater。✓ the greater his tension grew. ✗ great(原级)/ greatest(最高级)/ greatly(副词):此处要形容词比较级。

### s5 seq4 | ans: use
‘做…没有用’= it is no use + 动名词;固定说法填名词 use。✓ it is no use arguing. ✗ used / using / useful:固定结构是 no use。

### s5 seq5 | ans: doing
it is no use 后接动名词;填 do 的 -ing 形式 doing。✓ it is no use doing what you like. ✗ to do / do / done:use 后一律用 -ing。

### s5 seq6 | ans: waiting
it is no use 后接动名词;填 wait 的 -ing 形式 waiting。✓ it is no use waiting until late in life. ✗ to wait / wait / waited:use 后一律用 -ing。

### s5 seq7 | ans: those
‘…的那些人’= those who;先行词用 those,填 who 前的 those。✓ those who are worked to death. ✗ which / whom / what:指人的那些人用 those。

### s5 seq8 | ans: who
定语从句先行词 those 指人、在从句里作主语,用关系代词 who。✓ those who love their work. ✗ which(指物)/ whom(宾格)/ whose(所属):作主语指人用 who。

### s5 seq9 | ans: whose
定语从句表‘他们的爱好’这种所属关系,用 whose。✓ the people whose hobbies are all real. ✗ who(作主语)/ which / whom:表所属用 whose。

### s5 seq10 | ans: be
情态动词的被动 = 情态动词 + be + 过去分词;must 后填 be。✓ must be chosen with care. ✗ been / being / to be:情态动词后接原形 be。

### s5 seq11 | ans: banished
情态动词被动 be + 过去分词;填 banish 的过去分词 banished。✓ cannot be banished by will alone. ✗ banish / banishing / to banish:be 后用过去分词。

### s5 seq12 | ans: divided
情态动词被动 be + 过去分词;填 divide 的过去分词 divided。✓ may be divided into three classes. ✗ divide / dividing / to divide:be 后用过去分词。

### s5 seq13 | ans: To be
不定式作目的状语置于句首,‘为了…’;填 To be。✓ To be really happy, one ought to have hobbies. ✗ Being(动名词)/ Be / For be:表目的用不定式 To do。

### s5 seq14 | ans: to
不定式作目的状语,‘为了逃离…’;填 to。✓ took up painting to escape the strain. ✗ for(后不接动词原形)/ that / so:目的用 to + 动词原形。

### s5 seq15 | ans: to
不定式作目的状语,‘为了驱走…’;填 to。✓ reads novels to banish the worries. ✗ for / of / with:目的用 to + 动词原形。

### s5 seq16 | ans: futile
毫无效果、毫无结果的是 futile(徒劳的、无效的)。✓ a futile effort. ✗ fertile 肥沃的;✗ fatal 致命的;✗ final 最后的——都不对。

### s5 seq17 | ans: aggravate
使糟糕的局面变得更糟是 aggravate(加剧、使恶化)。✓ aggravate the strain. ✗ appreciate 欣赏;✗ aggregate 聚合;✗ agitate 搅动/使不安——都不对。

### s5 seq18 | ans: banish
把不想要的念头赶出脑海是 banish(驱除、消除)。✓ banish the thought from your mind. ✗ vanish 消失(不及物);✗ punish 惩罚;✗ polish 擦亮——都不对。
