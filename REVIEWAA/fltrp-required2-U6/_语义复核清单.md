### u6.01 -ing 分词作宾补(宾语主动发出、动作进行中)
- We see a woman ____ at night in a dark, cold sea.
  **swimming✓** / swum / to swim / swims
  考点:-ing 分词作宾补(宾语主动发出、动作进行中) ｜ 解析:宾语 woman 自己在游、动作进行中,主动关系 → -ing 形式 swimming。
- Diving near the reef, Lin Feng noticed several fish ____ around the coral.
