### s5 seq1 | ans: that
强调句 It is + 被强调部分 + that + 其余;此处被强调的是名词短语,连词用 that。✓ It is differences… that block trade. ✗ what / who(强调物用 that)/ which:强调句连词用 that。

### s5 seq2 | ans: is
强调句 It is/was … that …;叙述当前事实用 is。✓ It is national rules… that hinder trade. ✗ are(强调句 It 后恒用单数 is/was)/ was(时态)/ has:此处用 is。

### s5 seq3 | ans: who

### s5 seq9 | ans: before

### s5 seq10 | ans: that

### s5 seq11 | ans: which

### s5 seq12 | ans: that

### s5 seq13 | ans: enough

### s5 seq14 | ans: covering
现在分词作后置定语,主动表“涵盖…的”。✓ One pact, covering medical devices,… ✗ covered(被动)/ cover / to cover:主动后置定语用 -ing。

### s5 seq15 | ans: enough
形容词 + enough + to do:“…得足以做…”;simple 后这个副词是 enough。✓ simple enough to follow without help. ✗ too(too…to 表否定“太…而不能”)/ so / very:此结构用 enough。
