## L70《What Are You Good At?》· am2_l70 · U9
**考点(照原课:形容词+介词固定搭配)**
- G1 形容词 + for:eager for、famous for、ready for、responsible for、sorry for
- G2 形容词 + of:afraid of、fond of、proud of、capable of、full of
- G3 形容词 + at:good at、bad at、quick at、surprised at
- G4 形容词 + with/to/about:pleased with、kind to、grateful to、worried about、sorry about
**词表**:官方形容词+介词词条零遗漏(eager/famous/grateful/responsible/qualified/ready/proud/fond/afraid/capable 等)。
**新情节课文(校园社团招新)**
1. Our school is **famous for** its many clubs, and everyone is **eager for** a place in one.
2. Mia is really **good at** painting and **fond of** drawing cartoons.
3. Her brother is **proud of** her, but he's **afraid of** speaking in public himself.
4. "You should be **responsible for** the art club," the teacher said. "You're **capable of** leading it."
5. Mia was **surprised at** the offer and **grateful to** her teacher.
6. "I'm a little **worried about** the work," she said, "but I'm **ready for** a challenge."
7. The teacher was **pleased with** her answer and **kind to** everyone who joined.
8. Soon the club was **full of** students **keen on** art.
9. Mia was **sorry for** the ones who couldn't get in, but the room was **capable of** holding only twenty.
10. By the end of the year, she was **famous for** the best art show the school had ever seen.

## L75《The Big Race》· am2_l75 · U10
**考点(照原课 KS3/27/51:一般过去时综合)**
- G1 规则/不规则动词过去式;G2 一般过去时叙事(过去连续动作);G3 过去时间状语(yesterday/last week/ago);G4 否定与疑问(didn't / Did…?)
**新情节课文(学校运动会接力赛)**
1. Last Saturday our school **held** its yearly sports day.
2. The sun **shone**, and hundreds of families **came** to watch.
3. My class **entered** the relay race, and I **ran** the last leg.
4. When the whistle **blew**, the runners **shot** forward.
5. At first we **fell** behind, and I **thought** we **had** no chance.
6. But our third runner **passed** two others and **handed** me the baton.
7. I **took** a deep breath and **raced** as fast as I **could**.
8. The crowd **shouted**, and I **crossed** the line just ahead of the others.
9. We **won** by half a second! I **didn't** expect it at all.
10. It **was** the best sports day our class **ever had**.

## L76《What Have You Been Up To?》· am2_l76 · U10
**考点(照原课 KS4/28/52:现在完成时 vs 现在完成进行时)**
- G1 现在完成 has/have done(结果/经历);G2 现在完成进行 has/have been doing(持续过程);G3 两者对比;G4 since/for
**新情节课文(朋友久别重逢聊近况)**
1. "I **haven't seen** you **for** ages!" said Emma. "What **have** you **been doing**?"
2. "I **have been studying** photography **since** January," said Leo.
3. "I **have taken** hundreds of photos already, and I **have learned** so much."
4. "That's great! I **have been working** at the café, but I **haven't had** much free time."
5. "**Have** you **finished** your painting?" Leo asked.
6. "Not yet — I **have been painting** it **for** three weeks, but it's almost done."
7. "We **have both been busy**, then."
8. "Yes, but we **have** finally **found** an afternoon to meet!"
9. "I **have missed** these chats," Emma said with a smile.
10. "So **have** I. Let's not wait so long next time."

## L79《A Long Journey Home》· am2_l79 · U10
**考点(照原课 KS7/31/55:一般过去 vs 过去进行 vs used to/would)**
- G1 一般过去(what happened);G2 过去进行(what was happening,背景);G3 used to/would(过去习惯);G4 三者在叙事中配合
**新情节课文(难忘的火车旅行)**
1. When I **was** a child, my family **used to** take the train to my grandma's every summer.
2. We **would** always sit by the window and watch the fields go by.
3. One year, while we **were traveling** north, the train suddenly **stopped**.
4. It **was raining** hard, and a tree **had fallen** across the track ahead.
5. Everyone **was waiting** quietly while the workers **cleared** it away.
6. My little brother **was sleeping**, but I **watched** the rain **run** down the glass.
7. In the old days, my dad **would** tell us stories to pass the time.
8. That day he **told** us about the trips he **used to** take as a boy.
9. Hours later, the train **started** again, and we finally **arrived** at night.
10. I still **remember** how happy grandma **looked** when she **opened** the door.

## L81《A Day at the Museum》· am2_l81 · U11

2. We arrived **at** ten o'clock and waited **at** the door **with** our tickets.
3. A guide **in** a blue uniform led us **into** the first hall.
4. **With** great care, she showed us models **of** old machines.
5. **In** one room, a robot moved **with** surprising speed.
6. We stood **at** the front and looked **at** it **with** amazement.
7. **At** noon we sat **in** the café and talked **about** what we had seen.
8. **In** the afternoon we walked **into** the space section **with** excitement.
9. We stayed **at** the museum **until** it closed **at** five.
10. On the way home, we talked **about** going back **with** our friends.

## L84《The School Play》· am2_l84 · U11
**考点(照原课 KS12/36/60:一般将来时 will/be going to)**
- G1 will do(将来预测/决定);G2 be going to do(计划/迹象);G3 时间/条件状语从句用一般现在表将来(when/if + 现在时);G4 将来的否定与疑问
**新情节课文(筹备校园话剧)**
1. Our class **is going to** put on a play next month.
2. "It **will be** a lot of work," the teacher said, "but I'm sure it **will be** fun."
3. "When the tickets **go** on sale, we **will** invite all the parents."
4. Mia **is going to** design the costumes, and Leo **will** paint the background.
5. "If it **rains** on the day, we **will** move everything indoors."
6. "**Are** you **going to** sing in the play?" I asked Sam.
7. "Yes, I **am**. I **am going to** practice every evening this week."
8. "The show **won't** start until seven, so we **will have** time to get ready."
9. "As soon as the lights **go** down, the first scene **will begin**."
10. "I think this **is going to** be the best play our school **has** ever seen!"

## L86《The Runaway Kite》· am2_l86 · U11
**考点(照原课 KS14/38/62:过去完成 vs 过去完成进行)**
- G1 过去完成 had done(过去的过去,已完成);G2 过去完成进行 had been doing(持续到过去某点);G3 对比;G4 配 before/after/when/by the time

5. It **had been flying** high for only a minute when the string **broke**.
6. The kite **had disappeared** over the trees before we **could** catch it.
7. We searched everywhere, but it **had** already **landed** in a far field.
8. A little girl **had found** it and **had been holding** it carefully.
9. She **had been waiting** for someone to come back for it.
10. By the time we **got** home, we **had made** a new friend — and learned to hold on tighter!

## L87《What Did She Say?》· am2_l87 · U11
**考点(照原课 KS15/39/63:间接引语 said that / told / asked)**
- G1 直接引语→间接引语(时态后移);G2 said that / told sb that;G3 asked if/whether;G4 特殊疑问的间接引语(asked where/what/when);G5 语域注(所述仍真时口语可不后移)
**新情节课文(转述一次采访)**
1. Yesterday a reporter **asked** our coach about the big game.
2. She **asked** him **how** the team **had prepared**.
3. He **said that** they **had been training** hard all month.
4. He **told** her **that** the players **were** ready and confident.
5. The reporter **asked whether** the star player **would** join the match.
6. He **said that** she **was** feeling great and **would** definitely play.
7. She **asked when** the game **would** start.
8. He **told** her **that** it **would** begin at seven that evening.
9. He **added that** he **hoped** the fans **would** come and cheer.
10. Later, the reporter **wrote that** the coach **sounded** very positive.
📌 语域注(解释里加):口语转述当下仍成立的事,时态可不后移(He said the team **is** ready 也自然);正式书面用后移(was ready)。

## L90《What's for Dinner?》· am2_l90 · U12
**考点(照原课 KS17/41/65:情态动词 must/have to/need/should)**
- G1 must(必须/推测);G2 have to(客观必须);G3 needn't / don't have to(不必);G4 should(应该/建议);G5 must vs have to 区别
**新情节课文(一家人做晚饭)**
1. "We **should** start cooking soon," Mom said. "Dad **will** be home at six."
2. "**Do** we **have to** go shopping first?" I asked.
3. "No, we **needn't**. We **have** everything we need."
4. "You **must** wash your hands before you help," Mom reminded me.
5. "**Should** I chop the vegetables?" I asked.
6. "Yes, but you **must** be careful with the knife."
7. "We **don't have to** hurry — dinner **needn't** be ready until six-thirty."
8. "This soup **must** taste amazing," said my brother. "It smells great!"
9. "You **should** try it when it's done," Mom laughed.
10. "We **must** cook together more often — it's fun!"

## L94《The New Library》· am2_l94 · U12
**考点(照原课 KS21/45/69:被动语态各时态)**
- G1 一般现在被动(is done);G2 一般过去被动(was done);G3 现在完成被动(has been done);G4 将来被动(will be done);G5 情态被动(can/must be done)
**新情节课文(新图书馆落成)**
1. A beautiful new library **was built** in our town last year.
2. It **was designed** by a famous architect and **is admired** by everyone.
3. Thousands of books **have been added** to its shelves.
4. The reading rooms **are cleaned** every morning before opening.
5. Free classes **are offered** there, and anyone **can be taught** to use the computers.
6. Last month, a rare old map **was donated** and **is now displayed** in the hall.
7. Next year, a children's section **will be opened** on the second floor.
8. Books **must be returned** within three weeks, or a small fine **is charged**.
9. The library **has been visited** by more than ten thousand people already.
10. It **is loved** by the whole town, and it **will be enjoyed** for many years to come.
