# AM3 L11 逐题解释(定稿·原创承载·间接引语 + say/tell + 大写字母·海关无罪喜剧) · 供 gen-book3-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: had
转述别人的话时时态要后移一步:直接引语里的 have(一般现在)转述后变成 had。✓ He said that he had nothing to declare. ✗ has / have:没后移;✗ is having:have 表“有”不用进行。

### s5 seq2 | ans: was
转述时 is 后移成 was。✓ I told him that it was hair gel. ✗ is / are:没后移或人称不对;✗ has been:时态不合。

### s5 seq3 | ans: made
转述时 make 后移成过去式 made。✓ I said that I made it myself. ✗ make / makes:没后移;✗ making:不是谓语形式。

### s5 seq4 | ans: whether
转述 Yes/No(一般)疑问句,用 whether 或 if 引导。✓ He asked whether I had anything to declare. ✗ that:那用于转述陈述句;✗ what / who:用于转述特殊疑问句。

### s5 seq5 | ans: if
转述一般疑问句可用 if(=whether)。✓ He asked if that was perfume. ✗ that:用于陈述句;✗ what / which:用于带疑问词的问句。

### s5 seq6 | ans: whether
转述一般疑问句用 whether/if。✓ He asked whether I would open it. ✗ that:陈述句才用;✗ what / how:这句原本没有这些疑问词。

### s5 seq7 | ans: what
转述特殊疑问句要保留原来的疑问词 what,并把语序还原成陈述式。✓ He asked what was in the bottle. ✗ that / if / whether:都不能替代原来的 what。

### s5 seq8 | ans: why
转述时保留疑问词 why,语序还原:why I was so calm。✓ He asked why I was so calm. ✗ that / if / what:替代不了 why。

### s5 seq9 | ans: where
转述时保留 where,并把问句语序还原:where I had bought it(不是 where did I buy)。✓ He asked where I had bought it. ✗ that / if / whether:替代不了 where。

### s5 seq10 | ans: said
say 后面一般不直接接“人”,直接接内容:He said that…。✓ He said that the bottle was perfume. ✗ told:told 后要接人;✗ spoke / talked:不这样接 that 从句。

### s5 seq11 | ans: told
tell 后面要接“人”:tell sb that…。✓ I told him that it was hair gel. ✗ said:said 后不直接接人;✗ spoke / talked:不这样用。

### s5 seq12 | ans: We arrived on Monday.
句首字母要大写,星期(Monday)首字母也要大写,其余单词不必大写。✓ We arrived on Monday. ✗ monday 没大写;✗ we 句首没大写;✗ 每个词都大写不对。

### s5 seq13 | ans: He is from Canada and speaks English.
国家名 Canada、语言/国籍 English 首字母要大写,句首 He 要大写,其余小写。✓ He is from Canada and speaks English. ✗ canada/english 没大写;✗ he 句首没大写;✗ 全大写不对。

### s5 seq14 | ans: My trip was in July.
月份 July 首字母要大写,句首 My 要大写,其余小写。✓ My trip was in July. ✗ july 没大写;✗ my 句首没大写;✗ 每词都大写不对。

### s5 seq15 | ans: smuggler
