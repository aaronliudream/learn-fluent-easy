### s5 seq1 | ans: are filming
“此刻正在做”用现在进行时 = am/is/are + doing。✓ Some famous actors are filming a movie right now. ✗ film:一般现在,表经常;✗ filmed:一般过去;✗ have filmed:现在完成,表已拍完。

### s5 seq2 | ans: are wearing
“此刻正穿着”用现在进行时。✓ They are wearing costumes at the moment. ✗ wear:一般现在;✗ wore:一般过去;✗ wears:一般现在第三人称单数。

### s5 seq3 | ans: is talking
“此刻正在说”用现在进行时。✓ The director is talking to the actors now. ✗ talks:一般现在;✗ talked:一般过去;✗ talk:原形。

### s5 seq4 | ans: makes
“每年都拍(经常发生)”用一般现在时。✓ This director makes a new movie every year. ✗ is making:进行时表此刻;✗ made:一般过去;✗ make:第三人称单数要加 s。

### s5 seq5 | ans: wants
want(想要)表状态,且此处是普遍情况,用一般现在时。✓ The director wants the scene to look real. ✗ is wanting:状态动词不用进行时;✗ wanted:一般过去;✗ want:第三人称单数要加 s。

### s5 seq6 | ans: arrives
“每天早上到(经常)”用一般现在时。✓ Every morning the crew arrives at eight. ✗ is arriving:进行时表此刻;✗ arrived:一般过去;✗ arrive:第三人称单数要加 s。

### s5 seq7 | ans: know
know(认识、知道)表状态,不用进行时,用一般现在。✓ I know that actor. ✗ am knowing:状态动词不用进行时;✗ was knowing:同理不用进行;✗ have been knowing:状态动词不用完成进行。

### s5 seq8 | ans: recognize
recognize(认出)表状态,doesn't 后接原形。✓ The worker doesn't recognize them. ✗ recognizing:不接 -ing;✗ be recognizing:状态动词不用进行;✗ to recognizing:形式错。

### s5 seq9 | ans: seems
seem(显得)表状态,不用进行时,用一般现在。✓ She seems nervous before the scene begins. ✗ is seeming:状态动词不用进行;✗ was seeming:同理;✗ seem:第三人称单数要加 s。

### s5 seq10 | ans: out
“从…里出去、离开”用 get out (of)。✓ get out of the flower beds. ✗ in:是“进入”;✗ over:是“克服”;✗ on:构不成“离开”。

### s5 seq11 | ans: into
“进入(某空间)”用 get into。✓ I can't get into my trailer. ✗ out of:是“离开”;✗ over:是“克服”;✗ off:是“下来”。

### s5 seq12 | ans: over
“从(病)中恢复、克服”用 get over。✓ It took him a week to get over his cold. ✗ out:构不成“恢复”;✗ into:是“进入”;✗ off:是“下来”。

### s5 seq13 | ans: on with
