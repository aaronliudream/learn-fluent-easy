## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,间接引语密集 + 时态后移)

> The secretary told me that Mr. Harrison would see me right away. I felt very nervous as I took the elevator up to his office. He did not even look up from his desk when I walked in. After I had sat down, he said that business was very bad. He told me that the firm could not afford to pay such high salaries. Twenty people had already been let go, and I knew that my turn had come. "Mr. Harrison," I said in a weak voice, "may I say something?" "Don't interrupt me," he said sharply. Then he suddenly smiled and told me that I would get a raise. He said I would receive an extra thousand dollars a year. It was the best news I had heard all year!
