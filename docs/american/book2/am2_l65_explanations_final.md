### s5 seq1 | ans: must
"必须、一定要"做某事用 must + 动词原形。✓ I can't go to the cinema tonight; I must write some letters. ✗ may:表"也许/可以";✗ might:表"可能";✗ could:表"能够/可能",都不表"必须"。

### s5 seq2 | ans: had to
过去的"不得不"用 had to(must 没有过去式)。✓ I missed the train, so I had to take a taxi. ✗ must:没有过去式,不表过去;✗ have to:是现在;✗ ought:形式和意思都不合。

### s5 seq3 | ans: had to
过去的"不得不"用 had to。✓ Fifteen policemen had to push very hard. ✗ must:不表过去;✗ must to:形式错;✗ have:意思和形式都不合。

### s5 seq4 | ans: should
"应该、理应"(现在的建议或预期)用 should + 动词原形。✓ He should come at 4 o'clock if the plane arrives on time. ✗ must:表"必须";✗ will:表将来;✗ can:表"能够"。

### s5 seq5 | ans: ought to
"应该"还可用 ought to + 动词原形(注意有 to)。✓ You ought to ask for permission. ✗ must to / should to:这两个词后面不加 to;✗ have:意思和形式都不合。

### s5 seq6 | ans: should have
