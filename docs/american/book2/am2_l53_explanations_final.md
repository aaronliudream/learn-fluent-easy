### s5 seq1 | ans: stayed
有明确的过去时间 five years ago,用一般过去时。✓ I stayed at this hotel five years ago. ✗ have stayed / have been staying:现在完成(进行)不与 ago 连用;✗ stay:一般现在。

### s5 seq2 | ans: examined
Yesterday 表明是过去某时的动作,用一般过去时;examine 的过去式是 examined。✓ Yesterday the firemen examined the ground. ✗ have examined:不与 yesterday 连用;✗ examine:一般现在;✗ were examining:此处是完成的动作不是进行。

### s5 seq3 | ans: snatched

### s5 seq4 | ans: have put
"终于扑灭了、对现在有影响(火灭了)"用现在完成时 have + 过去分词;put 的过去分词仍是 put。✓ At last firemen have put out a big forest fire. ✗ put:此处强调对现在的结果,用现在完成;✗ have been putting:强调持续,不合"已扑灭";✗ are putting:现在进行。
