### s5 seq1 | ans: will be flying
将来某个时候正在进行,用将来进行时 will be + 动词-ing。✓ This time tomorrow, they will be flying over the ocean.

### s5 seq2 | ans: will be cooking
将来某个时刻正在进行,用将来进行时 will be + 动词-ing。✓ At 8 tonight, she will be cooking dinner.

### s5 seq3 | ans: will be arriving
tomorrow(明天)+ "将要到达"用将来进行时 will be arriving。✓ They will be arriving here tomorrow.

### s5 seq4 | ans: They are playing football.
"现在正在踢球"用现在进行时 are playing。✓ They are playing football.

### s5 seq5 | ans: They will be playing football tomorrow.
"明天这个时候将在踢球"用将来进行时 will be playing。✓ They will be playing football tomorrow.

### s5 seq6 | ans: He will be working in the garden tomorrow.
把现在进行(is working)改成明天,用将来进行时 will be working。✓ He will be working in the garden tomorrow.

### s5 seq7 | ans: I'll be seeing you next week.
将来进行时口语缩略:I will be → I'll be,后接动词-ing seeing。✓ I'll be seeing you next week.

### s5 seq8 | ans: He'll be coming by train.
he will be 缩略成 he'll be,后接动词-ing coming。✓ He'll be coming by train.

### s5 seq9 | ans: Will you be washing your car tomorrow?
将来进行时疑问:把 will 提到最前面(Will you be + 动词-ing…?)。✓ Will you be washing your car tomorrow?

### s5 seq10 | ans: won't be + 动词-ing
将来进行时否定 = won't(will not)be + 动词-ing。✓ I won't be working tomorrow.

### s5 seq11 | ans: He'll be arriving in a minute.
一般将来 He'll arrive 换成将来进行 He'll be arriving,意思一样,都表将来。✓

### s5 seq12 | ans: 两者常可互换,都表将来
将来进行时和一般将来时常常可以互换使用,都表示将来的动作。✓ He'll come = He'll be coming.

### s7 seq1 | ans: will be arriving
tomorrow + 将来某时正在 → 将来进行时 will be arriving。✓ The Silver Hawks will be arriving tomorrow.
✗ arrived:tomorrow 是将来,不用过去式;✗ are arriving now:now(此刻)与 tomorrow 矛盾;✗ arrive yesterday:yesterday 是过去,与 tomorrow 冲突.

### s7 seq2 | ans: will be coming
将来进行时 will be coming。✓ They will be coming by train.
✗ came:过去式,tomorrow 是将来;✗ come yesterday:yesterday 与将来的行程矛盾;✗ were coming:过去进行时不表将来.

### s7 seq3 | ans: will be meeting
将来进行时 will be meeting。✓ The fans will be meeting them at the station.
✗ met:过去式;✗ meet yesterday:yesterday 是过去;✗ were meeting:过去进行时,不表将来的接站安排.
