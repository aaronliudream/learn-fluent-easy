### s5 seq1 | ans: of
dream(梦想、想到)后面接介词 of。✓ dream of receiving a letter.

### s5 seq2 | ans: of
"想到会收到信"用 dream of + 动名词。✓ Jane never dreamed of receiving a letter. from / in / on 都不对。

### s5 seq3 | ans: from
receive / suffer / borrow 后面接 from。✓ receive a letter from someone.

### s5 seq4 | ans: from
"收到某人的来信"用 receive a letter from。✓ She received a letter from a girl in Holland.

### s5 seq5 | ans: in
believe / succeed / persist 后面接 in。✓ believe in, succeed in.

### s5 seq6 | ans: in
"相信、坚持要做某事"用 believe in + 动名词。✓ They believe in writing to each other.

### s5 seq7 | ans: on
depend / rely / insist 后面接 on。✓ depend on, rely on, insist on.

### s5 seq8 | ans: on
"取决于、全看"用 depend on。✓ It all depends on the weather.

### s5 seq9 | ans: on
"祝贺某人某事"用 congratulate sb on。✓ He congratulated me on my success.

### s5 seq10 | ans: 动名词(-ing)
介词后面接动词时,一律用动名词。✓ dream of receiving, interested in collecting.

### s5 seq11 | ans: becoming
dream of 后面接动名词,"梦想成为"= dream of becoming。✓ I dream of becoming a pilot.

### s5 seq12 | ans: collecting
be interested in 后面接动名词,"喜欢收集"= interested in collecting。✓ She is interested in collecting stamps.

### s5 seq13 | ans: from
save sb from = 使某人免于…。✓ The medicine saved him from death.

### s5 seq14 | ans: on
"成功取决于努力"用 depend on。✓ Success depends on hard work.

### s5 seq15 | ans: were
"正在旅行"是过去进行时,主语 we 用 were + 动词-ing。✓ We were traveling when Jane threw the bottle.

### s5 seq16 | ans: 描述当时正在进行的背景
过去进行时(was/were + 动词-ing)在故事里常用来描述当时正在进行的背景,再引出新动作。✓ We were traveling when she threw the bottle.

### s5 seq17 | ans: threw / thrown
throw 是不规则动词:throw – threw – thrown。别写成 throwed。

### s5 seq18 | ans: threw
"扔"的过去式是 threw(不规则)。✓ She threw the bottle into the sea. 别写成 throwed。

### s6 seq1 | ans: traveling
美语"旅行/行进"单写一个 l:traveling / traveled;英式双写:travelling / travelled。✓ traveling across the sea.

### s6 seq2 | ans: mail
美语"邮件、邮寄"常说 mail;英式说 post。✓ use the mail 用邮寄。

### s6 seq3 | ans: 梦想;想到
dream of = 梦想、想到。✓ dream of receiving a letter 想到会收到信。
顺带记:害怕 = be afraid of;厌倦 = be tired of;属于 = belong to.

### s6 seq4 | ans: 收到…的来信
receive a letter from = 收到…的来信。✓ a letter from a girl 一位姑娘的来信。
顺带记:给…写信 = write to;把信寄给… = send a letter to;拒收 = refuse to accept.

### s6 seq5 | ans: 想起;考虑
think of = 想起、考虑。✓ She never thought of it again 她再没想起它。
顺带记:同意 = agree with;逃离 = escape from;包含 = consist of.

### s6 seq6 | ans: 定期地,经常
regularly = 定期地、经常。✓ write regularly 定期通信。
顺带记:偶尔 = sometimes;从不 = never;突然 = suddenly.

✗ of:believe 表"信奉"时接 in,不接 of;✗ from:believe 不接 from;✗ on:believe 不接 on.

✗ of:depend 不接 of;✗ from:depend 不接 from;✗ in:depend 不接 in.

### s10 seq1 | ans: 扔,抛
