### s5 seq1 | ans: However
‘无论多么…’引导让步,用 However + 形容词。✓ However clever a porpoise may be, it feels no urge. ✗ Whatever(无论什么)/ Whenever(无论何时)/ Wherever(无论何地):修饰形容词用 However。

### s5 seq2 | ans: may
however + 形容词 + 主语 + may be:让步结构中常用情态词 may。✓ However intelligent they may be. ✗ must / need / dare:此结构用 may be。

### s5 seq3 | ans: tries
however + 副词 + 主语 + 谓语;主语 the turtle 单数,谓语用 tries。✓ However hard the turtle tries. ✗ try(复数/原形)/ trying / tried:单数用 tries。

### s5 seq4 | ans: It
真正的内容是后面的不定式 to credit…,句首用形式词 It。✓ It is a mistake to credit porpoises with a motive. ✗ That / This / There:形式先行用 It。

### s5 seq5 | ans: to watch
It is … + 不定式:真正内容后置用 to do。✓ It is great fun to watch a porpoise play. ✗ watch / watching / watched:此处用不定式 to watch。

### s5 seq6 | ans: for
It is + 形容词 + for sb + to do:引出动作者用 for。✓ It is natural for a porpoise to ride the wave. ✗ of / to / with:此结构引出动作者用 for。

### s5 seq7 | ans: sooner
no sooner … than …,‘一…就…’;与 than 呼应的词是 sooner。✓ had no sooner recovered than another hit it. ✗ longer / more / later:此结构用 sooner … than。

### s5 seq8 | ans: than
no sooner … than …;与 no sooner 呼应的连词是 than。✓ No sooner does it recover than a porpoise bumps it. ✗ then(那时)/ that / as:此结构用 than。

### s5 seq9 | ans: had
No sooner 置句首,主句部分倒装,把助动词提前;过去完成用 had。✓ No sooner had the turtle stood up than … ✗ has / have / was:倒装 + 过去完成用 had。

### s5 seq10 | ans: following
with + 名词 + 现在分词,表主动‘跟着’。✓ with the porpoise following close behind. ✗ followed(被动)/ follow / to follow:主动伴随用 -ing。

### s5 seq11 | ans: driven
with + 名词 + 过去分词,表被动‘被赶走’;drive 的过去分词 driven。✓ with the shark driven away. ✗ driving(主动)/ drive / to drive:被动伴随用过去分词。

### s5 seq12 | ans: pulled
with + 名词 + 过去分词,表被动‘被缩进’;pull 的过去分词 pulled。✓ with its feet pulled under its shell. ✗ pulling(主动)/ pull / to pull:被动伴随用过去分词。

### s5 seq13 | ans: who
非限定定语从句(逗号后)指人(the biologists),用 who。✓ The biologists, who study these animals, … ✗ which(指物)/ that(非限定不用)/ what:指人用 who。
