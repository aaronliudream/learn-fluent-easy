# AM3 L14 逐题解释(定稿·原创承载·would rather / would sooner 偏好 + have sth done·净化:传奇手艺人 Dawson) · 供 gen-book3-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: fix
would rather 后面接动词原形(不带 to):would rather fix。✓ He would rather fix the fence himself. ✗ to fix / fixing / fixed:would rather 后要用原形。

### s5 seq2 | ans: do
would rather 后接动词原形:would rather do。✓ I'd rather do it with my own two hands. ✗ to do / doing / did:要用原形。

### s5 seq3 | ans: stay
would rather 后接动词原形:would rather stay。✓ She would rather stay at home. ✗ to stay / staying / stayed:要用原形。

### s5 seq4 | ans: not
“宁愿不做”用 would rather not + 原形:would rather not go。✓ I would rather not go out at all. ✗ don't / not to / no:would rather 的否定直接加 not。

### s5 seq5 | ans: than
“宁愿 A 不愿 B”用 would rather A than B。✓ walk five miles than take the bus. ✗ then(那时)/ that / to:表“而不愿”要用 than。

### s5 seq6 | ans: have
than 后面同样接动词原形:than have someone else do it。✓ would rather work all day than have someone else do it. ✗ to have / having / has:要用原形 have。

### s5 seq7 | ans: sooner
would sooner = would rather,同样表示“宁愿”。✓ I'd sooner do it myself. ✗ soon / sooner than / more soon:固定说 would sooner。

### s5 seq8 | ans: than
would sooner A than B,和 would rather 一样用 than。✓ walk than wait for a ride. ✗ then / that / as:表“而不愿”要用 than。

### s5 seq9 | ans: work
would sooner 后接动词原形:would sooner work。✓ She would sooner work late. ✗ to work / working / worked:要用原形。

### s5 seq10 | ans: left
“希望某人怎样”用 would rather + 某人 + 过去式,这里的过去式表委婉愿望而非过去:would rather you left。✓ Dawson would rather you left his tools alone. ✗ leave / to leave / leaving:这个句式里要用过去式 left。

### s5 seq11 | ans: watched
would rather + 某人 + 过去式:would rather you watched。✓ I'd rather you watched and learned. ✗ watch / to watch / watching:要用过去式 watched(和后面 learned 一致)。

### s5 seq12 | ans: didn't touch
would rather + 某人 的否定,用过去式否定 didn't do。✓ I'd rather you didn't touch that wrench. ✗ don't touch / not touch / weren't touch:这个句式否定用 didn't touch。

### s5 seq13 | ans: repaired
have sth done“(请人)把某事做好”,done 用过去分词:have their roofs repaired。✓ Most people have their roofs repaired. ✗ repair / repairing / to repair:have + 名词后要用过去分词 repaired。

### s5 seq14 | ans: fixed
have sth done,用过去分词:have their cars fixed。✓ They had their cars fixed at the garage. ✗ fix / fixing / to fix:要用过去分词 fixed。

### s5 seq15 | ans: look
have a look = 看一看(have + 动作名词表示“做一次”)。✓ have a good look at his tools. ✗ seeing / watching / sight:固定搭配是 have a look。

### s5 seq17 | ans: destroy
把某物彻底破坏用 destroy(毁坏)。✓ a storm destroyed the fence. ✗ wash 洗;✗ paint 刷漆;✗ borrow 借——都不对。
