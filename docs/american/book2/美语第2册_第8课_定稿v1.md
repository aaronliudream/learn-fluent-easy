## 关1 · 课文(美语版 · 11 句,比较级最高级密集)

> Joe Sanders has the most beautiful garden in our whole town. Joe's garden is my favorite of all. Every year, almost everyone enters for the "Nicest Garden Competition." But Joe wins the prize every single time. Bill Frith's garden is bigger than Joe's, and Bill works much harder. He also grows more flowers and colorful vegetables. But Joe's garden is more interesting than Bill's. Joe has made the neatest paths and built a wooden bridge over a small pool. His garden is only ten meters wide, but it is the prettiest one around. I love gardens too, but I do not like hard work. Each year I enter for the competition as well, and I always win a little prize for the worst garden in town!
