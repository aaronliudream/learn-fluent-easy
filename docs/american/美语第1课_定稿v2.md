## 关5 · 语法专项（同 v1）

① Is this...? 一般疑问句（Yes, it is. / No, it isn't.，回答用 it）
② 指示代词 this　③ 物主代词 my/your（后必须跟名词）
④ Excuse me 用法表（引起注意 / 借过 / 没听清 Excuse me? vs I'm sorry 道歉）

## 关6 · 美语点睛（同 v1 对照表）

cell phone–mobile phone / No problem.–Not at all. / Excuse me?–Pardon? / Have a good one!–Cheers! / ma'am–madam

## 关7 · 对话填空（课文挖空，4 空）

TYLER: ___1___, ma'am.
EMMA: Yes?
TYLER: Is this ___2___ phone? It was on the chair.
EMMA: Oh! Yes, ___3___ is. It's my phone.
TYLER: ___4___ you go.
EMMA: Thank you so much!

| 空 | 选项 | 答案 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a) I'm sorry　b) Excuse me　c) Thank you　d) Hello you | b |
| 2 | a) my　b) you　c) your　d) I | c |
| 3 | a) this　b) that　c) phone　d) it | d |
| 4 | a) Here　b) There　c) Give　d) Take | a |

## 关8 · 听对话理解题（3 题，播放整篇后作答）

1. Where was the phone? — a) On the table　b) On the chair ✓　c) On the floor　d) In Emma's bag
2. Whose phone is it? — a) Tyler's　b) Emma's ✓　c) Nobody's　d) The store's
3. What does Emma say to thank Tyler? — a) "Have a good one!"　b) "No problem."　c) "You're a lifesaver." ✓　d) "Excuse me."

## 关9 · 情景应答（同 v1 练习C）

1. 有人说 "Thank you so much!" → No problem. / You're welcome.
2. 递东西给别人 → Here you go.
3. 想引起陌生人注意 → Excuse me!
