Over the past 29 years, there has been a shift in Australians’ beliefs about adventure. 
To survey how Australians describe adventure, we interviewed 1,000 people. As Figure 1 
shows, 43.4% of the interviewees said adventure was “getting off the beaten track and 
going to places less visited by ordinary tourists.” A total of 26.6% of them described it as
