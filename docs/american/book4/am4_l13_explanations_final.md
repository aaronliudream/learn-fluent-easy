### s5 seq1 | ans: deepest
最高级 the deepest,‘最深的’;deep 的最高级 deepest。✓ the deepest holes of all. ✗ deeper(比较级)/ deep(原级)/ more deep:单音节用 -est。

### s5 seq2 | ans: much
‘多达…之多’固定结构 as much as + 数量;中间的词是 much。✓ as much as 25,000 feet. ✗ many(不可数用 much)/ more / most:此结构中间用 much。

### s5 seq3 | ans: more
比较级 + than,‘胜过任何其他’;此处用 more。✓ improved deep drilling more than any other mining. ✗ most(最高级)/ much / very:与 than 搭配用比较级 more。

### s5 seq5 | ans: are
