### s5 seq1 | ans: can / could / may / might
提出请求"可以…吗",可以用 can / could / may / might。✓ Can I / May I have a ticket?

### s5 seq2 | ans: May
"我可以买两张票吗"是请求,用 May I have…。✓ May I have two tickets, please? Do / Am / Shall 都不对。

### s5 seq3 | ans: Can
"可以借你的笔吗"是请求,用 Can I borrow…。✓ Can I borrow your pen? Does / Is / Being 都不对。

### s5 seq4 | ans: Could / Might
提请求时 could / might 比 can / may 更委婉、更客气。✓ Could I have those tickets, please?

### s5 seq5 | ans: Could
更委婉地请求,用 Could I have…。✓ Could I have those two tickets, please? Do / Was / Am 都不对。

### s5 seq6 | ans: 可能,也许
may / might 表推测,意思是"可能、也许",说话人没有十足把握。✓ He may come tomorrow.

### s5 seq7 | ans: may
"可能随时开演"是推测,用 may begin。✓ The play may begin at any moment. must 是"一定"、does / is 不对。

### s5 seq8 | ans: may
"可能来,我不确定"是推测,用 may come。✓ He may come tomorrow; I'm not sure. will 是"一定会",把握太足。

### s5 seq9 | ans: may / might + have + 过去分词
"可能已经…了"(对过去的推测)= may / might + have + 过去分词。✓ It may have begun already.

### s5 seq10 | ans: may

### s5 seq11 | ans: may

### s5 seq14 | ans: May

### s5 seq15 | ans: may

### s5 seq17 | ans: 还是…的好;不妨
might as well = 不妨、还是…的好,表示"虽然没那么想,但还是做了吧"。✓ I might as well have them.

### s5 seq18 | ans: might
