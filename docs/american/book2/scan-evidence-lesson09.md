# L9《A cold welcome》三源扫描凭证(时间介词 at / in / on)

> 凭证纪律:凡"某书没有 X"结论必附逐页页眉清单。本课三源主题一致(时间介词),无冲突、无缺页、无错位。**单元2(L9–L16)开篇课**。

## 一、教材(新概念英语第二册 · WeLib 443pg)

- **PDF p46(印43)**:仍属 Lesson 8 收尾(Exercises 比较级)。
- **PDF p47(印44)**:`Lesson 9 A cold welcome 冷遇` — First listen…(What does 'a cold welcome' refer to?)+ 课文。核心句:
  - **On** Wednesday evening, we went to the Town Hall.
  - a large crowd of people **had gathered** under the Town Hall clock. It **would strike** twelve **in** twenty minutes' time.
  - **at** five to twelve, the clock stopped.
  - **At** that moment, everybody began to laugh and sing.
  - New words:welcome、crowd、gather、hand(指针)、shout、refuse、laugh(7 词)。
  - Notes:① Town Hall 市政厅;② a large crowd of 一大群;③ in twenty minutes' time 20分钟之后。
- **PDF p48(印45)**:`Key structures 关键句型 · When did you arrive? I arrived at 10 o'clock.`(**考纲边界权威页**):
  - a) **at**:at 8 o'clock, at 9 o'clock, at night。
  - b) **in**:in ten minutes, in half an hour, in 1939, in 1945, in summer, in July, in August, in winter, in January, in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening。
  - c) **on**:on Wednesday, on Tuesday, on April 27th, on May 5th。
  - d) other:from 9 till 5, during the night, until 10 o'clock。
  - Exercise B(填介词):in two years' time / On Saturdays / during the week / in thirteen seconds / at the moment / on November 7th, in 1974 / in December。
  - **⚠️ 课文 "had gathered"(过去完成时)、"would strike"(过去将来时)是偶现,不在 Key structures 考点 → 排除(留后续课)。**

## 二、语法同步(新概念第二册语法同步 · 356pg)

- **PDF p58–66(印57–65)**:LESSON 8 比较级章节(L8 已用完)。
- **PDF p67(印66)**:`LESSON 9 A cold welcome` 独立标题页,专题=**一、表示时间的介词 at, in, on, during**(**考纲边界权威**):
  - (一)**at**(主要表示时间点):1.特定时刻 at nine a.m. / at nine after ten;2.不确定时刻 at night/dawn/midnight/that time/the moment/the beginning/end of/Christmas/Easter(注意 on Christmas Day);3.年龄段 at the age of eight / at twenty。
  - (二)**in**(主要表示时间段):1.较长时间 in spring/summer/autumn/winter、in the 21st century、a man in his thirties;2.**"在…时间之内/之后"(将来)** He would come back in a month / The train is leaving in a minute;3.in+动名词(in doing sth)。
  - (三)**on**(主要表示具体某一天):具体日期和星期 on Monday / on my birthday / on their wedding anniversary(注意:只说几月用 in,如 in October)。

## 三、语法新思维(第2册 · 271pg)

- **时间介词 at/in/on** 为介词基础专题;新思维介词章节(at 时刻点/in 时间段/on 具体某天 + 易混对比)作讲深参照。三源(教材 KeyStruct + 同步 LESSON9 + 新思维介词)一致指向时间介词。

## 四、三源边界裁决(what / how deep)

- **考点边界(what)**:时间介词 = **at**(钟点/时刻词night-noon-moment-Christmas/年龄)· **in**(月/年/季节/世纪/上午下午晚上/一段时间后=将来)· **on**(星期/日期/具体某天)· 易混对比(in the morning vs on Monday morning;at night vs in the evening;in October 月 vs on Oct 5th 日)· other(from…till/during/until)。
- **讲深(how)**:at/in/on 三分(同步一/二/三)+ in+一段时间表将来(同步二.2)+ 月 vs 日对比(同步三注意)。
- **明确排除(有凭证)**:过去完成时(had gathered)、过去将来时(would strike)——课文偶现,不在 Key structures,留后续课。in+动名词(in doing sth)属进阶,本课不设题。
- **无冲突/无缺页/无错位**:教材 p47–48、同步 p67、新思维介词三源一致指向时间介词,边界闭合。**单元2 开篇**。
