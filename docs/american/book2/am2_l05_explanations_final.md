# AM2 L5 逐题解释(定稿) · 供 gen-book2-seed.mjs loadExp 合并

### s5 seq1 | ans: has
现在完成时 = have/has + 过去分词;主语 He(还有 she/it)用 has。✓ He has started his own service.

### s5 seq2 | ans: have
主语 They(还有 I/you/we)用 have。✓ They have sent many messages.

### s5 seq3 | ans: Up to now
"到目前为止"用 Up to now,是现在完成时的信号词。✓ Up to now, Jack has sent many orders.

### s5 seq4 | ans: has sent
句首 Up to now 提示用现在完成时;主语 Mr. Scott 用 has + 过去分词 sent。✓ Up to now, Mr. Scott has sent a great many messages.

### s5 seq5 | ans: messages
a great many 后面接可数名词复数,所以用 messages。✓ a great many messages.

### s5 seq6 | ans: 可数名词复数
a great many = 许多,只修饰可数名词复数(a great many messages / cities);修饰不可数名词要用 a great deal of。

### s5 seq7 | ans: since
"自从(某个过去时间点/某事发生起)"用 since。✓ I haven't heard from him since he left.

### s5 seq8 | ans: since 1990
"自从1990年(时间点)"用 since 1990。✓ 注意不能说 for 1990,也不能说 since 1990 years(那要说 since 1990)。

### s5 seq9 | ans: He opened a second store last month.
有明确的过去时间 last month,只能用一般过去时 opened,不能用现在完成时。✓ He opened a second store last month.

### s5 seq10 | ans: 有明确过去时间 yesterday,要用一般过去时(I finished it)
yesterday 是明确的过去时间点,现在完成时不能和它连用;要改成一般过去时 I finished it yesterday。

### s5 seq11 | ans: When did you see it?
先用现在完成时报"看过了"(Yes, I have),接着追问"什么时候看的"就换成一般过去时。✓ When did you see it?

### s5 seq12 | ans: 换成一般过去时
报新消息时用现在完成时开头;一旦追问"什么时候"这种过去的具体情况,就改用一般过去时。

### s5 seq13 | ans: bought
buy 是不规则动词,过去分词是 bought。✓ He has just bought ten pigeons. 别写成 buyed。

### s5 seq14 | ans: begun
begin 的过去分词是 begun(过去式是 began,别混)。✓ Jack has begun his own service.

### s5 seq15 | ans: sent
send 是不规则动词,过去分词是 sent。✓ He has sent many messages. 别写成 sended。

### s5 seq16 | ans: 想出(主意/办法)
come up with = 想出(主意、办法)。✓ He has come up with a clever idea.

### s5 seq17 | ans: away
"离……多远"用 miles away;away 表示"距离(某处)"。✓ The new store is only two miles away.

### s5 seq18 | ans: 越过,走完(一段距离)
cover 在这里是"越过、走完(一段距离)"的意思。✓ The bird covered the distance in four minutes. 不是"覆盖"。

### s6 seq1 | ans: store
美语里"商店"常用 store(bookstore);英式常用 shop。✓ a small bookstore downtown.

### s6 seq2 | ans: downtown
美语里"市中心"说 downtown;英式说 (in the) city centre。✓ a bookstore downtown.

### s6 seq3 | ans: mail
美语"邮寄 / 信件"用 mail;英式用 post。✓ his own private "pigeon mail" service.

### s6 seq4 | ans: 到目前为止
up to now = 到目前为止(和现在完成时连用)。✓ Up to now, he has sent many messages.
顺带记:很久以前 = long ago;马上 = right away / at once;从今以后 = from now on.

### s6 seq5 | ans: 许多(修饰可数名词复数)
a great many = 许多,后面接可数名词复数。✓ a great many requests.
顺带记:一点点 = a little;大约 = about / around;一半 = half.

### s6 seq6 | ans: 想出一个主意
come up with an idea = 想出一个主意/办法。✓ He has come up with a clever idea.
顺带记:追上某人 = catch up with sb;放弃主意 = give up an idea;站起来 = stand up.

### s7 seq1 | ans: opened
有明确的过去时间 last month,用一般过去时 opened,不能用现在完成时。✓ He opened a second store last month.
✗ has opened:有明确过去时间 last month,用一般过去时,不用现在完成;✗ opens:过去的事不用一般现在;✗ opening:缺助动词,单独不能作谓语.

### s7 seq2 | ans: installed
"还没安装"用现在完成时否定 hasn't installed;install 的过去分词是 installed。✓ The phone company hasn't installed a line yet.
✗ install:hasn't 后要用过去分词 installed;✗ installing:现在分词不作完成时;✗ installs:完成时用过去分词,不用单三 installs.

### s7 seq3 | ans: has
主语 he 用 has + 过去分词。✓ He has bought ten pigeons.
✗ have:主语 he 用 has,不用 have;✗ had:报新消息用现在完成 has,不用过去完成 had;✗ is:完成时用 has+过去分词,不用 is.

### s7 seq4 | ans: Up to
"到目前为止"是 Up to now,这里填 Up to。✓ Up to now, Jack has sent many messages.
✗ For:"到现在为止"是 up to now,没有 for now 这种说法;✗ Since:since 后接时间点,不构成"up to now";✗ In:搭配不对,应是 up to now.
